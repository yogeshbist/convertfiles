    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • Bachelor's degree or equivalent from a recognised university.
    • Age: minimum 21 years as on 01 August 2026; maximum 37 years (UR Male), 40 years (BC/EBC Male & Female and UR Female), 42 years (SC/ST Male & Female), with further relaxations as per rules. Note: the advertisement prints the maximum-age cutoff date as 01.08.2025, which differs from the minimum-age cutoff (01.08.2026) — likely a misprint in the advertisement.
    • Only Advt. No. 16/2026 is valid — it cancels the earlier Advt. No. 87/2025 dated 22 August 2025.
    • Rs.100 only for every candidate (plus bank charges).
    • Candidates who do not enter Aadhaar No. as identity proof pay an additional Rs.200 as biometric fee.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 935 Assistant Education Development Officer posts under the Education Department, Government of Bihar. Advt. No. 16/2026, dated 26 November 2026 — this advertisement cancels the earlier Advt. No. 87/2025 dated 22 August 2025.
    • Category breakup: UR 374 (women 131), EBC 93 (women 33), SC 150 (women 53), ST 10 (women 4), Extremely Backward Class 168 (women 59), Backward Class 112 (women 39), BC Women 28. Women total 319 (35% horizontal reservation); freedom-fighter descendant 2% = 19 posts; Divyang horizontal 37 posts (VI 10, DD 9, OH 9, MD 9).
    • Dates: online application 01 December 2026 to 31 December 2026; fee payment 01 December 2026 to 30 December 2026. One Time Registration (OTR) is mandatory at bpsconline.bihar.gov.in before applying.
    • Age: minimum 21 years as on 01 August 2026; maximum UR (Male) 37, BC/EBC (Male & Female) and UR (Female) 40, SC/ST (Male & Female) 42, with further relaxations as per rules. The advertisement prints the maximum-age cutoff date as 01.08.2025 (the minimum-age cutoff is 01.08.2026) — likely a misprint.
    • Qualification: Bachelor's degree or equivalent from a recognised university.
    • Selection: two stages — Preliminary Examination (General Studies, objective MCQ, 2 hours, 150 marks, 5 options, negative marking 1/3 per wrong answer including multiple/no answer; qualifying 40% General, 36.5% BC, 34% EBC, 32% SC/ST/women/Divyang) as a screening test, then Main Examination (3 papers, each 100 questions, 2 hours, 100 marks: General Language — General English 30 + General Hindi 70, qualifying 30% in each; General Studies; General Aptitude).
    • Pay: Level-5, basic Rs.29,200 during probation.
    • Fee: Rs.100 only for every candidate (plus bank charges); candidates who do not enter Aadhaar No. as identity proof pay an additional Rs.200 as biometric fee.
    • Photo/signature: live web-camera photo capture, then upload one English and one Hindi signature; each signature image not more than 20 KB, Width 160-220 pixels and Height 250-320 pixels (as printed in the advertisement).
    • All facts above are from Advt. No. 16/2026 (bpsc.bihar.gov.in). The apply portal is the official BPSC online application portal — the AEDO listing appears only after OTR login, so apply through the portal after completing OTR.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Complete your One Time Registration (OTR) on bpsconline.bihar.gov.in first — applications open 01 December 2026.
    • Read Advt. No. 16/2026 (dated 26 November 2026) fully on bpsc.bihar.gov.in before applying.
    • Keep your Aadhaar number ready as identity proof — without it, an extra Rs.200 biometric fee applies.
    • Prepare for the two-stage selection — Preliminary Examination (General Studies, 150 marks) as a screening test, then the Main Examination (3 papers).
    • Save the application number and fee receipt after submission.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply before 01 December 2026 — online applications open on 01 December 2026, not before.
    • Do not miss the two different last dates — fee payment closes 30 December 2026, online application closes 31 December 2026.
    • Do not skip the One Time Registration (OTR) — it is mandatory on bpsconline.bihar.gov.in.
    • Do not confuse this with the cancelled Advt. No. 87/2025 — only Advt. No. 16/2026 (dated 26 November 2026) is valid.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. bpsc.bihar.gov.in par Advt. No. 16/2026 padhen — online application 01 December 2026 se shuru.
    2. bpsconline.bihar.gov.in par One Time Registration (OTR) poora karen.

    4. Live photo capture aur English + Hindi signature (har ek 20 KB se kam) ke saath form bharen.
    5. Fee ₹100 bhugtan karen (30 December 2026 tak) aur form 31 December 2026 tak submit karen.

    Questions

    What is the last date to apply for BPSC Assistant Education Development Officer (AEDO) Recruitment 2026?
    The online form closes on 31 December 2026. The fee can be paid till 30 December 2026.
    Do I have to upload a photograph?
    No. In this form the photo is captured live through the camera while you apply. Sit before a plain, light background, face the light and keep your full face clearly visible — there is no photo file to prepare.
    BPSC AEDO 2026 me kitni vacancy hai?
    Kul 935 posts — UR 374, EBC 93, SC 150, ST 10, Extremely Backward Class 168, Backward Class 112, BC Women 28. Mahilaon ke liye 35% horizontal reservation (kul 319); freedom-fighter descendant 2% (19 posts); Divyang horizontal 37 posts.
    BPSC AEDO ki last date aur fee kya hai?
    Online application 01 December 2026 se 31 December 2026 tak; fee payment 01 December se 30 December 2026 tak. Fee sirf ₹100 har candidate ke liye (plus bank charges); jo Aadhaar number identity proof ke roop me nahi dete, unhe extra ₹200 biometric fee deni hogi.
    BPSC AEDO ke liye qualification aur age limit kya hai?
    Recognised university se Bachelor's degree ya equivalent. Age minimum 21 saal (01.08.2026 ko); maximum UR (Male) 37, BC/EBC (Male & Female) aur UR (Female) 40, SC/ST (Male & Female) 42 saal.
