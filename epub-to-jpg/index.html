Why convert EPUB to JPG?

People convert EPUB to JPG because EPUB is built for e-readers, so it reflows differently in every app and prints badly, while JPG opens on essentially every device built in the last thirty years.

What is a .epub file?

EPUB is the open ebook standard used by every major reader except the Kindle. Unlike a PDF it reflows text to fit the screen and respects the reader's font size, which is what makes it comfortable on a phone or e-reader. Inside, it is a ZIP of XHTML.

What is a .jpg file?

JPG (also written JPEG) is the universal photograph format — small files that open on every device made in the last twenty-five years. It is lossy, discarding detail the eye is least likely to miss, which makes it excellent for photos and poor for screenshots, logos and text, where it leaves visible smudging.

What to expect from this conversion

Each page is laid out and then rendered as a picture at the scale you choose. A document of several pages produces several images, delivered together in a zip. JPG has no transparency, so any transparent area is flattened onto white.
