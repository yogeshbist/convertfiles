    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • PUC (2nd PUC / Class 12) from a recognised board, or an equivalent qualification, held by 22 December 2026.
    • Age 18 years or above as on 22 December 2026, within the category maximum (32 / 35 / 37 years).
    • Mentally and physically fit, and free of the disqualifying physical defects listed in the notification.
    • Read Notification No. KFD/HOFF/B9(RCT)/8/2026-PnR-KFD fully on aranya.gov.in before applying.
    • ₹750 for General Merit and Category 2A/2B/3A/3B (₹500 application fee + ₹250 processing fee).
    • ₹500 for SC/ST/Category-I, Ex-Servicemen and Transgender candidates (₹250 application fee + ₹250 processing fee).
    • ₹250 for Persons with Disabilities (processing fee only).
    • Pay online only — UPI, NEFT, Credit/Debit Card or Internet Banking — by 23 December 2026. The fee is non-refundable.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 355 posts of Gasthu Aranya Palaka — called 'Aranya Rakshaka' (Forest Guard) in the recruitment rules — Group 'C', Residual Parent Cadre (RPC / Non-HK): 320 current + 35 backlog, across 11 forest circles of Karnataka.
    • Circle-wise current vacancies (320): Bengaluru 20, Chamarajanagar 75, Chikkamagaluru 27, Dharwad 3, Hassan 18, Kanara 33, Kodagu 26, Mangaluru 32, Mysuru 28, Shivamogga 58. Backlog (35): Bengaluru 5, Ballari 1, Chamarajanagar 3, Chikkamagaluru 2, Kanara 12, Kodagu 1, Mangaluru 4, Mysuru 1, Shivamogga 6.
    • Pay scale: ₹37,500–₹76,100 (₹37,500-900-39,300-1000-43,300-1125-47,800-1250-52,800-1375-58,300-1500-64,300-1650-74,200-1900-76,100).
    • Age as on 22 December 2026: minimum 18 years. Maximum — General Merit 32 years; Category 2A/2B/3A/3B 35 years; SC/ST/Category-I 37 years. These limits already include a one-time 5-year relaxation. Ex-Servicemen: category maximum + years of service + 3 years. Serving honorarium Forest Guards with 5+ years' continuous service: maximum 43 years (General), 46 years (2A/2B/3A/3B), 48 years (SC/ST/Category-I).
    • Qualification: PUC (2nd PUC / Class 12) from the Karnataka Pre-University Board, or an equivalent — CBSE/ICSE Class 12, other State Boards' Class 12, NIOS Higher Secondary, 3-year Diploma, 2-year ITI/JOC course, or GTTC 3+1 diploma treated as a 3-year diploma. Candidates awaiting results or without the marksheet by the last date are NOT eligible. Class 10 alone is not enough.
    • Physical standards (Physical Standard Test): Men — height minimum 163 cm (152 cm for ST-Local Forest Dwelling Tribe candidates); chest minimum 79 cm normal with 5 cm expansion (74 cm normal, 5 cm expansion for ST-LFT). Women — height minimum 150 cm (145 cm for ST-LFT); minimum weight 40 kg (38 kg for ST-LFT). Eye vision: distant vision better eye 6/6 or worse eye 6/9 (with or without glasses); near vision better eye 0/6 or worse eye 0/8; colour-vision test compulsory. Disqualifying defects: knock-knees, bow legs, flat feet, varicose veins, speech/hearing impediment, hydrocele (men), vision / colour blindness / night blindness.
    • Selection: (1) Shortlisting on PUC marks in 1:20 ratio (posts:candidates), circle-wise, for the physical tests. (2) Physical Standard Test. (3) Physical Endurance Test: Men 1600 m run in 07 minutes; Women and Ex-Servicemen 1000 m run in 06 minutes — fail means out. (4) Physical Efficiency Test: 5 events, pass at least 3 — 100 m run (Men 15 sec / Women 18.5 sec), High Jump (Men 1.20 m / Women 0.90 m), Long Jump (Men 3.80 m / Women 2.50 m), Shotput (Men 16 lb: 5.60 m / Women 8 lb: 3.76 m), 800 m run Men 2 min 50 sec, 200 m run Women 40 sec. (5) Written test — offline OMR, objective type: ONE paper, General Knowledge & Mathematics, 100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours; Mathematics 40 marks, General Knowledge 60 marks (II PUC level); question paper in Kannada and English only; 1 mark per correct answer, −0.25 for a wrong / multiple / blank answer. (6) Document verification.
    • Dates: online application 23 November–22 December 2026; fee payment by 23 December 2026. Physical test and written exam dates to be announced.
    • All facts above are from Notification No. KFD/HOFF/B9(RCT)/8/2026-PnR-KFD dated 17.11.2026 (46-page Kannada notification, aranya.gov.in). The notification PDF link on aranya.gov.in is session-bound, so the notice link is kept null and the notification number above is the citable reference.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only through the KEA portal (cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea) between 23 November and 22 December 2026 — no other mode is accepted.
    • Check the physical standards before applying — height, chest, weight, eye vision and the defect list are all qualifying.
    • Pay the fee online by 23 December 2026 (₹750 / ₹500 / ₹250 by category) and keep the payment receipt.
    • Prepare for the written test at II PUC level: Mathematics 40 marks + General Knowledge 60 marks, 100 questions in 2 hours.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply with Class 10 alone — PUC / Class 12 or an equivalent is compulsory.
    • Do not apply if your PUC result is still awaited — the marksheet must be in hand by 22 December 2026.
    • Do not ignore the physical standards — the endurance run and PET are qualifying, and failing any of them ends the candidature.
    • Do not wait for the last day — apply by 22 December and pay the fee by 23 December 2026.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • PUC / Class 12 marksheet and certificate (or equivalent qualification certificate)
    • Date of birth proof and ID proof
    • Caste/category certificate (SC/ST/Category-I/2A/2B/3A/3B), if applicable
    • Ex-Serviceman certificate, if applicable
    • PwD certificate, if applicable
    • Recent passport-size photo and signature scans
    • Valid email ID and mobile number

    Documents you will need

    • PUC / Class 12 marksheet and certificate
    • Date of birth proof and ID proof
    • Caste/category certificate, if applicable
    • Recent photo + signature
