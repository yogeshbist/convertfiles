    Eligibility & fees

    • 14 vacancies — Technical Officer 7 (Level-7), Security Officer 1 (Level-7), Security Assistant 1 (Level-6), Technician (1) 4 (Level-2), Multi-Tasking Staff 1 (Level-1), at CSIR-AMPRI, Bhopal. The advertisement is RECT-02/2026.
    • TWO separate application windows: Technical Officer and Technician (1) — 7 October to 13 November 2026; Security Officer, Security Assistant and MTS — 15 October to 23 November 2026. Both portals close at 5:30 PM on their last date. The deadlines were extended by 7 days by the corrigendum of 30 October 2026 (the portals were down 23–26 October 2026).
    • Age: Technical Officer maximum 30 years, Security Officer 35 years, Security Assistant 28 years, Technician (1) 28 years, MTS 25 years (as on the closing date of the post), with relaxation as per Government of India rules.
    • Qualification: Technical Officer — a Master's degree in Material Science, Physics or a related field with at least 55% marks. Security Officer and Security Assistant — ex-servicemen JCO. Technician (1) — 10th pass with ITI in the trade. MTS — 10th pass. Check the post-wise qualification in the notification before applying.
    • Selection is post-wise — Technical Officer: trade test followed by a written test (Papers I–III); MTS: a written test of 150 questions, 450 marks, with −1 per wrong answer. Other posts follow the notification's scheme.
    • Apply online only on ampri.res.in — Technical Officer and Technician (1) by 13 November 2026, 5:30 PM; Security Officer, Security Assistant and MTS by 23 November 2026, 5:30 PM.
    • Application fee: ₹500 per post code for General/OBC/EWS candidates, paid online. SC/ST/PwBD/women and ex-servicemen pay nothing.
    • Pay the fee before the final submission — 13 November 2026, 5:30 PM for Technical Officer and Technician (1); 23 November 2026, 5:30 PM for the other posts.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check which of the two windows your post falls in — Technical Officer/Technician close on 13 November, the others on 23 November.
    • Read the post-wise qualification in the notification — Technical Officer needs a Master's degree.
    • Pay the ₹500 fee per post code online (SC/ST/PwBD/women/ESM exempt).
    • Submit before 5:30 PM on your post's last date — the portal closes then.
    • Keep the corrigendum (30 October 2026) dates in mind — the original deadlines were 6 and 16 November.

    Avoid this

    • Do not mix up the two deadlines — Technical Officer and Technician (1) close on 13 November 2026, not 23 November.
    • Do not apply for a Security post unless you are an ex-serviceman JCO.
    • Do not apply for Technical Officer without a Master's degree in a related field.
    • Do not wait for the last hours — the portal closes at 5:30 PM sharp.
