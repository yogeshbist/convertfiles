    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • 4-year full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc Engineering degree, PASS CLASS, from an AICTE/UGC-recognised institute/university, in Electronics (Electronics / Electronics & Communication / Electronics & Telecommunication / Communication / Telecommunication), Mechanical (Mechanical) or Electrical (Electrical / Electrical & Electronics). AMIE/Grad IETE in the relevant Electronics disciplines is also accepted.
    • Maximum age 28 years as on 01 November 2026 (General/EWS); relaxation OBC (NCL) 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwBD 10 years (minimum 40% disability).
    • No post-qualification experience is required.
    • Rs. 150 + 18% GST (Rs. 177 in total) per application, payable via SBI Collect (category 'Trainee Engineer – Panchkula Unit'); the SBI Collect reference number must be entered in the application form.
    • Exempt: SC, ST and PwBD candidates and Ex-servicemen. Fee is non-refundable.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 49 Trainee Engineer-I posts at the Panchkula Unit — Electronics 28 (UR 11, EWS 3, OBC 8, SC 4, ST 2), Mechanical 20 (UR 7, EWS 3, OBC 5, SC 3, ST 2), Electrical 1 (UR 1). Advertisement No. 2026-27/11/PK/TE/006, released 25 November 2026.
    • Age: maximum 28 years as on 01 November 2026 for General/EWS candidates; relaxation OBC (NCL) 3 years, SC/ST 5 years, PwBD (minimum 40% disability) 10 years in addition to category relaxation, as per Government rules.
    • Qualification: 4-year full-time B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc Engineering degree, PASS CLASS, from an AICTE/UGC-recognised institute/university, in the relevant discipline — Electronics (Electronics / Electronics & Communication / Electronics & Telecommunication / Communication / Telecommunication), Mechanical (Mechanical), Electrical (Electrical / Electrical & Electronics). AMIE/Grad IETE in the relevant Electronics disciplines is also accepted. Post-qualification experience: nil. Cut-off date for age and qualification: 01 November 2026.
    • Pay and tenure: Trainee Engineer-I Rs. 40,000 per month in the 1st year; Trainee Engineer-II Rs. 45,000 in the 2nd year; Trainee Engineer-III Rs. 50,000 in the 3rd year. Engagement is initially for TWO years as Trainee Engineer-I, extendable by one more year based on project requirement and performance. After 2 years, candidates meeting the criteria and performance (80%+ in annual appraisal, no disciplinary proceedings) may advance to Project Engineer-I. Additional benefits: Rs. 12,000 per annum for attire/safety shoes, Rs. 12,000 per annum insurance premium, retention bonus Rs. 10,000 per year (subject to conditions), gratuity as per the Act, and 10% tough-location allowance where applicable.
    • Fee: Rs. 150 + 18% GST (Rs. 177 in total) per application, payable via SBI Collect (category 'Trainee Engineer – Panchkula Unit'); the SBI Collect reference number must be entered in the application. Exempt: SC, ST and PwBD candidates and Ex-servicemen. Fee is non-refundable.
    • Selection: purely on the basis of a Written Test, in merit order category-wise and discipline-wise. The test carries 100 marks with negative marking of 1/4th mark for each incorrect response. Minimum qualifying marks: General/OBC (NCL) 35%, SC/ST/PwBD 30%. Tie-break: overall percentage in the qualifying degree. No TA is payable for the written test. Written test location: Chandigarh.
    • Photo/signature: the advertisement lists 'Recent Photograph and Signatures' among the documents to be uploaded, but gives no dimensions, file size or format — upload what the portal asks for.
    • All facts above are from Advt. No. 2026-27/11/PK/TE/006 (bel-india.in) and the verified apply portal jobapply.in/BEL2026PANCHKULATE/.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the 12-page advertisement (Advt. No. 2026-27/11/PK/TE/006) fully on bel-india.in before applying.
    • Pay the fee first on SBI Collect (category 'Trainee Engineer – Panchkula Unit') and note the reference number.
    • Apply only on the verified portal jobapply.in/BEL2026PANCHKULATE/ — its registration page shows 'Selection of Trainee Engineers for Panchkula Unit' with last date 10-Dec-2026.
    • Check your discipline's eligibility carefully — Electronics covers Electronics / ECE / E&TC / Communication / Telecommunication.
    • Save the registration details and application number after submitting the form.

    Avoid this

    • Do not treat this as a permanent BEL job — these are fixed-tenure Trainee Engineer posts, initially 2 years, extendable by 1 more year.
    • Do not forget the SBI Collect fee step — the SBI Collect reference number must be entered in the application form.
    • Do not apply in the wrong discipline — Electronics, Mechanical and Electrical have separate vacancy lists.
    • Do not miss the last date — online applications close on 10 December 2026.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • AMIE/Grad IETE certificate, if applying through that route (Electronics)
