    Eligibility & fees

    • Posts: Assistant Grade-3 — 1,206; ASI (District Unit Cadre) — 279; Registrar Clerk — 50; ASI (General Branch) — 40; SI-A Stenographer — 34; Stenographer (Hindi) — 30; Data Entry Operator — 25; Steno-Typist — 23; Stenographer (Bilingual) — 13; Stenographer (English) — 3.
    • Qualification and age are post-wise — these are Graduate and Post-Graduate level Class-III posts, and the detailed advertisement's eligibility table on the portal is final. Check your post's row carefully before paying the fee.
    • Selection is through a single written examination on 10 January 2027 (10:00 AM–12:15 PM) at the five divisional headquarters. Admit cards are expected around 4 January 2027.
    • A correction window runs from 28 to 30 November 2026 for mistakes in the submitted form.
    • The application fee is charged online by category on the CGSSB portal while you apply.
    • Special rule of this board: the exam fee is refunded to Chhattisgarh local-resident candidates who actually appear in the written examination.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The advertisement does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — upload them in the format and size the portal asks for on the upload screen.
    • Keep a recent colour photograph and a clear signature scan (signed on white paper) ready before you start.
    • Upload only clear, readable scans; a blurred photo or signature can get the form rejected.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the post-wise eligibility table in the detailed advertisement before choosing a post.
    • Apply by 27 November 2026 (5:00 PM) — the window is short.
    • Use the correction window (28–30 November) for any genuine mistake.
    • CG local residents: keep your domicile certificate ready — the fee refund and reservation both depend on it.
    • Note your exam centre division; the exam is held only at the five divisional headquarters.

    Avoid this

    • Do not assume every post needs the same qualification — eligibility is post-wise.
    • Do not skip appearing in the exam if you want the fee refund — it is paid only to those who actually appear.
    • Do not apply on any site other than the official CGSSB portal.
    • Do not wait for the correction window to fix a wrong post choice if the notice treats it as final — read the correction rules first.
    • Do not upload a blurred photo or signature.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Degree / PG marksheets and certificates scanned (as your post requires)
    • Chhattisgarh domicile certificate scanned, if applicable
    • Caste certificate scanned, if applicable
    • 10th certificate (age proof) scanned
    • Photo and signature scans ready
    • Application fee payment method ready

    Documents you will need

    • Chhattisgarh domicile certificate (for reservation and fee refund)
    • Caste certificate, if applicable
    • 10th certificate for date of birth
    • Photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID)

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Choosing a post without reading its own qualification row in the eligibility table.
    • Missing the 5:00 PM close on 27 November — the window ends earlier in the day than most portals.
    • Forgetting that the fee refund applies only to CG local residents who actually sit the exam.
    • Letting the 28–30 November correction window pass with a wrong detail still in the form.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open cgssb.cgstate.gov.in and read the detailed advertisement for Exam G20227.
    2. Register and fill the application for your post before 27 November 2026, 5:00 PM.
    3. Upload photo, signature and documents, and pay the fee online by category.
    4. Use the correction window from 28 to 30 November 2026 if anything is wrong.
    5. Download the admit card around 4 January 2027 and appear in the written exam on 10 January 2027.
