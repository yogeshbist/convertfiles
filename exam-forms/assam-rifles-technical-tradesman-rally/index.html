    Eligibility & fees

    • 354 Group C posts in 17 trades — Warrant Officer (Cipher) 124, Rifleman (Electrical Fitter Signal) 32, Rifleman (Armourer) 29, Rifleman (Mason) 29, Rifleman (Vehicle Mechanic/Fitter) 23, Rifleman (Electrician Mechanic Vehicle) 22, Rifleman (Lineman Field) 21, Rifleman (Metalsmith) 19, Rifleman (Store Keeper Technical) 16, Rifleman (Recovery Vehicle Mechanic) 13, Rifleman (Engineer Equipment Mechanic) 10, Havildar (Instrument Repairer/Mechanic) 6, Rifleman (Upholster) 3, Rifleman (Welder) 2, Warrant Officer (Draughtsman) 2, Warrant Officer (Physiotherapist) 2, Warrant Officer (Operation Theatre Technician) 1. The notification is Memo No. I.12016/A Branch (Rect Cell)/2026/1892, dated 3 November 2026 (Employment News 14–20 November 2026).
    • Male Indian citizens only, from across India — a valid domicile or Permanent Resident Certificate (PRC) is required. Persons with disabilities are not eligible for this rally. Vacancies are all-India, all-class — there is no state-wise quota. 10% of vacancies in every trade are reserved for ex-servicemen.
    • Age as on 1 July 2026: 18–23 years for most trades; 18–25 years for Cipher, Recovery Vehicle Mechanic and Draughtsman. Relaxation: SC/ST +5 years, OBC +3 years, ex-servicemen as per rules. Only the date of birth on the matriculation (10th) certificate is accepted.
    • Qualification is trade-wise: Cipher — 12th pass + 2-year ITI in Radio & Television or Electronics, or a Bachelor's degree in Electronics or IT. Most Rifleman trades — 10th pass, with an ITI in the trade where specified (Lineman Field, Engineer Equipment Mechanic, Recovery Vehicle Mechanic, Electrician Mechanic Vehicle, Metalsmith, Upholster, Welder). Havildar (Instrument Repairer/Mechanic) — 12th pass + ITI in Instrumentation. Draughtsman — 12th + 3-year Diploma in Architectural Assistantship. Operation Theatre Technician — 12th + Diploma in Operation Theatre Technician. Physiotherapist — 12th + Diploma in Physiotherapy.
    • Selection in six stages: Physical Standard Test (height/chest/weight) and document check → Physical Efficiency Test (5 km run in 24 minutes; 1.6 km in 7 minutes for Ladakh) → Computer Based Test, 100 marks (pass 35% for General/EWS, 33% for SC/ST/OBC) → Trade Skill Test (qualifying only) → Document Verification and Detailed Medical Examination (Review Medical Examination available) → trade-wise, category-wise merit list.
    • Physical standards: height 170 cm (165 cm for Garhwalis, Kumaonis, Dogras, Marathas and candidates from Assam, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, Arunachal Pradesh, Meghalaya, Himachal Pradesh and Kashmir/Leh/Ladakh; 162.5 cm for ST), chest 80/85 cm (78/83 cm and 76/81 cm for the relaxed groups). PST/PET are held with RFID tracking at Jorhat or Diphu (Assam); the rally is tentatively scheduled from 18 January 2027 onwards.
    • Pay and allowances as applicable to Assam Rifles personnel — 7th CPC levels: Rifleman Level 3 (₹21,700–69,100), Havildar Level 4 (₹25,500–81,100), Warrant Officer Level 5 (₹29,200–92,300), plus DA, HRA and ration money. Selected candidates serve a 2-year probation.
    • ₹100 for General/OBC/EWS candidates — pay online, or in cash at any SBI branch into SBI Current Account No. 37088046712 (HQ DGAR, Recruitment Branch, Shillong). Upload the receipt/challan online and carry the original to the PST/PET. The fee is non-refundable.
    • SC, ST and ex-servicemen candidates pay no fee.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The notification does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — upload them in the format and size the portal asks for on the upload screen.
    • Keep your domicile/PRC certificate and category certificate ready — the state and trade cannot be changed after submission.
    • Carry the printed application form and a copy of the notification to the PST/PET — both are required there.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only on assamrifles.gov.in from 15 November 2026 — use the 'Online Application' option for this rally.
    • Choose your trade and state of domicile very carefully — neither can be changed later.
    • Keep your domicile/PRC and category certificates ready before you start.
    • Pay the ₹100 fee (if applicable) and upload the receipt/challan; carry the original to the PST/PET.
    • Start running practice now — the PET is a 5 km run in 24 minutes.

    Avoid this

    • Do not submit more than one application — multiple applications are rejected.
    • Do not expect any correction window — no changes are allowed after submission.
    • Do not apply if you are a female candidate or a person with disability — this rally is male-only and PwD candidates are not eligible.
    • Do not use the earlier (25 September 2026) advertisement — it was cancelled on 30 October 2026; only the 3 November 2026 notification counts, and any fee paid under the cancelled one is refunded to the same bank account.
