    Eligibility & fees

    • LATERAL recruitment — you must be in employment at the time of application and at interview/selection, and must be properly relieved at joining.
    • Deputy General Manager E-7 (Mechanical/Electrical/Civil): Bachelor Degree in the relevant discipline + 19 years' experience, including 5 years in Coal/Lignite Thermal Power Projects of 500 MW or above (Civil: 19 years in the power sector, 5 years in planning/construction/execution of power projects).
    • Executive Engineer E-4: Bachelor Degree in the relevant discipline + 5 years in the notified area (Thermal/Mines/Projects/C&I/Environmental/Chemical/Mining/Safety); Executive Engineer (Mining) also needs a First Class Mine Manager Certificate of Competency under Coal Mine Regulations, 2017.
    • Assistant Executive Manager (Scientific) E-2: M.Sc. Chemistry (Analytical/Organic/Inorganic/Physical) + 4 years' R&D/Analytical experience in Fuel/Water Laboratories of Thermal Power Plants.
    • Deputy Manager (HR) E-3: Degree + PG in HR/Social Work/Business Administration or PG Degree/Diploma of at least 2 years in HRM/Personnel Management/Industrial Relations/Labour Welfare + 1 year in HR/Personnel/IR/Labour welfare/HRD.
    • Deputy Manager (Legal) E-3: LLB + enrolled as an Advocate + 1 year as a practising advocate or in a Legal Department of a PSU/Nationalised Bank/Government/listed company.
    • Additional Chief Manager (Security) E-6: any degree + the specified Armed Forces/paramilitary service (Major/Commandant or above, 5 years in security management) or Chief/In-charge of security in a 10,000+ workforce organisation with 13 years' executive experience. Assistant Manager (Security) E-1: any degree + Hon'ry Lieutenant/Captain rank or Executive/In-Charge Security in a 5,000+ workforce organisation.
    • Age as on 1 November 2026 (already including relaxations): E-7 52/55/57/57, E-6 47/50/52/52, E-4 36/39/41/41, E-3 32/35/37/37, E-2 30/33/35/35, E-1 30/33/35/35 (UR/EWS, OBC, SC, ST). Maximum age 58 years. Read Advt No. 11/2026 fully on nlcindia.in before applying.
    • ₹854 for UR/EWS/OBC (NCL) candidates — ₹500 application fee + ₹354 processing fee (₹300 + ₹54 GST at 18%), payable online by SBI E-Collect, by 19 December 2026, 11:45 PM.
    • ₹354 for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates — processing fee only (application fee exempt), inclusive of 18% GST. Non-refundable.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advertisement No. 11/2026 fully on nlcindia.in before applying — check the exact qualification, experience and age for your post (25 different posts).
    • Apply only through the official NLC recruitment portal (web.nlcindia.in/rec112026) between 20 November and 19 December 2026, 5:00 PM.
    • Use a separate application and a unique email ID for each post you apply for — and upload documentary proof of the required area and length of experience.
    • Pay the fee by 19 December 2026, 11:45 PM (₹854 UR/EWS/OBC; ₹354 SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-SM) via SBI E-Collect; complete the final submission by 22 December 2026, 5:00 PM.
    • Check the private-sector minimum CTC rule (50%/60%/70% of the grade CTC) before applying — it applies at application and at interview/selection.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply if you are not currently in employment — this is a lateral recruitment for experienced candidates only.
    • Do not use the same email ID for two posts — each post needs a separate form and a unique email ID.
    • Do not apply for a post whose notified experience area does not match yours — experience other than the notified area will not be considered, and qualifications other than the notified branch will not be considered.
    • Do not wait for the last day — registration closes 19 December 2026 at 5:00 PM and fee payment at 11:45 PM.
