    J&K Bank PO & Banking Associate form: photo and signature size — live photo rules, signature 10-20 KB

    5 min read · Convert Files guides

    J&K Bank is hiring 1,397 freshers — 343 Probationary Officers and 1,054 Banking Associates — through IBPS, and applications close on 26 November 2026. Most bank forms ask for a photo and a signature; this one asks for five things. Along with the uploaded photograph (20-50 KB), the form also captures a live photograph through your webcam or phone while you fill it. Then come the signature (10-20 KB), the left thumb impression (20-50 KB) and a hand-written declaration (50-100 KB), plus your certificates as PDFs. Every size below is from section (c) of the bank's detailed notification (No. JKB/HR/Rectt/2026-492, dated 7 November 2026) — prepare all five before you start, because the portal gives you no time to fix files mid-form.

    The photograph — 20 to 50 KB, 200 × 230 pixels

    Use a recent, passport-style colour photograph, 4.5 cm × 3.5 cm, taken against a light-coloured — preferably white — background, with your face looking straight at the camera. The file must be a JPG or JPEG of 200 × 230 pixels and between 20 KB and 50 KB. If your photo is some other size, set the exact pixels first with the image resizer, then bring the file into the band with the 50 KB image compressor — aim for about 40 KB, safely inside the band. Do not use an old or heavily edited photo: it has to match the live photograph the form is about to capture.

    The live photograph — captured while you fill the form

    This is the step that surprises people. Uploading a photo file is not enough: the form also captures a live photograph of you, through your computer's webcam or by scanning a QR code with your phone. So fill the form yourself, in person, in a well-lit place — a cyber café operator cannot do this step for you unless you are sitting there. Sit against a plain, light background with the light falling on your face, not behind you; look straight into the camera; keep your face uncovered — no cap, no dark glasses, and no shadow falling across your face. If the live capture does not look like your uploaded photo, the bank can question the application, so use a recent uploaded photo that actually looks like you do now.

    The signature — 10 to 20 KB, never in capitals

    Sign with a black ink pen on plain white paper, scan or photograph just the signature cropped tight, and save it as a JPG or JPEG of 140 × 60 pixels and between 10 KB and 20 KB — if the scan is heavy, bring it down with the 20 KB image compressor. Two warnings from the notification itself. First, a signature in CAPITAL LETTERS is not accepted. Second, the signature you upload is the one you will have to reproduce at the exam: if the signature on the attendance sheet does not match the uploaded one, you can be disqualified. Sign the way you normally sign, not the way that looks neatest.

    Thumb impression and hand-written declaration

    Press your left thumb in black or blue ink on white paper — clean and complete, not smudged — and scan it at 240 × 240 pixels at 200 DPI, which is a 3 cm × 3 cm image, as a JPG or JPEG between 20 KB and 50 KB; if your left thumb cannot be used, the right thumb is allowed. The declaration is a short statement, in the bank's prescribed wording, in which you confirm that the information and documents in your application are true and that you will produce the originals when asked — write it out yourself, by hand, in English only, in black ink on white paper (capitals are not accepted here either), and scan it at 800 × 400 pixels at 200 DPI (10 cm × 5 cm), as a JPG or JPEG between 50 KB and 100 KB. If the scan crosses 100 KB, trim it with the 100 KB image compressor.

    Certificates — PDF only, one combined file, 500 KB each

    Certificates go as PDFs, each file up to 500 KB. The notification is specific about clubbing: all your educational certificates go in ONE single PDF file, and all experience certificates in one single PDF — not a separate file per certificate. Scan the documents at a minimum of 200 DPI, and scan the photograph-type images in True Colour. If you only have photos of your certificates, combine them into one PDF with the images to PDF tool, and if the combined file crosses 500 KB, bring it down with the 500 KB PDF compressor.     Why J&K Bank applications get rejected

    The usual reasons are all size and matching problems: a photo outside the 20-50 KB band or not 200 × 230 pixels; a signature in capital letters, or different from the one signed at the exam; a smudged thumb impression; a declaration typed on a computer instead of written by hand; certificates uploaded as separate files, in JPG instead of PDF, or over 500 KB; and a live capture taken in poor light, with a cap or glasses, that does not match the uploaded photo. Check each file's size and dimensions once before you open the form — the last date is 26 November 2026, and no correction window has been announced to fix a bad upload later.

    Questions

    What photo size does the J&K Bank PO form need?
    A recent colour photograph, 4.5 cm × 3.5 cm, 200 × 230 pixels, JPG or JPEG between 20 KB and 50 KB, against a light-coloured, preferably white, background. The form also captures a live photograph of you through a webcam or your phone while you apply — both are compulsory.
    What is the live photograph in the J&K Bank form?
    Besides the photo file you upload, the application captures your photograph live, either through your computer's webcam or by scanning a QR code with your phone. Sit in good light against a plain background, face the camera directly, and wear no cap or dark glasses, so the live capture matches your uploaded photo.
    Can I sign in capital letters on the J&K Bank form?
    No. The notification says a signature in capital letters is not accepted, and the same rule applies to the hand-written declaration. Sign in your normal running hand, and use exactly that signature at the exam — a mismatch with the signature on the attendance sheet can disqualify you.
    What size are the thumb impression and declaration files?
    The left thumb impression is a JPG or JPEG of 240 × 240 pixels at 200 DPI (3 cm × 3 cm), between 20 KB and 50 KB, in black or blue ink on white paper, not smudged. The hand-written declaration, written by you in English in black ink, is 800 × 400 pixels at 200 DPI (10 cm × 5 cm), between 50 KB and 100 KB.
    How do I upload my certificates, and what size?
    Certificates are uploaded as PDFs only, each up to 500 KB. Put all educational certificates in one single combined PDF and all experience certificates in one single combined PDF, scanned at a minimum of 200 DPI. Do not upload one file per certificate.
