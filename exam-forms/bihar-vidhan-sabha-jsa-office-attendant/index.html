    Eligibility & fees

    • 20 posts — Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) 15 and Office Attendant 5, in the Bihar Vidhan Sabha Secretariat.
    • JSA: Intermediate (10+2) in any subject. Office Attendant: Matric (10th) pass. The qualification must be held by 6 December 2026.
    • Age: minimum 18 years as on 1 August 2026; maximum 37 years for UR men, 40 for UR women and BC/EBC candidates, 42 for SC/ST. Extra relaxation: Bihar government servants +5 years, PwD +10 years, ex-servicemen +3 years plus service, with an overall cap of 57 years. Reservation applies only to Bihar domiciles.
    • Selection — JSA: an OMR preliminary exam (100 MCQs, 400 marks, +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one, 2 hours; qualifying 40% General, 36.5% BC, 34% EBC, 32% SC/ST/women/PwD), followed by Hindi and English typing tests at 30 words per minute each (Hindi in Kruti Dev font, 10 minutes, 1.5% error allowance). There is no interview. Office Attendant: an interview of 100 marks (a preliminary OMR exam is held only if applicants exceed 40,000).
    • Apply online on blasrecruit.in from 7 November 2026 (11:00 AM) to 6 December 2026. There is NO correction facility — the submission is final.
    • JSA: ₹600; Office Attendant: ₹400 — for Bihar-domicile EBC/BC/EWS/UR men and all out-of-state candidates. Bihar-domicile SC/ST, PwD (40% and above) and all Bihar-domicile women pay ₹150 (JSA) / ₹100 (Office Attendant). No category is fully exempt.
    • Pay the fee online by 6 December 2026.

    • Do not apply for JSA without Intermediate (10+2).
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes on 6 December 2026.
