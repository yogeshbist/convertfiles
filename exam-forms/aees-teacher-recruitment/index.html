    Eligibility & fees

    • Posts: 254 (tentative) — Principal 4, Vice-Principal 1, PGT 27, TGT 102, Librarian 13, Primary Teacher 89, Primary Teacher (Music) 10, Balvatika Teacher 8. Vacancies are tentative and may increase or decrease.
    • Age (as on 22 November 2026): Principal 35–50 years; Vice-Principal 35–45; PGT 40 or below; TGT and Librarian 35 or below; PRT, PRT (Music) and Balvatika 30 or below. Minimum age 18. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, Women 10 years (PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT/Balvatika), PwBD 10/13/15 years.
    • Education: Principal — Master's (50%) + B.Ed (50%) + 12 years' service as PGT or 3 years as Vice-Principal; Vice-Principal — Master's (50%) + B.Ed (50%) + 6 years as PGT; PGT — Master's in the subject (50%) + B.Ed (50%); TGT — Bachelor's in the subject (50%) + B.Ed (50%) + CTET Paper-II; Librarian — Bachelor's in Library Science (50%); PRT — 10+2 (50%) + D.El.Ed/B.El.Ed + CTET Paper-I; PRT (Music) — 10+2 (50%) + Bachelor's in Music/Performing Arts; Balvatika — 10+2 (50%, with English) + 2-year NTT/Pre-School/ECE diploma or B.Ed (Nursery).
    • CTET: a valid CTET certificate (issued on or before 22 November 2026) is essential for PRT and TGT posts (except Computer Science, Art Education and P&HE). CTET qualifying marks: 60% for UR, 55% for reserved categories.
    • Selection: Computer Based Test (MCQ, no negative marking) — Principal/Vice-Principal 150 questions/150 marks in 2 hours 30 minutes; PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT 120 questions/120 marks in 2 hours. CBT qualifying: 60% (UR/EWS) or 54% (SC/ST/OBC/PwBD). Then a skill test (chalk-and-board teaching demo, Go/No Go) for PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT, or an interview for Principal/Vice-Principal (50:50 CBT:interview). CBT centres in 44 cities; skill test/interview only at AEES, Anushaktinagar, Mumbai.
    • Posting: selected candidates may be posted at any of the 30 AEES schools across 15 centres in India. Probation: 2 years.
    • ₹750 application fee + ₹250 processing fee = ₹1,000 in total.
    • Women, SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen candidates pay no application fee, but every applicant must pay the ₹250 processing fee — so ₹250 in total for them.
    • GST as applicable is charged extra by the bank. The fee is paid online only and is not refunded.
    • The fee is charged separately for each post applied for — and within PGTs you may apply for only one subject, and within TGTs for only one subject.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The notice does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — the sizes are given on the online application portal, so upload them in the format and size the portal asks for.
    • Recent colour photograph (JPG/JPEG; PNG also for Principal) and a clear signature scan; scan documents with a proper scanner, not a mobile phone camera, or the form may be rejected.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply for only one subject within PGTs and only one subject within TGTs — but you may apply for different posts separately, paying the fee for each.
    • Check that your CTET certificate is valid and was issued on or before 22 November 2026 (for PRT and TGT).
    • Keep your Police Verification Certificate ready — it must not be older than 6 months at the skill test/interview.
    • Read the post-wise qualification in the official notice before applying — Principal and Vice-Principal need years of service, not just degrees.
    • Submit before 22 November 2026, 11:50 PM and save/print the confirmation page — the printout is needed at document verification.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for two PGT subjects or two TGT subjects — only one subject is allowed in each group.
    • Do not apply for PRT/TGT without a valid CTET certificate (except CS, Art and P&HE TGT).
    • Do not scan documents with a mobile phone camera — the notice says to use a proper scanner.
    • Do not send the printout of the confirmation page to AEES — keep it for document verification.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the fee and the form both close on 22 November 2026, 11:50 PM.

    • Applying for two PGT subjects or two TGT subjects — only one subject is allowed in each group.
    • Applying for PRT/TGT without a valid CTET certificate.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open aees.onlineregistrationforms.com and read the official notice 01/2026.
    2. Choose your post (and one subject, for PGT/TGT) and register with a valid email ID and mobile number.
    3. Fill the form and upload your recent colour photograph, signature and documents in the format and size the portal asks for.
    4. Pay the fee online — ₹1,000 (₹250 for Women/SC/ST/PwBD/ESM, the processing fee only) — separately for each post.
    5. Submit before 22 November 2026, 11:50 PM and save/print the confirmation page.
    6. Prepare for the CBT (no negative marking) and keep your Police Verification Certificate ready for the skill test/interview.

    What is the last date to apply for AEES Teacher Recruitment (Principal, PGT, TGT, PRT)?

    What size should the photo and signature be?
    The notice does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — the sizes are given on the online application portal, so upload them in the format and size the portal asks for. Recent colour photograph (JPG/JPEG; PNG also for Principal) and a clear signature scan; scan documents with a proper scanner, not a mobile phone camera, or the form may be rejected.
    Do I need CTET for PGT posts in AEES?
    No. CTET is essential only for PRT and TGT posts (except TGT Computer Science, Art Education and P&HE). PGT posts need a Master's in the subject (50%) plus B.Ed (50%), and Librarian posts need a Bachelor's in Library Science.
    Is there negative marking in the AEES CBT?
    No. The CBT is MCQ-based with no negative marking — Principal/Vice-Principal 150 questions in 2 hours 30 minutes; PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT 120 questions in 2 hours.
    Can I apply for two PGT subjects?
    No. Within PGTs you may apply for only one subject, and within TGTs for only one subject. You may apply for different posts separately, paying the fee for each.
    What is the fee for women candidates?
    Women candidates pay no application fee, but every applicant must pay the ₹250 processing fee — so ₹250 in total (plus GST as applicable).
