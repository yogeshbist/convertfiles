    Eligibility & fees

    • 1,393 vacancies — Technical Assistant (T-3) 1,254 and Senior Technical Assistant (T-4) 139, in ICAR institutes across India. The notification (F. No. 2(2)/2025-Exam) is dated 11 November 2026.
    • Education: a relevant Bachelor's degree for the functional group you choose. A 3-year engineering diploma is accepted only for Workshop Staff groups. Choose only ONE functional group/subgroup — applying for more than one group is not allowed.
    • Age: 21–30 years as on 17 December 2026, with relaxation as per Government of India rules.
    • Selection: Prelims (100 questions, 100 marks, 1 hour, −0.25 per wrong answer; qualifying 30% UR, 25% EWS/OBC, 20% SC/ST/PwBD) then Mains (100 questions, 300 marks, 2 hours, −1 per wrong answer, same qualifying percentages). There is no interview. Prelims are tentatively in February 2027.
    • Apply online only on asrb.gov.in from 18 November to 17 December 2026, 11:59 PM. Correction window: 18–20 December 2026.
    • Fee per post: ₹300 for UR, ₹150 for EWS/OBC. Applying for both posts: ₹600 (UR) / ₹300 (EWS/OBC). SC/ST/PwBD/women and ex-servicemen pay nothing. Pay through Bharatkosh and upload the receipt with the form.
    • Pay the fee before the final submission on 17 December 2026, 11:59 PM.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: JPG, maximum 200 KB, white background, face covering at least two-thirds of the frame, no spectacles. Signature: JPG, maximum 100 KB, in black or blue ink.
    • Upload the Bharatkosh fee receipt with the application — the form is incomplete without it.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Choose only ONE functional group/subgroup — more than one group is not allowed.
    • Check the relevant Bachelor's degree for your group in the notification before applying.
    • Pay through Bharatkosh and upload the receipt with the form.
    • Upload a white-background photo (max 200 KB, face at least two-thirds of the frame, no spectacles).
    • Use the correction window (18–20 December 2026) if you made a mistake — it is only 3 days.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for more than one functional group.
    • Do not apply with a 3-year engineering diploma for a non-Workshop group — it is accepted only for Workshop Staff groups.
    • Do not forget the Bharatkosh receipt — the form is incomplete without it.
    • Do not miss the 17 December 2026, 11:59 PM deadline.
