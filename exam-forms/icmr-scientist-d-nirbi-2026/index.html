    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • Master's degree in a subject related to Biomedical Sciences from a recognised university, OR Pharm-D recognised by UGC/PCI.
    • 8 years' essential experience in Research & Development related to Biomedical Sciences, relevant to the job (PhD counts as 4 years, MD/MS as 3 years, MVSc as 2 years towards this).
    • Age not exceeding 45 years as on the closing date, 31 December 2026 (relaxable per Government of India instructions).
    • All eligibility conditions must be fulfilled on the last date of submission of the application.
    • ₹1,500 (non-refundable), payable online through the link given in the application form (Net Banking / Credit Card / Debit Card).
    • No fee for SC / ST / Women / PwD / EWS candidates.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 1 vacancy — Scientist-D (Non-Medical) — initial posting at ICMR-National Institute for Research in Bacterial Infections (NIRBI), Kolkata, with all-India transfer liability under the Council.
    • Pay: Level 12 of the Pay Matrix, ₹78,800–₹2,09,200 (7th CPC) plus usual allowances as admissible to ICMR employees. Appointment on REGULAR basis under Direct Recruitment.
    • Advt. No. ICMR/Rectt/Sc-D/02/2026-RPCell. The notification PDF is digitally signed 20.11.2026; no separate printed advertisement date appears on it.
    • Essential qualification: Master's degree from a recognised university in a subject related to Biomedical Sciences OR Pharm-D degree recognised by UGC/PCI. Essential experience: 8 years in Research & Development related to Biomedical Sciences, relevant to the job, in Govt. / Public sector / Private institutions.
    • How experience is counted: PhD counts as 4 years' experience, MD/MS as 3 years, MVSc as 2 years; MPH / M.Tech / ME counts as no experience. The period spent obtaining the minimum essential qualification does not count towards experience.
    • Desirable: PhD from a recognised university in Biophysics / Molecular Biology / Biochemistry, with experience in Research & Development in Biomedical Sciences — research on structural biology using Cryo-Electron Microscope (Cryo-EM) for biological specimens, in particular macromolecular complexes, with Cryo-EM reconstruction algorithms and computational skills.
    • Age: upper age limit 45 years as on the closing date (31 December 2026), relaxable in accordance with Government of India instructions. Eligibility (age, qualification, experience) is judged on the last date of submission.
    • Selection: Computer-Based Test (MCQs) or interview, or both. The CBT, if conducted, is qualifying in nature and based on research methods, techniques and areas of expertise relevant to the post. Shortlisted candidates may be further shortlisted if numbers are high.
    • Dates: online applications up to 31 December 2026 till 5:30 PM. No apply start date is stated in the notice — the ICMR listing says the portal for this advertisement 'shall be live shortly'. The form-opening date shown on this page is 20 November 2026, the date the notice was digitally signed. When checked on 23 November 2026, recruitment.icmr.org.in listed 11 vacancies and the Scientist-D (NIRBI) form was NOT yet among them, so check the portal before applying.
    • All facts above are from Advt. No. ICMR/Rectt/Sc-D/02/2026-RPCell (5 pages, icmr.gov.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only on the official portal https://recruitment.icmr.org.in — register first, then log in with the User ID and password sent to your email and mobile.
    • Upload self-attested certificates from Matric onwards, detailed marks, date of birth proof, category certificate and experience certificates.
    • Keep the final printout of the submitted form — a hard copy with all uploaded documents may be asked for later.
    • Check icmr.gov.in regularly — all further communication about this recruitment will be displayed on the ICMR website only.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply offline — no offline form or downloaded form copy is accepted.
    • Do not submit more than one application — duplicate applications from one applicant will cancel all of them.
    • Do not count the years spent getting your minimum essential qualification as experience — the notice expressly excludes them.
    • Do not wait for the last date — the portal for this advertisement was not yet live on 23 November 2026, so apply as soon as the form appears.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Master's / Pharm-D degree certificate and mark sheets (Matric onwards)
    • Experience certificates — 8 years in Biomedical Sciences R&D (PhD/MD/MS/MVSc counting as per the notice)

    • Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD), if applicable
    • Recent passport-size photo (unattested; no cap or goggles; spectacles allowed)
    • Valid email ID and mobile number for registration
