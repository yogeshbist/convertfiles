    Eligibility & fees

    • B.Tech/B.E. (50%) / MCA / M.Tech/M.Sc in Computer Science/IT/Electronics/ECE/Cyber Security.
    • 15–20 years' post-qualification experience in IT/Information Security, including a minimum of 5 years' core Information Security experience at senior management level.
    • Maximum age 50 years as on 31 October 2026.
    • Preferred certifications: CISSP, CISM, CCISO, CISA. Read CRPD/SCO/2026-27/36 fully on sbi.bank.in before applying.
    • ₹750 for General/EWS/OBC candidates, payable online by 3 December 2026.
    • No fee for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 1 post — General Manager & Head-CSTRA (Cyber Security Threat & Risk Assessment), UR, Specialist Cadre Officer (CRPD/SCO/2026-27/36).
    • Maximum age 50 years as on 31 October 2026.
    • Qualification: B.Tech/B.E. (50%) / MCA / M.Tech/M.Sc in Computer Science/IT/Electronics/ECE/Cyber Security; preferred certifications CISSP, CISM, CCISO, CISA.
    • Experience: 15–20 years' post-qualification experience in IT/Information Security, including a minimum of 5 years' core Information Security experience at senior management level.
    • Tenure: fixed term 5 years (3 years + 2 years on extension). Posting suggested: Navi Mumbai. CTC is negotiable — 80:20 fixed:variable.
    • Selection: shortlisting, followed by a 100-mark interview (merit on interview only), followed by CTC negotiation.
    • Dates: online registration and fee payment 19 November–3 December 2026. Apply on SBI's own recruitment portal — recruitment.sbi.bank.in (CRPD/SCO/2026-27/36), NOT on the IBPS portal.
    • All facts above are from Detailed Advertisement CRPD/SCO/2026-27/36, SBI (10-page notification, sbi.bank.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Detailed Advertisement CRPD/SCO/2026-27/36 fully on sbi.bank.in before applying — check the exact experience and certification requirements.
    • Apply only through SBI's own recruitment portal (recruitment.sbi.bank.in/crpd-sco-2026-27-36/apply) between 19 November and 3 December 2026.
    • Pay the ₹750 fee online by 3 December 2026 (General/EWS/OBC; SC/ST/PwBD exempt) and save the application printout.
    • Keep your degree, certification and experience certificates ready before starting the form.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply on the IBPS portal — this recruitment is on SBI's own portal (recruitment.sbi.bank.in).
    • Do not apply without the required senior-level Information Security experience — a minimum of 5 years' core InfoSec experience at senior management level is required.
    • Do not treat this as a regular permanent post — it is a fixed-term engagement of 5 years (3+2).
    • Do not wait for the last day — registration and fee payment both close on 3 December 2026.
