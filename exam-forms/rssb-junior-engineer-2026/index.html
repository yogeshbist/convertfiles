    Eligibility & fees

    • Engineering Diploma or Degree in the relevant trade/branch from a recognised institute in India (JE Civil — Degree or Diploma; JE Civil Electrical — Degree or Diploma in Electrical).
    • Age as on 1 January 2027: minimum 18 years, maximum 40 years. Age relaxation extra as per RSSB Junior Engineer Recruitment Rules (Advt No. 10/2026).
    • Read Advt No. 10/2026 fully on rssb.rajasthan.gov.in before applying — post-wise trade and department details differ.
    • ₹600 for General/OBC/Other-State candidates — pay online by 17 December 2026.
    • ₹400 for OBC-NCL/MBC/EWS/SC/ST/PH candidates of Rajasthan.
    • Correction charge: ₹300 (correction window 22–31 December 2026).

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 1,909 Junior Engineer posts across Rajasthan departments: Public Works Department 403, Water Resources Department 251, Public Health Engineering Department 294, Rajasthan State Disaster Management Board 41, Urban Development & Housing 770, Local Self Government Department 150.
    • By qualification: Degree 1,241 posts, Diploma 619 posts, Electrical 49 posts (JE Civil Degree/Diploma and JE Civil Electrical).
    • This is a RE-OPENED window for Advt No. 10/2026 — online applications 18 November–17 December 2026. Correction window: 22–31 December 2026 (fee ₹300).
    • Apply through the Rajasthan Single Sign-On portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in) — log in with your SSO ID, then open the Recruitment Portal. The JE application itself sits behind the SSO login.
    • Written examination on 3–5 February 2027; admit card before the exam.
    • Age as on 1 January 2027: minimum 18 years, maximum 40 years (relaxation as per RSSB rules in Advt No. 10/2026).
    • All facts above are taken from Advt No. 10/2026 (Rajasthan Staff Selection Board).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advt No. 10/2026 fully on rssb.rajasthan.gov.in before applying — check the post-wise trade and department for your qualification.
    • Apply only through the Rajasthan SSO portal (sso.rajasthan.gov.in) between 18 November and 17 December 2026.
    • Keep your SSO ID ready — without it you cannot open the Recruitment Portal.
    • Keep your Diploma/Degree certificate, marksheet and a recent photo ready before applying.
    • Use the correction window (22–31 December 2026) to fix any mistake — each correction costs ₹300.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for a trade/branch your Diploma or Degree does not match.
    • Do not apply through any site other than the Rajasthan SSO portal.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes on 17 December 2026.
    • Do not pay the fee twice — one successful payment completes the form.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Date of birth proof
    • Caste / category certificate, if applicable
    • Recent passport-size photo and signature scan
    • Domicile certificate, if claiming the Rajasthan fee slab
