    Eligibility & fees

    • Intermediate (10+2) pass from the Uttarakhand Board, UP Board or an equivalent recognised board.
    • Age: minimum 18 years; maximum 25–35 years depending on the post, as on 1 July 2026. Check the post-wise age limit in Advt 84/2026 before applying.
    • Must meet the post-wise physical standards (height/chest) given in the notification.
    • Read Advt 84/2026 fully on sssc.uk.gov.in before applying — age, physical standards and selection stages differ by post.
    • ₹300 for General/OBC candidates — pay online by 25 December 2026.
    • ₹150 for SC/ST/EWS/Divyang candidates. Orphan candidates pay no fee.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 1,694 posts across 11 roles: District Police Constable (Male) 464, PAC/IRB Constable (Male) 284, District Police Constable (Female) 238, Fireman 201, Forest Guard 171, Special Tiger Guards 75, Jail Warder 117, Female Jail Warder 27, Secretariat Guard 54, Excise Constable 45, Transport Constable 18.
    • Apply online 25 November–25 December 2026 (11:59 PM) at sssc.uk.gov.in. Correction window: 28–30 December 2026.
    • Written examination tentatively on 4 April 2027, followed by physical standards/measurement and physical efficiency tests, medical examination and document verification — see Advt 84/2026 for the post-wise stages.
    • Age as on 1 July 2026: minimum 18 years; maximum varies by post (25–35 years) — check the post-wise limits in the notification.
    • Physical standards apply as per the notice (male height 165 cm, female height 152 cm).
    • Some websites quote 986 posts — that counts only the three police constable posts (464 + 284 + 238). The full Advt 84/2026 covers 1,694 posts across 11 roles.
    • All facts above are taken from Advt No. 84/UKSSSC/2026, dated 17 November 2026.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advt No. 84/UKSSSC/2026 fully on sssc.uk.gov.in before applying — check the post-wise age limit and physical standards for your post.
    • Apply only on the UKSSSC portal (sssc.uk.gov.in) between 25 November and 25 December 2026.
    • Keep your Intermediate marksheet, domicile/category certificate and a recent photo ready before the form opens.
    • Use the correction window (28–30 December 2026) to fix any mistake — after that no change is allowed.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for a post whose age limit or physical standard you do not meet — post-wise limits differ.
    • Do not quote the 986-post figure — the full advertisement covers 1,694 posts across 11 roles.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes on 25 December 2026 at 11:59 PM.
    • Do not pay any fee if you are an orphan candidate — you are exempt.

    1. sssc.uk.gov.in par Advt No. 84/UKSSSC/2026 padhen aur apni post chunen.
    2. 25 नवंबर 2026 se portal par registration karen aur form bharen.
