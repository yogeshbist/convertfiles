    Eligibility & fees

    • Graduate in any stream (B.E., B.Com., B.A., B.Sc., BCA etc.) from a recognised University/Institute as prescribed by AICTE or UGC.
    • Age 18–25 years as on 1 November 2026 (relaxation as per rules).
    • Must not have already undergone, or be undergoing, apprenticeship in the same trade under the Apprentices Act, 1961.
    • Register on the NATS 2.0 MIS portal (nats.education.gov.in) before applying — the enrolment/registration number is needed on the form.
    • Read the Mumbai Port Authority Graduate Apprentice 2026-27 notification fully on mumbaiport.gov.in before applying.
    • ₹100 by NEFT only, to Board of Mumbai Port Authority, State Bank of India, Mumbai Main Branch (Current A/c No. 10996685430, IFSC SBIN0000300).
    • Physically handicapped candidates need not pay the fee.
    • Application forms are not sold and are not sent by post.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 48 Graduate Apprentice seats for 2026-27, 12 months' training at the Apprentices Training Centre (ATC), Mechanical & Electrical Engineering Department, Mumbai Port Authority. Total stipend ₹12,300 per month — Establishment share ₹6,150 + Government share ₹6,150, paid through DBT.
    • The notification does not specify an application start date — the advertisement is live on mumbaiport.gov.in. The filled form, with documents and the fee (UTR) receipt, must reach the ATC by 18 December 2026, 5:00 PM, by hand or by post. Late or incomplete applications are rejected.
    • Age 18–25 years as on 1 November 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3 years, Divyang/PwD 10 years, as per Government rules (certificate required).
    • Selection: merit list on the basis of percentage of total marks in the Degree Examination. Tie: the older candidate ranks above. Preference is given to wards of Mumbai Port Authority employees, with separate merit lists for wards and other candidates. The list is displayed on the ATC notice board and on the website; listed candidates are called for verification of original documents at the ATC.
    • Selected candidates undergo a medical examination and must produce a fitness certificate from the Port Authority Hospital. Final selection is subject to BOAT registration.
    • This is a 12-month apprenticeship under the Apprentices Act, 1961 — NOT a permanent job. There is no obligation of employment after training. Candidates who have already undergone, or are undergoing, apprenticeship in the same trade under the Apprentices Act, 1961 are not eligible. Leaving the training midway makes the apprentice liable for the cost of training, up to a maximum of 3 months' stipend.
    • CGPA/grade-point conversion formula must be produced. Caste certificate must be produced in original at document verification. No TA/DA is paid for document verification or the medical examination. Help desk: 022-66566637 / 66566625, 10 AM–5 PM, except Saturdays, Sundays and holidays.
    • All facts above are taken from the Mumbai Port Authority Graduate Apprentice 2026-27 notification (mumbaiport.gov.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the notification fully on mumbaiport.gov.in before applying.
    • Register first on NATS 2.0 (nats.education.gov.in) and print the email copy mentioning the enrolment/registration number.
    • Download the application form from mumbaiport.gov.in (People & Career > Jobs > Advertisements) and fill it carefully.
    • Pay ₹100 by NEFT to the Board of Mumbai Port Authority account and keep the UTR receipt.
    • Send the form with attested documents by hand or by post so that it reaches the ATC, Mazgaon, by 18 December 2026, 5:00 PM.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply if you have already done an apprenticeship in the same trade — you are not eligible.
    • Do not send the form without the NATS registration number or the fee (UTR) receipt.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the form must REACH the ATC by 18 December 2026, 5:00 PM; postal delays are your risk.
    • Do not treat this as a permanent job — it is a 12-month apprenticeship with no employment obligation afterwards.
