    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Indian citizen.
    • Senior Resident: concerned specialty me MBBS + PG degree/DNB/Diploma (Dental ke liye MDS), Delhi Medical Council se registered. Non-PG MBBS jinhe recognised 100-bedded hospital me kam se kam 2 saal ka experience ho (specialty me 1 saal) bhi consider kiye ja sakte hain. Ek UR Paediatrics post PG aur Non-PG dono ke liye khuli hai.
    • Junior Resident: MBBS, Junior Residents ke Recruitment Rules ke anusaar.
    • Senior Resident maximum age 45 saal (application submit karne ke aakhiri din ko); relaxation SC/ST 5 saal, OBC 3 saal, PwD 10 saal. Junior Resident ki age hosted corrigendum me nahi di gayi hai.
    • Senior Resident ke liye ₹1,000 (General/OBC/EWS) aur ₹500 (SC/ST), non-refundable demand draft se — 'COMMISSIONER, MUNICIPAL CORPORATION OF DELHI' ke naam, Delhi me payable; Persons with Disability exempt hain.
    • Junior Resident ki fee hosted corrigendum me nahi di gayi hai (Annexure-I missing hai) — jaane se pehle notice check karen.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • 35 posts — Senior Resident 28 + Junior Resident 7 (UR 6, ST 1) — Mata Gujri Hospital (pehle Tilak Nagar Colony Hospital), Municipal Corporation of Delhi me. Advt. No. 1361/MS/MGH/2026 dated 10 November 2026; corrigendum Ref. No. 1389/MS/MGH/2026 dated 21 November 2026 se 7 Junior Resident posts jode gaye.
    • Senior Resident department-wise breakup (28): Anaesthesia 2, Obstetrics & Gynaecology 7, Paediatrics 7, Emergency Medicine 5, Medicine 4, Surgery 2, Radiology 1. Categories: UR 11, OBC 9, SC 3, ST 2, EWS 3 (PH 01). Ek UR Paediatrics post PG aur Non-PG dono candidates ke liye khuli hai.
    • Walk-in interview: Thursday 3 December 2026, 11:00 AM se 4:00 PM tak; reporting aur document verification usi din 9:30–11:00 AM. Venue: Mata Gujri Hospital, Administration Department, Block-11, Near Tilak Nagar Metro Station, Tilak Nagar, New Delhi – 110018. Reserved-category (OBC/SC/ST/EWS) PG Senior Resident candidates ke liye 3 December 2026 se pehle har Thursday ko bhi walk-in interviews hote hain, aur vacancies rehne par uske baad bhi har Thursday.
    • Qualification: Senior Resident — concerned specialty me MBBS + PG degree/DNB/Diploma (Dental ke liye MDS), Delhi Medical Council se registered; non-PG MBBS jinhe recognised 100-bedded hospital me kam se kam 2 saal ka experience ho (specialty me 1 saal) bhi consider kiye ja sakte hain. Junior Resident — MBBS, Junior Residents ke Recruitment Rules ke anusaar.
    • Age: Senior Resident maximum 45 saal (application submit karne ke aakhiri din ko); relaxation SC/ST 5 saal, OBC 3 saal, PwD 10 saal. Junior Resident ki age hosted corrigendum me NAHI di gayi hai — uska Annexure-I (JR Recruitment Rules, marking pattern aur terms) MCD site par hosted 2-page corrigendum PDF me missing hai; jaane se pehle notice check karen.
    • Fee: ₹1,000 General/OBC/EWS aur ₹500 SC/ST, non-refundable demand draft se — 'COMMISSIONER, MUNICIPAL CORPORATION OF DELHI' ke naam, Delhi me payable; Persons with Disability exempt hain. Ye Senior Resident ki fees hain — Junior Resident ki fee hosted corrigendum me nahi hai.
    • Pay aur tenure: Senior Resident — Pay Matrix Level-11 + usual allowances; shuru me 1 saal, PG candidates ke liye 1-1 saal karke maximum 3 saal tak extend ho sakta hai. Junior Resident — shuru me 6 mahine, 6 mahine aur badhkar maximum 1 saal. Junior Resident ki pay hosted corrigendum me nahi hai. Ye contractual posts hain, permanent MCD posts nahi.
    • Selection: walk-in interview + document verification (reporting par originals aur photocopies); marking PG Degree 20 / PG Diploma 17 / Non-PG 14 + Interview 5, kul 25. Applications sirf prescribed proforma me walk-in par — warna summarily reject ho jayengi. Koi online application nahi hai.
    • Upar ke saare facts Advt. No. 1361/MS/MGH/2026 aur corrigendum 1389/MS/MGH/2026 (mcdonline.nic.in) se hain.

    यह करो

    • Jaane se pehle Advt. No. 1361/MS/MGH/2026 aur corrigendum 1389/MS/MGH/2026 mcdonline.nic.in par poora padhen.
    • Thursday 3 December 2026 ko 9:30 AM tak Mata Gujri Hospital, Block-11, Tilak Nagar, New Delhi pahunchen — originals aur photocopies saath le jayen.
    • Exact fee ka demand draft saath le jayen (₹1,000 General/OBC/EWS, ₹500 SC/ST; PwD exempt) — 'COMMISSIONER, MUNICIPAL CORPORATION OF DELHI' ke naam.
    • Reserved-category (OBC/SC/ST/EWS) PG Senior Resident candidates 3 December 2026 se pehle wale har Thursday ke walk-ins ko bhi note karen.
    • Junior Resident ki details ke liye notice check karen — official corrigendum se sirf MBBS qualification, 7 posts aur 6 mahine/1 saal ki tenure confirm hai.

    यह मत करो

    • Inhe permanent MCD posts mat samjhen — Senior Resident contractual hai 1 saal ke liye (PG candidates ke liye maximum 3 saal tak extend ho sakta hai); Junior Resident 6 mahine, maximum 1 saal tak.
    • Prescribed proforma ke bina mat jayen — kisi aur format me application summarily reject ho jata hai.
    • 11:00 AM ke baad mat pahunchen — registration aur document verification 3 December 2026 ko 9:30–11:00 AM hai.
    • Junior Resident ki fee, pay ya age aggregator pages se mat maan len — hosted corrigendum me Annexure-I nahi hai, isliye wo details yahan officially confirm nahi hain.
