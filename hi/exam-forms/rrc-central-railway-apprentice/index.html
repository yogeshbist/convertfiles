    ध्यान दो: यह Apprentices Act, 1961 के तहत Act Apprentice training है — railway ki naukri nahi. Notice me saaf likha hai ki training se Railways me absorption ka koi adhikar nahi milta.

    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Aayu 3 November 2026 ko 15–24 saal, notice ke anusar varg chhoot ke saath.
    • Yogyata: kam se kam 50% kul ankon ke saath 10th pass, aur NCVT/SCVT manyata prapt sansthan se notified trade me ITI / National Trade Certificate. Engineering graduates aur diploma holders in slots ke liye patra NAHI hain.
    • Cluster ke hisaab se slots: Mumbai 1,712, Bhusawal 500, Pune 271, Nagpur 221, Solapur 157.
    • Chayan merit list se hoga — tumhare 10th percentage aur ITI trade ankon ka sadhaaran ausat, cluster/unit/trade ke hisaab se. Koi likhit pariksha nahi hai. Barabari par zyada umr wale ko, phir pehle 10th pass karne wale ko prathmikta milti hai.
    • Training 1 saal ki hai aur stipend ₹9,600 prati mahina hai. Hostel ki suvidha nahi di jayegi.
    • General, OBC aur EWS ummeedwaron ke liye ₹100.
    • SC, ST, PwBD aur mahila ummeedwaron ke liye koi fee nahi.
    • Fee online deni hai aur wapas nahi hoti.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Photograph: nayi colour photo, 3.5 cm × 3.5 cm, JPG/JPEG 100 DPI par, 20–70 KB, pichhle 3 mahine ke andar ki, saaf front view — topi ya kala chashma nahi.
    • Signature: 3.5 cm × 3.5 cm, JPG/JPEG 100 DPI par, 20–30 KB.
    • Sirf ummeedwar ki apni nayi photo upload karo; purani ya dhundli photo document verification par form reject karwa sakti hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Pehle dekho ki tumhara trade chune hue cluster me notified hai ya nahi — slots trade aur unit ke hisaab se hain.
    • Photo ko sakhti se 20–70 KB aur signature ko 20–30 KB ke andar rakho.
    • Form bharne se pehle 10th percentage notice ke tareeke se sahi-sahi nikalo.
    • 2 December 2026 se pehle fee online do aur raseed sambhalo.
    • ITI ki har semester/saal ki marksheet ready rakho — merit me poore ITI ank judte hain.

    यह मत करो

    • Engineering degree ya diploma hai to apply mat karo — notice me in slots ke liye ve ayogya hain.
    • Photograph me topi ya kala chashma mat pehno.
    • Kisi unofficial website se form mat bharo — sirf rrccr.com se apply karo.
    • Correction window ki ummeed mat karo; notice me koi correction window nahi hai.
    • Training ko railway naukri ka vaada mat samjho.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • Passport photo (3.5 × 3.5 cm, 20–70 KB) aur signature (20–30 KB) ready
    • 10th marksheet aur certificate scan kiye hue
    • ITI marksheets aur National Trade Certificate scan kiye hue
    • Jaati / PwBD certificate scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho
    • Aadhaar card ready
    • ₹100 fee ke liye debit card / UPI ready, yadi lagu ho

    दस्तावेज़ जो लगेंगे

    • 10th ki marksheet aur certificate (kam se kam 50% kul ank)
    • Notified trade me ITI marksheets aur National Trade Certificate
    • Jaati praman patra (SC/ST/OBC/EWS), yadi lagu ho
    • PwBD praman patra, yadi lagu ho
    • Aadhaar card
    • Notice ke format me medical fitness certificate — document verification ke samay lagega

    फॉर्म भरते समय आम गलतियाँ

    • Chune hue cluster ya unit me notified na hue trade ke liye apply karna.
    • 10th percentage galat nikalna — merit list 10th + ITI ke ausat par banti hai, ek galat ank rank gira deta hai.
    • 3 mahine se purani photo ya topi/chashme wali photo upload karna.
    • Ye na dekhna ki engineering graduates aur diploma holders Act Apprentice slots ke liye patra nahi hain.

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. rrccr.com kholo aur Notification RRC/CR/AA/2026 poora padho.
    2. Apne mobile number aur email ID se register karo.
    3. Form bharo aur apna cluster, unit aur trade dhyaan se chuno.
    4. Photo (20–70 KB) aur signature (20–30 KB) JPG format me upload karo.
    5. Lagu ho to ₹100 fee online do (SC/ST/PwBD/Mahilaon ke liye free hai).
    6. 2 December 2026, shaam 5:00 baje se pehle submit karo aur application print sambhalo.
