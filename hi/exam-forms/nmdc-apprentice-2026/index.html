    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Bharat ke nagrik hon.
    • Trade Apprentice: 10th pass (10+2 ke tahat) ya equivalent + sambandhit trade me ITI Trade Certificate.
    • Graduate Apprentice: sambandhit discipline me full-time Bachelor's Degree.
    • Technician Apprentice: sambandhit discipline me full-time Diploma.
    • Pass karne aur contract banne ke beech ka gap 5 saal se zyada nahi hona chahiye; pehle apprenticeship training ya 1 saal se zyada kaam nahi hona chahiye.
    • Age: notification ki date (24 November 2026) ko kam se kam 18 saal. Upper age limit notification me nahi di gayi hai.
    • Notification me koi application fee nahi di gayi hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • 247 seats — Trade Apprentices 172, Graduate Apprentices 55, Technician Apprentices 20. Advt. No. KC/T&S/2026, dated 24 November 2026.
    • Walk-in interviews (trade-wise): Machinist 4 + Fitter 16 — 14/12/2026; Welder 27 — 15/12/2026; Mechanic (Diesel) 32 + Mechanic (Motor Vehicle) 12 — 16/12/2026; Electrician 34 — 17/12/2026; COPA 47 — 18/12/2026; Chemistry 2, Computer Science 3, B.Pharm 1 — 19/12/2026; BBA 2, Electrical & Electronics 3, Electrical 8 — 21/12/2026; Mechanical 14, Mining 14, Civil 8 (Graduate) + Civil 3, Mechanical 6, Mining 5 (Technician) — 22/12/2026; Electrical 6 (Technician) — 21/12/2026.
    • Venue: Training Institute, B.I.O.M., Kirandul Complex, Kirandul, Dist. Dantewada (Chhattisgarh) – 494556, subah 9:00–shaam 5:30. Interview help desk par 1:00 PM se pehle register karna hoga — 1:00 PM ke baad report karne walon ko entry nahi milti.
    • Pehle NAPS (apprenticeshipindia.gov.in) par Trade Apprentices ke liye ya NATS (nats.education.gov.in) par Graduate/Technician Apprentices ke liye apna profile register/approve karwayen, phir interview wale din khud jakar Help Desk par diya gaya online Google Form bharen.
    • Qualification: Trade — 10th pass (10+2 ke tahat) ya equivalent + sambandhit trade me ITI Trade Certificate; Graduate — sambandhit discipline me full-time Bachelor's Degree; Technician — sambandhit discipline me full-time Diploma.
    • Pass karne aur contract banne ke beech ka gap 5 saal se zyada nahi hona chahiye; pehle apprenticeship training ya 1 saal se zyada kaam nahi hona chahiye. Age: 24/11/2026 ko kam se kam 18 saal; upper age limit notification me nahi di gayi hai.
    • Stipend: koi rupee amount nahi di gayi hai — notice me sirf itna likha hai ki stipend Apprenticeship Rules, 1992 (samay-samay par amended) ke hisaab se milega. Training period 1 saal.
    • Selection: interview performance + satisfactory Document Verification. Fee: koi fee nahi di gayi hai. Bio-data form ke saath ek passport-size photograph, NAPS/NATS profile ka proof aur original certificates le jayen.
    • Upar ke saare facts Advt. No. KC/T&S/2026 (nmdc.co.in) se liye gaye hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Walk-in se pehle NAPS (Trade Apprentices) ya NATS (Graduate/Technician Apprentices) par apna profile register aur approve karwayen.
    • Apne trade ki walk-in date par 1:00 PM se pehle Training Institute, B.I.O.M., Kirandul Complex, Kirandul, Dist. Dantewada (Chhattisgarh) pahunchen.
    • Bio-data form ke saath ek passport-size photograph, NAPS/NATS profile ka proof aur saare original certificates le jayen.
    • Walk-in se pehle nmdc.co.in par Advt. No. KC/T&S/2026 poori padhen.

    यह मत करो

    • Interview help desk par 1:00 PM ke baad report mat karen — 1:00 PM ke baad aane wale candidates ko entry nahi milti.
    • Ise permanent job mat samjhen — ye 1 saal ki apprenticeship hai.
    • Agar aap pehle apprenticeship training kar chuke hain ya 1 saal se zyada kaam kar chuke hain to apply mat karen — aap eligible nahi hain.
    • Apne trade ki walk-in date miss mat karen — har trade ki date 14 se 22 December 2026 ke beech alag hai.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • ITI Trade Certificate / Bachelor's Degree / Diploma certificate aur mark sheets (post ke hisaab se)
    • NAPS (Trade) ya NATS (Graduate/Technician) par registered aur approved profile
    • Bio-data form ke liye ek passport-size photograph
    • Walk-in par Document Verification ke liye original certificates
    • Aadhaar aur dusra identity proof

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. nmdc.co.in par Advt. No. KC/T&S/2026 padhen aur apne trade ki walk-in date check karen (14-22 December 2026).
    2. NAPS (Trade) ya NATS (Graduate/Technician) par apna profile register aur approve karwayen.
    3. Apne trade ki date par 1:00 PM se pehle Training Institute, B.I.O.M., Kirandul pahunchen.
    4. Help Desk par Google Form bharen aur bio-data form + 1 passport-size photo + NAPS/NATS proof + original certificates dikhayen.
    5. Interview den aur Document Verification karwayen.
