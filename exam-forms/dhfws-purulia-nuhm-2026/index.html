    Eligibility & fees

    • Staff Nurse (NUHM): completed GNM course (Indian Nursing Council-recognised institute) OR completed B.Sc. Nursing course; registered under the West Bengal Nursing Council; proficiency in the local language.
    • Pharmacist (NUHM): 2-year Diploma in Pharmacy (D-Pharma, Allopathic) recognised by the Government of West Bengal; registered as 'A' category Pharmacist under the West Bengal Pharmacy Council; efficiency in computers including MS Office and internet.
    • Pharmacist (NUHM): permanent resident of West Bengal.
    • Age maximum 40 years as on 1 April 2026 (Pharmacist: minimum 18 years; relaxation as per West Bengal Government rules).
    • Read Memo No. CMOH/Samiti/1045 fully on wbhealth.gov.in before applying.
    • No application fee is stated in the notification.
    • No TA/DA is paid.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 3 posts under the National Health Mission, District Health & Family Welfare Samity, Purulia — Staff Nurse (NUHM) 2 (ST 1, EWS 1), Pharmacist (NUHM) 1 (UR 1). Consolidated remuneration: Staff Nurse ₹25,000/month, Pharmacist ₹22,000/month.
    • Both posts are purely contractual and temporary under the National Health Mission — selected candidates have no claim to permanent appointment. See the note above.
    • Online applications 19 November 2026 (10:00 AM)–3 December 2026, only through wbhealth.gov.in (Quick links → Career, Education & Recruitment → Online Recruitment). Last date of online registration: 2 December 2026. One application per post — more than one application for the same post cancels all of them.
    • Age: Staff Nurse (NUHM) — maximum 40 years as on 1 April 2026. Pharmacist (NUHM) — minimum 18, maximum 40 years as on 1 April 2026. Age relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of West Bengal rules.
    • Qualification: Staff Nurse (NUHM) — completed GNM course from an Indian Nursing Council-recognised institute OR completed B.Sc. Nursing course; registered under the West Bengal Nursing Council; proficiency in the local language. Desirable: experience working in a hospital setting. Pharmacist (NUHM) — permanent resident of West Bengal; 2-year Diploma in Pharmacy (D-Pharma, Allopathic) recognised by the Government of West Bengal, registered as 'A' category Pharmacist under the West Bengal Pharmacy Council; efficiency in computers including MS Office and internet. Weightage for higher qualification (B-Pharma/M-Pharma).
    • Selection: Staff Nurse (NUHM) — as per marks obtained in the GNM examination (the notification's selection cell mentions GNM marks only, although B.Sc. Nursing is also listed as eligible). Pharmacist (NUHM) — total 100 marks: proportionate marking (D-Pharma 65, B-Pharma 75, M-Pharma 85; max marks 85) + Computer Test 15 marks (minimum 50% qualifying).
    • No physical documents need to be sent to the office. At document verification, carry originals of all testimonials and documents supporting every claim, and submit one set of self-attested copies. Experience certificates must state the duration, post, nature of work and the employer's signature with date, with salary slips for the last three months for each claimed period. DGPA/CGPA conversion formula must be attached.
    • Posting may be anywhere under the District Health & Family Welfare Samity, Purulia. The Samity reserves the right to cancel the recruitment process at any stage without assigning any reason. Prescribed conditions are non-relaxable. No TA/DA is paid.
    • All facts above are taken from DH&FWS Purulia Memo No. CMOH/Samiti/1045, dated 18 November 2026 (wbhealth.gov.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Memo No. CMOH/Samiti/1045 fully on wbhealth.gov.in before applying.
    • Apply online on hr.wbhealth.gov.in between 19 November 2026 (10:00 AM) and 3 December 2026 — complete the registration by 2 December 2026.
    • Submit only one application per post, even if you apply for both posts.
    • Keep your GNM/B.Sc. Nursing or D-Pharma certificate, Council registration certificate, Madhyamik admit card (age proof) and a recent photo ready.
    • Carry originals of all testimonials to document verification, with one set of self-attested copies.

    Avoid this

    • Do not submit more than one application for the same post — all of them will be cancelled.
    • Do not send any physical documents to the office — documents are checked only at verification.
    • Do not hide or misstate any information — omission or suppression results in rejection at any stage.
    • Do not treat these as permanent government jobs — both posts are purely contractual and temporary.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Computer-generated application form, duly signed, with a recent passport-size photo pasted on it
    • Madhyamik (or equivalent) Admit Card / Certificate as proof of age
    • All relevant mark sheets and pass certificates (Class 10, Class 12, GNM / B.Sc. Nursing / D-Pharma)
    • Council registration certificate (Nursing / Pharmacy, as per post)
    • Valid EWS certificate, if applicable
    • Voter ID / Aadhaar Card / any other valid address proof
    • Experience certificates with salary slips for the last three months, if claiming experience
    • Caste certificate issued by the competent authority of the Government of West Bengal, if applicable
    • DGPA/CGPA conversion formula, if applicable

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Ek hi post ke liye ek se zyada application bharna (sab cancel ho jayenge)
    • Registration ki last date 2 December miss karna (apply 3 December tak hai, registration 2 December tak)
    • Document verification par originals na le jana (cancellation ho sakti hai)
    • Inhe permanent naukri samajh lena (ye purely contractual NHM posts hain)

    How to apply — step by step

    1. wbhealth.gov.in par Memo No. CMOH/Samiti/1045 पढ़ें।
    2. hr.wbhealth.gov.in par 19 नवंबर 2026 (सुबह 10 बजे) से 3 दिसंबर 2026 तक online apply करें — registration 2 दिसंबर 2026 तक पूरा करें।
    3. Sahi post (Staff Nurse NUHM ya Pharmacist NUHM) चुनकर form भरें — ek post ke liye sirf ek application।
    4. Photo, GNM/B.Sc. Nursing ya D-Pharma certificate, Council registration certificate aur age proof upload करें।
    5. Form submit करें aur computer-generated application form ka print save करें — verification ke time original documents ke saath le jana hai।
