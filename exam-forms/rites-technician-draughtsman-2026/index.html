    Eligibility & fees

    • Matriculation + 2-year ITI Draughtsman Civil course (RG/51/26) or ITI Draughtsman Architecture/Civil course (RG/52/26); full-time Diploma in Civil / Architecture/Civil also eligible.
    • Minimum 3 years post-qualification experience, of which minimum 2 years in the named drawings (bridge/railway alignment drawings for RG/51/26; railway station architectural drawings for RG/52/26); proficient in AutoCAD.
    • Age 33 years as on 15 December 2026 (relaxation as per rules).
    • Minimum 50% marks in the essential qualification for UR/EWS; 45% for reserved-category candidates against reserved posts.
    • Only Indian nationals aged 18 years and above. Read the combined notice for Vc. Nos. RG/50/26 to RG/52/26 fully on rites.com before applying.
    • General/OBC ₹300 + taxes as applicable; EWS/SC/ST/PwD ₹100 + taxes as applicable, online.
    • Payment issues: 011-33557000, Ext. 13221, or pg.service@hdfc.bank.in.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 5 Technician-I (Draughtsman) posts on regular basis: RG/51/26 — Technician-I (Draughtsman / Bridges & Railway alignment), Civil, 4 posts (UR 2, EWS 1, ST 1); RG/52/26 — Technician-I (Draughtsman / Stations), Architecture, 1 post (UR 1). Pay ₹22,000–83,000, fixed at the minimum of the scale. Probation 1 year; liable to be posted anywhere in India or abroad.
    • Online applications 16 November–15 December 2026 on recruit.rites.com. Age, experience and all other eligibility criteria are reckoned as on 15 December 2026. One application for one vacancy — apply separately, with a separate fee, for each post code. Hard copies are NOT to be sent by post/courier.
    • Age: 33 years for both RG/51/26 and RG/52/26. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) 3 years, J&K Domicile 5 years, PwBD 10 years (in addition to category relaxation), RITES regular/contract employees 5 years. Identified PwBD categories: Locomotor disability, Hearing Impairment.
    • Qualification and experience: RG/51/26 — Matriculation plus 2-year ITI Draughtsman Civil course; minimum 3 years post-qualification experience, of which minimum 2 years in preparation of Bridge Drawings and railway alignment drawings and related structures; proficient in AutoCAD. RG/52/26 — Matriculation plus 2-year ITI Draughtsman Architecture/Civil course; minimum 3 years post-qualification experience, of which minimum 2 years in preparation of architectural drawings for railway stations, station buildings/depots; proficient in AutoCAD and conversant with general arrangement drawings (GADs), floor plans, elevations, sections, architectural detailing and quantity-related drawings.
    • Full-time Diploma in Civil (for RG/51/26) or in Architecture/Civil (for RG/52/26) holders are also eligible at the same pay scale, but other higher qualifications are NOT considered for eligibility. Minimum marks in the essential qualification: 50% for UR/EWS (and reserved-category candidates applying against unreserved posts); 45% for reserved-category candidates against reserved posts.
    • Selection: (1) Written Test — 125 objective questions, 1 mark each, 2.5 hours, NO negative marking; PwBD candidates get 50 minutes compensatory time; minimum 50% marks for UR/EWS (45% for SC/ST/OBC(NCL)/PwBD against reserved posts) to be considered. (2) Document Scrutiny — qualifying candidates only, initially in a ratio of 1:3 of the vacancies; offers in order of merit only to the extent of vacancies. Test centres (two preferences): Delhi/Gurgaon, Kolkata, Bangalore, Mumbai, Guwahati, Bhubaneswar — all except Delhi/Gurgaon are tentative.
    • RG/50/26 Technical Assistant (Draughtsman / Bridges & Railway alignment), Civil, 3 posts, is a DIFFERENT post advertised in the same combined notice — it is not included in these 5 Technician-I posts.
    • Allowances include Basic + DA, Performance Related Pay, Medical, HRA, superannuation benefits (PF, Gratuity, Post Retirement Medical Scheme), leaves, maternity/paternity leave, medical and group insurance, leave encashment. OBC-NCL certificate must not be older than 1 year on the crucial date (15 December 2026) and must carry the Annexure III declaration; EWS certificate must be valid for FY 2026-27. Queries only to rectt@rites.com with VC No., Registration/Roll No., full name and the email given in the application.
    • All facts above are taken from the RITES combined notice for Vc. Nos. RG/50/26 to RG/52/26, hosted 16 November 2026 (rites.com).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the combined notice for Vc. Nos. RG/50/26 to RG/52/26 fully on rites.com before applying.
    • Choose the right vacancy code — RG/51/26 (Civil) or RG/52/26 (Architecture) — and apply separately for each, with a separate fee.
    • Fill the online form on recruit.rites.com, note the Registration No. generated on top of the form, then upload documents and pay the fee online.
    • Keep your ITI certificate, mark sheets, experience proof (offer letter, joining order, relieving letter, service certificate, pay slips in one chronological PDF), PAN and Aadhaar ready.
    • Download the final application form after successful payment and keep a copy to carry at selection, if called.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for RG/51/26 or RG/52/26 with a higher qualification other than the full-time Diploma — other higher qualifications are NOT considered for eligibility.
    • Do not send hard copies by post/courier — they are not accepted.
    • Do not apply with an OBC-NCL certificate older than 1 year on 15 December 2026, or an EWS certificate not valid for FY 2026-27.
    • Do not wait for the last moment — RITES is not responsible for non-submission due to issues reported at the last moment.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • High School certificate (proof of date of birth)
    • ITI Draughtsman (Civil / Architecture) certificate and mark sheets + percentage conversion document, if applicable
    • Full-time Diploma certificate and mark sheets, if claiming Diploma eligibility
    • Experience proof in a single chronological PDF (offer letter, joining order, relieving letter, service certificate, pay slips)
    • EWS / SC / ST / OBC-NCL / PwBD certificate in the prescribed Government of India format, if applicable
    • Proof of identity and address (Passport, Voter ID, Driving Licence, Aadhaar)
    • PAN Card
    • Recent passport-size colour photograph
    • Registration No. (generated on the form) and fee payment receipt

    How to apply — step by step

    1. rites.com (Career section) par Vc. Nos. RG/50/26 to RG/52/26 ka combined notice पढ़ें।
    2. recruit.rites.com par 'Online Registration' par click करें — frmRegistration.aspx khulega।
    3. Sahi vacancy code (RG/51/26 Civil ya RG/52/26 Architecture) चुनकर online form भरें — Registration No. note करें।
    4. 'Fill/Modify Application Form' ke baad 'Upload Document' me saare documents upload करें।
    5. 'Make Payment' me fee online भरें (General/OBC ₹300 + taxes; EWS/SC/ST/PwD ₹100 + taxes) — 15 दिसंबर 2026 तक।
    6. Payment ke baad final application form download करें aur copy save करें — selection ke time le jana hai।
