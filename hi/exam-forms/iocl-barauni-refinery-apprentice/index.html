    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Pad: kul 272 — vigyapan me kul sankhya chhapi nahi hai; 272 chaar code-wise totals ka jod hai: Trade Apprentice Attendant Operator (Chemical Plant) 190, Trade Apprentice (Boiler) 38, Technician Apprentice (Mechanical) 21, Technician Apprentice (Instrumentation) 23. Shreni-war kul: UR 126, EWS 27, SC 43, ST 3, OBC (NCL) 73; PwBD 10 (horizontal, sirf do Technician codes ke liye). Seats tentative hain.
    • Aayu: 30 November 2026 ko 18–24 saal (Gen/EWS). Chhoot: SC/ST 5 saal, OBC (NCL) 3 saal, PwBD 10 saal (SC/ST-PwBD ko 5 aur, OBC-PwBD ko 3 aur). Aayu ka praman: sirf Class 10 ki marksheet/certificate.
    • Shiksha: Code 101 aur 103 — 3-saal ki B.Sc (Maths, Physics, Chemistry ya Industrial Chemistry); B.Sc ummeedwar 101/103 me se sirf EK ke liye apply kar sakta hai. Code 105 — Mechanical Engineering me 3-saal ka Diploma. Code 107 — Instrumentation Engineering / Instrumentation & Electronics / Instrumentation & Control / Applied Electronics & Instrumentation me 3-saal ka Diploma. Kam se kam 50% aggregate (aarakshit seats par SC/ST/PwBD ke liye 45%), regular full-time, 30 November 2026 tak pass hona zaroori. Lateral entry (XII-Sc/ITI se 2nd year me) patra hai.
    • Patra nahi hain: part-time/correspondence/distance yogyata wale; BE/B.Tech, MBA, CA, LLB ya MCA wale; yogyata ke baad 1 saal ya zyada job anubhav wale; yogyata 5 saal se purani hone wale; ek se zyada trade ke liye apply karne wale.
    • Chayan: koi likhit pariksha nahi. Nirdharit yogyata ke ankon ke pratishat par merit, phir document verification aur pre-engagement medical fitness check. Tentative DV list: 11 December 2026; DV: 14–18 December 2026.
    • Stipend Apprentices Act, 1961 aur Apprentices Rules ke anusar — vigyapan me rupaye ka ank nahi chhapa hai. Chhutti: saal me 32 din General + 12 din Casual; SC/ST/PwBD ummeedwaron ko DV ke liye 2nd-class rail fare wapas milta hai (30 km ya zyada).
    • Vigyapan me koi application fee nahi di gayi hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Vigyapan me photo/signature ka koi numeric size nahi chhapa hai — application NAPS/NATS portal aur Microsoft Form se hoti hai, isliye photo aur signature wahan mange format aur size me upload karo.
    • Ek haal ki colour photo aur saaf signature scan pehle se ready rakho.
    • Sirf saaf, padhne layak scan upload karo; dhundhli photo ya signature se form reject ho sakta hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Sirf EK trade code ke liye apply karo — B.Sc ummeedwar ko code 101 aur 103 me se ek chunna hai.
    • DONO steps poore karo — NAPS (code 101 aur 103) ya NATS (code 105 aur 107) enrolment, AUR iocl.com par mandatory Microsoft Form 'Apprenticeship Additional Data Form: Barauni Refinery 2026'.
    • Apply karne se pehle 50% (SC/ST/PwBD ke liye 45%) ankon ki shart aur 30 November 2026 ki cut-off check karo.
    • Apna NATS/NAPS Establishment ID ready rakho (NATS EBRBEC000003, NAPS E11191000001).
    • Tentative tithiyan note karo: DV list 11 December 2026, DV 14–18 December 2026.

    यह मत करो

    • Agar tumhare paas BE/B.Tech, MBA, CA, LLB ya MCA degree hai to apply mat karo — tum patra nahi ho.
    • Ek se zyada trade ke liye apply mat karo.
    • Microsoft Form mat chhodo — sirf NAPS/NATS step se application poori nahi hoti.
    • CBT par bharosa mat karo — kuch job sites is bharti ke liye galat tareeke se likhit pariksha likhti hain; vigyapan me koi likhit pariksha nahi hai.
    • Part-time, correspondence ya distance yogyata ke saath apply mat karo.

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. IOCL Barauni Refinery ke liye NAPS portal (code 101 aur 103) ya NATS portal (code 105 aur 107) par register karo.
    2. iocl.com par jao (What's New → Engagement of Apprentices at Barauni Refinery 2026-27 (II)) aur mandatory Microsoft Form 'Apprenticeship Additional Data Form: Barauni Refinery 2026' kholo.
    3. Microsoft Form me wahi jaankari bharo jo tumne NAPS/NATS application me bhari hai.
    4. Dono steps 6 December 2026 se pehle poore karo.
    5. Tentative DV list 11 December 2026 ko dekho — DV 14–18 December 2026 me hoga. Sawal ke liye: brrecttcell@indianoil.in, 06243-275258 / 275240.
