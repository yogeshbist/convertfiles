    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Pad: J&K UT ke liye — Banking Associate 1,033 aur Probationary Officer 323; Ladakh UT ke liye — Banking Associate 21 aur Probationary Officer 20. Har UT aur har post ki alag notification hai — apne UT ki notification par hi apply karo.
    • Sambandhit kendra shasit pradesh (J&K ya Ladakh) ka domicile anivarya hai. Chayan ke baad allotted zone me kam se kam 5 saal ki seva lagu hoti hai.
    • Shiksha: kisi manyata prapt vishvavidyalaya se kisi bhi vishay me graduation.
    • Aayu 1 January 2026 ko 20–30 saal. Chhoot: SC/ST +5 saal, SEBC J&K me +3 saal (Ladakh me +5 saal), PwBD +10 saal.
    • Chayan IBPS ke madhyam se hota hai. Banking Associate: ek hi online pariksha — 100 prashn / 100 ank, 60 minute, har galat jawab par −0.25. Probationary Officer: Prelims, phir Mains (170 prashn / 200 ank + 25 ank ka descriptive paper), phir 100 ank ka Interview — antim merit Mains 80 : Interview 20 ke anupaat se banti hai.
    • Vetan: PO ki shuruaat basic ₹48,480 se (JMGS-I scale ₹48,480–85,920); Banking Associate ki shuruaat basic ₹24,050 se (scale ₹24,050–64,480). Probation: PO 2 saal (bond ₹2 lakh), Banking Associate 6 mahine (bond ₹1 lakh).
    • Probationary Officer: ₹1,200 (SC/ST/SEBC/PwBD: ₹1,000), GST alag se.
    • Banking Associate: ₹800 (SC/ST/SEBC/PwBD/ESM: ₹600), GST alag se.
    • Fee application ke dauran online deni hai aur wapas nahi hoti.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Photograph: nayi colour photo, 4.5 cm × 3.5 cm, 200 × 230 pixels, JPG, 20–50 KB.
    • Live photograph: application ke dauran live photo bhi li jati hai — form khud bharo aur achhi roshni me bharo, taaki live capture upload photo se match kare.
    • Signature: safed kagaz par kali syahi se sign karo — CAPITAL aksharon me NAHI — aur 140 × 60 pixels, JPG, 10–20 KB me scan karo.
    • Bayein angoothe ka nishan: safed kagaz par, 240 × 240 pixels 200 DPI par scan, JPG, 20–50 KB.
    • Hand-written declaration: declaration ka text apni handwriting me English me likho (capital me nahi), 800 × 400 pixels 200 DPI par scan karo, JPG, 50–100 KB.
    • Baaki documents: har ek 500 KB tak ki PDF files; documents kam se kam 200 DPI par scan karo.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Apne UT ki notification par apply karo — J&K aur Ladakh ki notifications aur post counts alag hain.
    • Paanchon uploads exact size me ready rakho: photo, live photo, signature, angoothe ka nishan aur hand-written declaration.
    • Declaration apne haath se likho — typed declaration reject hoti hai.
    • Apply se pehle domicile certificate check karo; UT domicile ke bina form valid nahi hai.
    • Form achhi roshni me bharo taaki live photograph saaf aaye.

    यह मत करो

    • CAPITAL aksharon me sign mat karo — aise signature reject hote hain.
    • J&K ya Ladakh UT ke domicile na ho to apply mat karo.
    • Scan ki hui photo ki jagah kisi aur ki photo ya selfie upload mat karo.
    • Ye mat bhoolo ki PO aur Banking Associate ki fee aur pariksha alag-alag hain.
    • Form 26 November ki shaam tak mat chhodo — fee payment application ke saath hi band hota hai.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • Photo (200 × 230 px, 20–50 KB) ready, aur live photo ke liye achhi roshni
    • Signature scan (140 × 60 px, 10–20 KB) — kali syahi, capital nahi
    • Bayein angoothe ka nishan (240 × 240 px, 20–50 KB) ready
    • Hand-written declaration (800 × 400 px, 50–100 KB) likhkar scan ki hui
    • Graduation degree aur marksheets scan ki hui (PDF, har ek 500 KB tak)
    • UT domicile certificate scan kiya hua
    • Jaati / SEBC / PwBD certificate scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho

    दस्तावेज़ जो लगेंगे

    • Graduation degree aur marksheets
    • Lagu hisaab se J&K ya Ladakh UT ka domicile certificate
    • Varg praman patra (SC/ST/SEBC/PwBD/ESM), yadi lagu ho
    • Janm tithi ke liye 10th certificate
    • Photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / Voter ID)

    फॉर्म भरते समय आम गलतियाँ

    • Dusre UT ki notification par apply karna — J&K ke 1,356 pad aur Ladakh ke 41 pad alag notifications hain.
    • Capital aksharon me signature, ya hand-written ki jagah typed declaration.
    • Live photograph bhool jana — andhere kamre me ya kisi aur ke dwara form bharne par mismatch hota hai.
    • UT ke domicile certificate ke bina apply karke verification par reject hona.

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. jkb.bank.in kholo, Careers me jao aur apne UT ke liye PO ya Banking Associate notification kholo.
    2. Apni email aur mobile number se register karo.
    3. Form bharo, photo upload karo aur achhi roshni me live photograph capture karo.
    4. Signature, bayein angoothe ka nishan aur hand-written declaration exact pixel aur KB size me upload karo.
    5. Fee online do (PO ₹1,200 / BA ₹800, aarakshit vargon ke liye kam) aur 26 November 2026 se pehle submit karo.
    6. Registration number aur application print sambhal kar rakho.

    आम सवाल

    J&K Bank PO & Banking Associate के लिए आवेदन की आखिरी तारीख क्या है?
    Online फॉर्म 26 नवंबर 2026 को बंद होगा। शुल्क 26 नवंबर 2026 तक भरा जा सकता है।
    क्या मुझे फोटो upload करनी होगी?
    नहीं। इस फॉर्म में फोटो आवेदन के समय camera से live ली जाती है। Plain, हल्की background के सामने बैठो, रोशनी चेहरे पर रखो और पूरा चेहरा साफ दिखे — कोई फोटो फाइल तैयार नहीं करनी।
    Main J&K aur Ladakh se bahar rehta hoon — kya main apply kar sakta hoon?
    Nahi. In notifications me sambandhit kendra shasit pradesh ka domicile anivarya hai — J&K ke pad J&K domiciles ke liye aur Ladakh ke pad Ladakh domiciles ke liye hain.
    J&K Bank ki pariksha kaun karata hai?
    Bank ki notifications ke anusar chayan prakriya IBPS ke madhyam se hoti hai — Banking Associate ke liye ek hi online pariksha, aur Probationary Officer ke liye Prelims + Mains + Interview.
