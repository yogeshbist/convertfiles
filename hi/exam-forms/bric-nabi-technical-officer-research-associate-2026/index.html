    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Bharat ke nagrik hon.
    • Technical Officer-I (ICT): recognised University/Institute se Information Technology / Computer Science / MCA me Master's Degree + 1 saal ka relevant experience, YA Information Technology / Computer Science Engineering me Bachelor's Degree + networking/communication field me 3 saal ka experience. Desirable: Database Development & Management / Networking / IT Management me expertise. Age limit 35 saal.
    • Research Associate-I (Plant): Science/Engineering/Technology me PhD, YA Engineering ya Technology me Master's Degree + R&D me 3 saal ka experience + SCI journal me kam se kam ek research paper. Desirable: Biotechnology / Bioprocessing / Biochemical Engineering / Chemical Engineering / Biochemistry / Chemistry ya relevant area me specialisation. Age limit 40 saal.
    • Age me relaxation Govt. of India norms ke hisaab se milegi (advertisement me koi figures nahi di gayi hain).
    • Experience wahi mana jayega jo minimum educational qualifications hasil karne ke baad hasil ki gayi ho.
    • Official advertisement me koi application fee nahi di gayi hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • 2 posts — Technical Officer-I (ICT) 1 aur Research Associate-I (Plant) 1. Advt. NABI/Project Positions/2026(5). Project: BRIC-NABI Biomanufacturing Center 'Agri-Food Bio-Mug', 27 March 2027 tak.
    • Walk-in interview: 08 December 2026 subah 09:00 baje, BRIC – National Agri-Food and Biomanufacturing Institute, Knowledge City, Sector-81, Mohali, Punjab-140306 par. Registration usi din 09:00–10:00 baje. Interview ke liye koi TA/DA nahi milega.
    • Online interview (sirf exceptional circumstances me): email se request techasstnabi@gmail.com par bhare hue application form ke saath, 02 December 2026 tak. Final decision selection committee ka hoga; 02 December 2026 ke baad ke requests par vichar nahi hoga.
    • Pay: Technical Officer-I (ICT) ₹60,000 per month (1st year) / ₹66,000 per month (2nd year), consolidated. Research Associate-I (Plant) ₹58,000 per month + 18% HRA (1st year) / ₹61,000 per month + 18% HRA (2nd year).
    • Age limits: Technical Officer-I 35 saal; Research Associate-I 40 saal. Relaxation Govt. of India norms ke hisaab se (koi figures nahi di gayi hain).
    • Qualification: Technical Officer-I (ICT) — IT/CS/MCA me Master's + 1 saal relevant experience, YA IT/CS Engineering me B.E./B.Tech + networking/communication me 3 saal ka experience. Research Associate-I (Plant) — Science/Engineering/Technology me PhD, YA Engineering/Technology me Master's + R&D me 3 saal ka experience + SCI journal me kam se kam ek research paper. Research Associate-I candidates ko advertisement me di gayi 9 research areas ki apni preference order likhni hogi.
    • Ye posts purely temporary hain aur project poora hote hi khatam ho jayengi; candidates ka regular ya permanent employment par koi claim nahi hoga.
    • Fee: koi application fee nahi di gayi hai. Photo/signature specifications nahi di gayi hain — prescribed application form nabi.res.in se download karke registration ke samay in person submit karna hota hai.
    • Walk-in par duly filled application form (prescribed format), experience certificates, original degree certificates/transcripts/CV aur attested copies ka ek set le jayen.
    • Upar ke saare facts official advertisement (nabi.res.in, Advt. NABI/Project Positions/2026(5)) se liye gaye hain.
