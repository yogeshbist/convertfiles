    Eligibility & fees

    • 130 tentative vacancies for Junior Court Attendant (Group 'C', Non-Clerical), Level 3 of the Pay Matrix — ₹21,700–69,100 plus allowances. The number may change as per the Registry's requirements.
    • Education: minimum Class X (10th) pass and maximum Class XII (10+2) from a board or institute recognised by the Government. Candidates with education above Class 12 are not eligible.
    • Age: 18–27 years as on 1 October 2026. Relaxation as per Government of India rules for SC, ST, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), physically challenged and ex-servicemen; up to 5 years extra for dependants of freedom fighters; widows, divorced women and women judicially separated (not remarried) up to 35 years (38 for OBC, 40 for SC/ST). No upper age limit for departmental candidates of the Supreme Court Registry.
    • Selection: an objective-type test (100 questions, 100 marks, 1½ hours — 25 each on General Intelligence & Reasoning, Numerical Aptitude, General English and General Awareness; −0.25 per wrong answer; minimum qualifying marks 50), then an interview (25 marks, minimum 13). If many candidates qualify, interviews are held in a 1:3 ratio by merit.
    • Exam: the objective-type test is tentatively scheduled for 26 December 2026. Answer keys will be uploaded on 28 December 2026; objections can be filed on 28–29 December 2026 at ₹100 per objection (refunded if the objection is valid).
    • Application fee: ₹1,500 for General/Unreserved candidates, plus bank charges, paid online through the UCO Bank payment gateway.
    • SC, ST, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), ex-servicemen and physically challenged candidates pay ₹750, plus bank charges. The fee is never refunded.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check the Class 10 minimum / Class 12 maximum rule before applying — education above Class 12 makes you ineligible.
    • Keep your application number safe — admit cards are downloaded only from sci.gov.in, never sent by post.
    • Pay the fee online through the UCO Bank gateway and keep the receipt.
    • Submit before 25 November 2026, 11:55 PM — the application cannot be withdrawn or modified after submission.
    • Choose your test cities in order of preference — the Registry may shift you to another city for administrative reasons.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply if you have studied above Class 12 — the notice caps qualification at Class 12.
    • Do not submit more than one form — only the last-submitted form is considered.
    • Do not expect a correction window — a submitted application cannot be withdrawn or modified.
    • Do not expect TA/DA for the test or interview — none is payable.

    Can graduates apply for this post?
    No. The notification caps the qualification at Class XII (10+2) — candidates with education above Class 12 are not eligible. The minimum is a Class X pass.
