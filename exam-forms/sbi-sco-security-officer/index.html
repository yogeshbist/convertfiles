    Eligibility & fees

    • 60 regular Specialist Cadre Officer vacancies — Manager (Security) in MMGS-III and Deputy Manager (Security) in MMGS-II, combined total (UR 25, OBC 16, SC 9, EWS 6, ST 4). The number is provisional.
    • Education: graduation in any discipline from a Government-recognised university or institution. Graduation alone is not enough — the service experience below is mandatory.
    • Manager (Security): an officer not below the rank of Major in the Indian Army (or equivalent in Navy/Air Force) with at least 10 years of commissioned service, or not below Deputy Commandant in paramilitary forces with at least 10 years of service as an officer.
    • Deputy Manager (Security): an officer not below the rank of Captain in the Indian Army (or equivalent in Navy/Air Force) with at least 5 years of commissioned service, or not below Assistant Commandant in paramilitary forces with at least 5 years of service as an officer.
    • Age: 25–40 years as on 31 October 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per Government of India guidelines (cumulative relaxation is not available). Only candidates with medical fitness in SHAPE-1 or an equivalent medical category as on the advertisement date are eligible.
    • Selection: an online written test (200 objective questions, 200 marks, 2 hours — Reasoning 50Q/25 marks, English 50Q/50 marks, General Awareness 50Q/25 marks, Professional Knowledge 50Q/100 marks; only Professional Knowledge counts for merit; no negative marking), then an interview of 100 marks. Final merit is 70% Professional Knowledge + 30% interview. Tentative schedule: call letters December 2026, online exam December 2026 / January 2027, interview January / February 2027, final result February 2027.
    • Pay: Manager (Security) ₹85,920–1,05,820; Deputy Manager (Security) ₹64,820–93,960, plus DA, HRA, CCA, PF/NPS, LFC and medical facility. Probation: 6 months.
    • Application fee and intimation charges: ₹750 for UR, EWS and OBC candidates, paid online.
    • SC and ST candidates pay no fee. The fee is non-refundable.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: recent passport-style colour picture, light-coloured (preferably white) background — 20–50 KB, 200×230 pixels (preferred). A live photograph is also captured through webcam or mobile phone during the application.
    • Signature: black ink on white paper — 10–20 KB, 140×60 pixels (preferred). Signature in capital letters is not accepted.
    • Left-hand thumb impression (JPG) and handwritten declaration in English, black ink on white paper — 800×400 pixels, 50–100 KB. Declaration in capital letters is not accepted.
    • Supporting documents: PDF, A4 page size, up to 500 KB per file.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check the rank and service-length rule for your post before applying — graduation alone does not qualify you.
    • Keep your discharge book / service record ready — your SHAPE-1 (or equivalent) medical category must be uploaded.
    • Upload the photo (20–50 KB), signature (10–20 KB), thumb impression and handwritten declaration in the exact sizes.
    • Capture the live photograph through webcam or mobile phone when the form asks for it.
    • Submit and pay before 25 November 2026 — editing is allowed only until fee payment, not after.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply as a fresh graduate — this recruitment is only for officers with the prescribed armed-forces or paramilitary service.
    • Do not sign in capital letters — signatures and handwritten declarations in capitals are not accepted.
    • Do not send a hard copy of the application — SBI accepts online applications only.
    • Do not expect a correction window — no change or editing is allowed after the fee is paid.

    2. Find Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/28 and download the notification
    3. Register online and fill the form with service rank, experience and medical category details

    5. Pay the fee online (₹750; SC/ST exempt) and submit before 25 November 2026
