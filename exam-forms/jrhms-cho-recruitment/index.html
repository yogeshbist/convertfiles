    Eligibility & fees

    • Posts by category: UR 253, ST 280, SC 288, EWS 104, BC-II 58, BC-I 55 — backlog vacancies under Advt 05/2026/JRHMS-CHO-Integrated.
    • Education: B.Sc Nursing or Post-Basic B.Sc Nursing WITH the integrated Certificate Programme in Community Health (CPCH) curriculum, passed in or after the academic years 2016–2020, from a recognised college running the integrated curriculum. Registration with the nursing council is required as per the notification.
    • Maximum age as on 22 November 2026: General/EWS 35 years, BC-I/BC-II 37 years, Female (UR) 38 years, SC/ST 40 years; PH candidates get 10 years of extra relaxation.
    • Pay: a fixed ₹25,000 per month, plus a performance-based incentive of up to ₹15,000 per month.
    • Selection is as per NHM norms through document verification — the notification does not announce a written examination or interview for this engagement.
    • The official notification does not state any application fee amount. If the portal shows a payment step while you apply, check the amount there before paying anything.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The advertisement does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — upload them in the format and size the portal asks for on the upload screen.
    • Keep a recent colour photograph and a clear signature scan (signed on white paper) ready before you start.
    • Upload only clear, readable scans; a blurred photo or signature can get the form rejected.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check the integrated CPCH condition first — only the 2016–2020 batches with the integrated curriculum in their B.Sc Nursing are covered.
    • Keep your nursing council registration ready and valid.
    • Apply well before 22 November 2026 (11:59 PM) — no correction request is entertained afterwards.
    • Fill category details exactly as per your certificates; the posts are backlog vacancies by category.
    • Save the submitted application print for document verification.

    Avoid this

    • Do not treat this as a permanent post — it is a contractual engagement of 1 year, renewable on performance.
    • Do not apply if your B.Sc Nursing did not include the integrated CPCH curriculum.
    • Do not pay any fee amount that is not shown on the official portal — the notification states no fee.
    • Do not expect a correction window — the notification says correction requests will not be entertained.
    • Do not apply on any portal other than recruitment.jharkhand.gov.in.

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Applying without the integrated CPCH curriculum in the nursing degree — the core eligibility of this advt.
    • Treating a contractual CHO engagement as a regular government post.
    • Missing the deadline because the window opened on 31 October and closes on 22 November 2026.
    • Entering a category different from the caste certificate — these are category-wise backlog posts.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Read Advt 05/2026/JRHMS-CHO-Integrated on jrhms.jharkhand.gov.in.
    2. Register on recruitment.jharkhand.gov.in and open the CHO application.
    3. Fill your nursing qualification, CPCH and registration details carefully.
    4. Upload photo, signature and certificates.
    5. Submit before 22 November 2026, 11:59 PM and save the application print for document verification.
