    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Medical उम्मीदवार: Indian Medical Council Act 1956 के I/II schedule या Third Schedule के Part II वाली medical qualification + respective discipline में MD/MS (surgical superspeciality के लिए M.Ch., medical superspeciality के लिए D.M.)।
    • Non-medical उम्मीदवार: discipline/allied subject में postgraduate qualification (master's degree) + doctorate।
    • Qualifying degree के बाद teaching/research अनुभव: Professor 14 साल, Additional Professor 10 साल, Associate Professor 6 साल, Assistant Professor 3 साल (D.M./M.Ch. वालों के लिए कम; non-medical अनुभव doctorate के बाद उसी scale पर गिना जाता है)।
    • आयु (online application की closing date को): Professor और Additional Professor 58 वर्ष से अधिक नहीं; Associate Professor और Assistant Professor 50 वर्ष से अधिक नहीं।
    • Apply करने से पहले aiimsmangalagiri.edu.in पर AIIMS मंगलागिरी faculty recruitment advertisement No. AIIMS/MG/Admin/Recruitment/03/Faculty/2026/03 पूरा पढ़ें।
    • UR/EWS/OBC ₹3,100 (application fee ₹3,000 + processing fee ₹100); SC/ST/Women ₹2,100 (₹2,000 + ₹100); PwBD ₹100 (application fee माफ + ₹100 processing)।
    • Non-refundable। हर post के लिए अलग application और fee।

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • AIIMS मंगलागिरी के विभिन्न departments में 26 फैकल्टी (Group-A) पद, direct recruitment / deputation basis पर: Professor 11, Additional Professor 05, Associate Professor 06, Assistant Professor 04। PwBD के लिए 4% horizontal reservation।
    • Department-wise: Medical Haematology — Associate Professor 01 UR, Assistant Professor 01 UR (नया जोड़ा गया) + 01 SC; Cardiothoracic and Vascular Surgery — Professor 01 SC, Additional Professor 01 OBC; Endocrinology and Metabolism — Associate Professor 01 SC; Gastroenterology — Professor 01 UR; General Medicine — Professor 01 OBC; Neonatology — Professor 01 OBC; Nuclear Medicine — Additional Professor 01 SC, Associate Professor 01 UR, Assistant Professor 01 UR; Obstetrics and Gynaecology — Additional Professor 01 OBC; Paediatric Surgery — Associate Professor 01 ST; Pulmonary Medicine — Professor 01 OBC (PwBD), Associate Professor 01 OBC; Radiology/Radio Diagnosis — Additional Professor 01 ST; Radiotherapy/Radiation Oncology — Professor 01 UR (PwBD); Surgical Gastroenterology — Professor 01 UR, Additional Professor 01 OBC, Associate Professor 01 OBC, Assistant Professor 01 SC; Surgical Oncology — Professor 01 OBC; Transfusion Medicine and Blood Bank — Professor 01 SC (PwBD); Trauma & Emergency Medicine — Professor 01 UR; Urology — Professor 01 ST।
    • Online आवेदन 20 नवंबर–31 दिसंबर 2026 तक AIIMS मंगलागिरी exams portal (aiimsmgexams.netlify.app/faculty — 'Go to Applications' candidate login/registration page पर ले जाता है) पर। Hard copy मिलने की last date: 10 जनवरी 2027।
    • यह re-advertisement है: Advt. No. AIIMS/MG/Admin/Recruitment/01/Faculty/2026/01 दिनांक 11.08.2026 के तहत इन posts के लिए कोई application नहीं आया था।
    • आयु (online application की closing date को): Professor और Additional Professor 58 वर्ष से अधिक नहीं; Associate Professor और Assistant Professor 50 वर्ष से अधिक नहीं। छूट: PwBD +10 साल; SC/ST +5 साल और OBC +3 साल, उनके लिए आरक्षित posts पर; Government servants +5 साल (DoPT के अनुसार); unreserved vacancies के लिए apply करने वाले SC/ST/OBC/EWS उम्मीदवारों को कोई छूट नहीं। अनुभव 31.12.2026 को गिना जाएगा।
    • Pay: Professor Level 14-A ₹1,68,900–2,20,400; Additional Professor Level 13-A2 ₹1,48,200–2,11,400; Associate Professor Level 13-A1 ₹1,38,300–2,09,200; Assistant Professor Level 12 ₹1,01,500–1,67,400।
    • चयन: Standing Selection Committee के सामने interview; interview में minimum qualifying marks: UR/EWS 50%, OBC 45%, SC/ST 40%, PwBD applicable category के अनुसार।
    • DNB equivalence NMC rules के अनुसार लागू होती है। हर post के लिए अलग application और fee।
    • ऊपर के सभी तथ्य AIIMS मंगलागिरी faculty recruitment advertisement No. AIIMS/MG/Admin/Recruitment/03/Faculty/2026/03 दिनांक 18.11.2026 (aiimsmangalagiri.edu.in) से लिए गए हैं।

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Apply करने से पहले aiimsmangalagiri.edu.in पर AIIMS मंगलागिरी faculty advertisement (Advt. No. /03/Faculty/2026/03) पूरा पढ़ें।
    • सिर्फ AIIMS मंगलागिरी exams portal पर apply करें — aiimsmgexams.netlify.app/faculty > 'Go to Applications' (candidate login/registration page)।
    • पहले portal par register/sign in करें, फिर हर post के लिए अलग application (और fee) भरें।
    • Fee online भरें (UR/EWS/OBC ₹3,100; SC/ST/Women ₹2,100; PwBD ₹100), 31 दिसंबर 2026 से पहले।
    • Application की hard copy AIIMS मंगलागिरी तक 10 जनवरी 2027 तक पहुंचा दें।

    यह मत करो

    • एक ही form से दो posts के लिए apply न करें — हर post के लिए अलग application और fee चाहिए।
    • Hard-copy की deadline (10 जनवरी 2027) miss न करें — सिर्फ online form काफी नहीं है।
    • 31.12.2026 को गिने जाने वाले post-wise teaching/research अनुभव के बिना apply न करें।
    • आखिरी दिन का इंतज़ार न करें — portal 31 दिसंबर 2026 को बंद हो जाएगा।

    AIIMS मंगलागिरी फैकल्टी भर्ती 2026 के लिए आवेदन की आखिरी तारीख क्या है?

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर का size क्या होना चाहिए?
    AIIMS मंगलागिरी के विभिन्न departments में 26 फैकल्टी (Group-A) पद, direct recruitment / deputation basis पर: Professor 11, Additional Professor 05, Associate Professor 06, Assistant Professor 04। PwBD के लिए 4% horizontal reservation। Department-wise: Medical Haematology — Associate Professor 01 UR, Assistant Professor 01 UR (नया जोड़ा गया) + 01 SC; Cardiothoracic and Vascular Surgery — Professor 01 SC, Additional Professor 01 OBC; Endocrinology and Metabolism — Associate Professor 01 SC; Gastroenterology — Professor 01 UR; General Medicine — Professor 01 OBC; Neonatology — Professor 01 OBC; Nuclear Medicine — Additional Professor 01 SC, Associate Professor 01 UR, Assistant Professor 01 UR; Obstetrics and Gynaecology — Additional Professor 01 OBC; Paediatric Surgery — Associate Professor 01 ST; Pulmonary Medicine — Professor 01 OBC (PwBD), Associate Professor 01 OBC; Radiology/Radio Diagnosis — Additional Professor 01 ST; Radiotherapy/Radiation Oncology — Professor 01 UR (PwBD); Surgical Gastroenterology — Professor 01 UR, Additional Professor 01 OBC, Associate Professor 01 OBC, Assistant Professor 01 SC; Surgical Oncology — Professor 01 OBC; Transfusion Medicine and Blood Bank — Professor 01 SC (PwBD); Trauma & Emergency Medicine — Professor 01 UR; Urology — Professor 01 ST।
    AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty Recruitment 2026 me kitni vacancies hain?
    26 posts — Professor 11, Additional Professor 5, Associate Professor 6, Assistant Professor 4। 4% horizontal reservation PwBD ke liye। Ye re-advertisement hai: Advt. /01 (11.08.2026) ke tahat in posts ke liye koi application nahi aaya tha।
    AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty ki last date aur fee kya hai?
    Online आवेदन 20 नवंबर से 31 दिसंबर 2026 तक, AIIMS Mangalagiri exams portal (aiimsmgexams.netlify.app) par। Hard copy 10 जनवरी 2027 तक पहुंचनी चाहिए। Fee: UR/EWS/OBC ₹3,100, SC/ST/Women ₹2,100, PwBD ₹100; non-refundable; har post ke liye alag application aur fee।
    AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty ke liye qualification aur age limit kya hai?
    Medical: IMC Act 1956 ke schedule wali medical qualification + respective discipline me MD/MS (surgical superspeciality ke liye M.Ch., medical superspeciality ke liye D.M.)। Non-medical: discipline/allied subject me master's degree + doctorate। Experience (qualifying degree ke baad): Professor 14 saal, Additional Professor 10 saal, Associate Professor 6 saal, Assistant Professor 3 saal (D.M./M.Ch. walon ke liye kam)। Age (closing date ko): Professor aur Additional Professor 58 saal se zyada nahi; Associate aur Assistant Professor 50 saal se zyada nahi।
    AIIMS Mangalagiri Faculty ka selection kaise hota hai?
    Standing Selection Committee ke saamne interview; interview me minimum qualifying marks: UR/EWS 50%, OBC 45%, SC/ST 40%, PwBD applicable category ke anusaar। Pay: Professor Level 14-A ₹1,68,900–2,20,400; Additional Professor Level 13-A2 ₹1,48,200–2,11,400; Associate Professor Level 13-A1 ₹1,38,300–2,09,200; Assistant Professor Level 12 ₹1,01,500–1,67,400।
