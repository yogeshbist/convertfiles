    Eligibility & fees

    • 247 Industrial Development Officer posts in the Directorate of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of West Bengal.
    • Qualification: a Degree from a recognised university OR a Diploma in Engineering/Technology.
    • Age: 18–44 years as on 1 January 2024 (backdated cut-off).
    • Bengali (read, write and speak) is mandatory.
    • ₹160 plus service charges; SC/ST (West Bengal) and PwBD candidates pay nothing.
    • The form closes at 3:00 PM on 26 November 2026 — not at midnight.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Edit window: 27 November to 2 December 2026 — only one chance to correct the form.
    • Age is reckoned as on 1 January 2024 — a backdated cut-off, confirmed by the detailed notification.
    • Bengali (read, write and speak) is mandatory.
    • Selection: 100-mark written examination followed by a 15-mark interview.
    • Pay: Level-10, ₹32,100–82,900.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only on psc.wb.gov.in before 3:00 PM on 26 November 2026.
    • Check the backdated age cut-off (1 January 2024) — your age is counted from that date, not 2026.
    • Use the edit window (27 November–2 December) if you made a mistake — it is your only chance.
    • Keep your degree/diploma certificate and Bengali proof ready.

    Avoid this

    • Do not wait till midnight on 26 November — the form closes at 3:00 PM.
    • Do not apply without Bengali read/write/speak ability — it is mandatory.
    • Do not treat the 1 January 2024 age cut-off as a typo — it is the notified date.
