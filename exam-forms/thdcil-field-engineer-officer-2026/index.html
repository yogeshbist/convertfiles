    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • Field Officer (Law): full-time graduate degree in Law (60%) or 5-year integrated Law degree (60%), plus 1 year of post-qualification experience in a Legal department.
    • Field Engineer (Safety): full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Engineering (4 years, 60%) plus 1-year PG Diploma in Industrial Safety (60%), plus 1 year of experience.
    • Field Engineer (Structural): full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Civil (60%) AND M.Tech. in Structural Engineering — no experience required.
    • Field Engineer (Geo-Technical): full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Civil (60%) AND M.Tech. in Geo-Technical Engineering — no experience required.
    • Age: upper age limit 32 years for all four posts, as on the date of advertisement (25 November 2026). Age relaxation as per Government of India guidelines.
    • ₹600 for UR/EWS/OBC candidates (online only). No fee for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen/Departmental candidates/Doob Kshetra/Project Affected candidates.

    • 13 posts — Field Officer (Level I) – Law: 6 (UR 2, EWS 1, OBC 2, SC Nil, ST 1); Field Engineer – Safety: 4 (UR 2, EWS Nil, OBC 1, SC Nil, ST 1); Field Engineer – Structural Engineering: 1 (UR 1); Field Engineer – Geo-Technical Engineering: 2 (UR 1, OBC 1).     • Photo/signature and document specs (notice): recent passport-size colour photograph on white background, max 50 KB (JPG/PNG/PDF); signature in black ink on white background, max 20 KB (JPG/PNG/PDF); Xth/XIIth certificates 125 KB; Master's certificates 1 MB; caste/PwBD/experience documents as listed (125 KB-2 MB).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only on the verified THDC portal (recruitment.thdcil.in) — select Adv-09/2026 in the Job Advertisement dropdown and click Show.
    • Check the post-wise qualification and experience — Law needs 1 year of experience, Safety needs a PG Diploma in Industrial Safety + 1 year, Structural/Geo-Technical need M.Tech. with no experience.
    • Keep your photo (max 50 KB) and signature (max 20 KB) ready before starting the form.
    • Read Advt. No. 09/2026 fully on thdc.co.in before applying.

    Avoid this

    • Do not treat this as a permanent job — engagement is purely on fixed-tenure basis, 1 year extendable yearly to a maximum of 5 years, with no claim for regularization.
    • Do not expect TA/DA for the interview, joining time, leave (except as stated) or accommodation — no benefits beyond the listed remuneration.
    • Do not miss the last date — online applications close on 24 December 2026 at 06:00 PM.
    • Do not forget the pre-engagement medical test — shortlisted candidates must undergo it at their own expense.
