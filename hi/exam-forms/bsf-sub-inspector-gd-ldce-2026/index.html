    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Sirf sevarat karmchari — ye LDCE aam public ke liye khula nahi hai.
    • BSF, CRPF ya ITBP ke Constable (GD), Head Constable (GD), ASI (GD) ya Constable (Tradesmen); ya SSB ke ASI (GD) — parent Units/Forces ke bahar deputation/attachment par tainat karmchari bhi shamil hain.
    • Basic training samet 4 saal ki regular service poori honi chahiye.
    • Recognised university se Degree ya equivalent.
    • Closing date 31 December 2026 par upper age 32 saal (SC/ST ko 5 saal ki relaxation).
    • Pichhle 5 saal ke achhe APARs aur unblemished clean record zaroori hai.
    • Notification me koi application/examination fee nahi di gayi hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • LDCE se 1,167 Sub Inspector (GD) posts — BSF 235 (Male 223, Female 12), CRPF 377 (Male 347, Female 30), SSB 125, ITBP 430.
    • SIRF DEPARTMENTAL — ye Limited Departmental Competitive Examination hai. Ye aam public ke liye khula NAHI hai: sirf BSF, CRPF, ITBP aur SSB ke sevarat karmchari (jaisa specified hai) apply kar sakte hain.
    • Advt. SI(GD)/2026. Applications 12 November 2026 ko khule; last date 31 December 2026.
    • Eligibility: BSF, CRPF aur ITBP ke sevarat male/female Constables (GD), Head Constables (GD), ASIs (GD) aur Constables (Tradesmen), aur SSB ke ASIs (GD) — parent Units/Forces ke bahar deputation/attachment par tainat karmchari bhi shamil hain.
    • Service: basic training samet 4 saal ki regular service poori honi chahiye. Education: recognised university se Degree ya equivalent.
    • Age: upper age limit 32 saal, crucial date = submission ki closing date (31 December 2026). SC/ST ko 5 saal ki relaxation.
    • Pichhle 5 saal ke achhe APARs aur unblemished clean record zaroori hai.
    • Fee: notification me koi application/examination fee nahi di gayi hai.
    • 13 November 2026 ka corrigendum bhi BSF recruitment homepage par listed hai — apply karne se pehle rectt.bsf.gov.in check karen.
    • Upar ke saare facts Advt. SI(GD)/2026 (rectt.bsf.gov.in) se liye gaye hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Apply sirf official portal https://rectt.bsf.gov.in par karen — naam, email ID aur mobile number (OTP) se register karen.
    • Apply karne se pehle BSF homepage par 13 November 2026 ka corrigendum check karen.
    • Apply karne se pehle apni unit se 4 saal ki service aur APAR record confirm karen.
    • Registration number aur final printout safe rakhen.

    यह मत करो

    • Agar aap sevarat CAPF/SSB karmchari nahi hain to apply mat karen — ye LDCE aam public ke liye nahi hai.
    • Basic training samet 4 saal ki regular service poori kiye bina apply mat karen.
    • Koi disciplinary record mat chhupayen — clean record zaroori hai.
    • Last date ka wait mat karen — portal 31 December 2026 ko band hota hai.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • Service record proof — basic training samet 4 saal ki regular service
    • Degree certificate aur mark sheets
    • Pichhle 5 saal ke APARs
    • Category certificate (SC/ST), agar applicable ho
    • Recent passport-size photo aur signature scans
    • Registration ke liye valid email ID aur mobile number (OTP-based)

    दस्तावेज़ जो लगेंगे

    • Service certificate

    BSF Sub Inspector (GD) LDCE 2026 के लिए आवेदन की आखिरी तारीख क्या है?

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर का size क्या होना चाहिए?
    LDCE se 1,167 Sub Inspector (GD) posts — BSF 235 (Male 223, Female 12), CRPF 377 (Male 347, Female 30), SSB 125, ITBP 430. SIRF DEPARTMENTAL — ye Limited Departmental Competitive Examination hai. Ye aam public ke liye khula NAHI hai: sirf BSF, CRPF, ITBP aur SSB ke sevarat karmchari (jaisa specified hai) apply kar sakte hain.
    BSF SI (GD) LDCE 2026 me kitni vacancy hai?
    Kul 1,167 posts — BSF 235 (Male 223, Female 12), CRPF 377 (Male 347, Female 30), SSB 125, ITBP 430.
    Kya BSF SI (GD) LDCE ke liye aam nagrik apply kar sakte hain?
    Nahi. Ye Limited Departmental Competitive Examination hai — sirf BSF, CRPF, ITBP aur SSB ke sevarat karmchari (Constable GD / Head Constable GD / ASI GD / Constable Tradesmen, aur SSB me ASI GD) apply kar sakte hain, jinki 4 saal ki regular service (basic training samet) poori ho.
    BSF SI (GD) LDCE ki last date, fee aur age limit kya hai?
    Last date 31 December 2026. Notification me koi application/examination fee nahi di gayi. Age: closing date par maximum 32 saal (SC/ST ko 5 saal ki relaxation).
    BSF SI (GD) LDCE ke liye apply kaise karen?
    Apply online https://rectt.bsf.gov.in par — Register page par naam, email ID aur mobile number + OTP se registration hoti hai (Step 1 of 8). Apply karne se pehle 13 November 2026 ka corrigendum bhi check karen.
