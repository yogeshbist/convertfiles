    Eligibility & fees

    • Graduation in any stream — this CEN is for graduate-level NTPC posts.
    • Four graduate posts, 3,548 in all (provisional): Goods Train Manager 2,750 (Pay Level 5), Senior Clerk cum Typist 371 (Level 5), Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist 300 (Level 5) and Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor 127 (Level 6).
    • Age 18-33 years as on 1 January 2027 (relaxation for reserved categories as per the notice).
    • Application fee: ₹500 for most candidates — ₹400 of it is refunded (less bank charges) when you appear in CBT 1.
    • ₹250 for SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, PwBD, Female, Transgender, Minorities and EBC candidates — refunded in full (less bank charges) when you appear in CBT 1.
    • Correction window: 9-18 November 2026 — each change costs a non-refundable ₹250.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: LIVE capture during the form — the 2026 NTPC form takes your photo through the camera while you apply; an old studio file may not be accepted.
    • Photo (standby, only if your category offers an upload option): 35 x 45 mm passport-style JPG, roughly 20-50 KB, on a white or light background.
    • Signature: JPG, 30-49 KB, minimum 140 x 60 pixels — running handwriting (not block capitals), black or blue pen on plain white paper.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Sit facing a plain, light-coloured wall, with the light on your face — not behind you
    • Keep the camera at eye level so your full face is visible and sharp
    • Sign in running handwriting with a black or blue pen on plain white paper
    • Photograph just the signature, cropped tight
    • Aim for about 40 KB — the middle of the 30-49 KB band

    Avoid this

    • No cap or sunglasses for the live photo
    • Do not reuse a bank or SSC signature file — their 10-20 KB band fails here
    • Do not sign in BLOCK CAPITAL letters
    • Do not let the signature slip below 30 KB — it is rejected just as firmly
    • Do not depend on an old studio photo file — the 2026 form captures the photo live

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Your one-time account on rrbapply.gov.in — the form needs it before anything else
    • Signature scan ready: JPG, 30-49 KB, at least 140 x 60 px, running handwriting
    • Aadhaar and Class 10 certificate side by side — the name and date of birth must match 100%
    • For the live photo: a plain, light-coloured wall and light falling on your face
    • Copies of the final files, kept safe with your application number

    Questions

    What is the last date to apply for RRB NTPC Graduate?
    The online form closes on 8 November 2026. The fee can be paid till 8 November 2026.
    Do I have to upload a photograph?
    No. In this form the photo is captured live through the camera while you apply. Sit before a plain, light background, face the light and keep your full face clearly visible — there is no photo file to prepare.
    Can I correct the form after submitting?
    Yes. The correction window runs from 9 November 2026 to 18 November 2026.
    My signature file is only 20 KB. Will it be accepted?
    No. The Railway band for NTPC is 30-49 KB — a file below 30 KB is rejected too. Compress or re-scan to a target of about 40 KB, the safe middle of the band.
    Which NTPC CEN should I apply to — 06/2026 or 07/2026?
    Graduates apply under CEN 06/2026 (last date 8 November 2026, extended by Corrigendum-1). If you are 12th (10+2) pass, your CEN is 07/2026 (last date 13 November 2026).
    Should the signature match the one I use in the exam hall?
    Yes. Sign in the same running-hand style you will use in the exam hall — a mismatch can cause trouble at document verification.
    Where is the form finally submitted?
    Only on the official portal, rrbapply.gov.in. Convert Files only helps you prepare — dates, sizes and free tools. No form is submitted on this website, and Convert Files is not a government website.
