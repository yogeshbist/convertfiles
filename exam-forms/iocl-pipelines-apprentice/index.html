    Eligibility & fees

    • Posts: around 500 (tentative) across five pipeline regions — Eastern Region 116, Western Region 149, Northern Region 110, Southern Region 46 and South Eastern Region 79. The notice gives the breakup by region, state and location only, not by trade.
    • Age: 18–24 years as on 30 November 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, PwBD 10 years (plus 5 more for SC/ST-PwBD, 3 more for OBC-PwBD).
    • Education: Technician Apprentice — 3-year full-time Diploma in Mechanical/Automobile, Electrical, or Electronics & Communication/Instrumentation; Trade Apprentice (Assistant-HR) — full-time Bachelor's degree in any stream, (Accountant) — full-time B.Com; Data Entry Operator — 12th pass (below Graduate); Domestic DEO — plus a Domestic DEO skill certificate. Minimum 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST/PwBD on reserved seats), regular full-time only, passed by 30 November 2026.
    • Not eligible: BE/B.Tech, MBA, CA, LLB or MCA holders; candidates who have already done an apprenticeship; candidates with 1 year or more of training or job experience after the qualification; candidates whose qualification is more than 5 years old.
    • Selection: merit list on the percentage of marks in the essential qualification — no written test and no interview. Then document verification and a pre-engagement medical fitness check.
    • Training: 12 months. Stipend as prescribed under the Apprentices Act, 1961 and the Apprentices Rules — the notice prints no rupee figure. No other benefits are paid.
    • No application fee is specified in the advertisement.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Colour photograph: JPG, 20–50 KB.
    • Signature in black ink: JPG, 10–30 KB.
    • Upload both in Part-II of the application on the IOCL Pipelines portal.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • First enrol on NATS (for Technician/Trade Apprentice) or NAPS (for DEO trades) — the IOCL form needs your enrolment.
    • Apply for only ONE trade code — the notice allows a single trade only.
    • Keep your NATS/NAPS Establishment ID ready for your region (ERPL, WRPL, NRPL, SRPL or SERPL).
    • Check the 50% (45% for SC/ST/PwBD) marks rule and the 30 November 2026 cut-off before applying.
    • Upload the photo (20–50 KB) and signature (10–30 KB) in Part-II, exactly in the sizes the notice prints.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply if you already hold a BE/B.Tech, MBA, CA, LLB or MCA degree — you are not eligible.
    • Do not apply if you have already completed an apprenticeship or have 1 year or more of experience after the qualification.
    • Do not apply for more than one trade code.
    • Do not use a result that is awaited — only candidates who passed by 30 November 2026 are eligible.
    • Do not expect a job in IOCL after training — the notice says there is no obligation of employment.

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Applying with a BE/B.Tech, MBA, CA, LLB or MCA degree — those holders are not eligible.
    • Applying for more than one trade code — only one trade is allowed.
    • Skipping the NATS/NAPS enrolment and trying to apply on the IOCL portal directly.
    • Applying after having already completed an apprenticeship earlier.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Enrol first on the NATS portal (for Technician/Trade Apprentice trades) or the NAPS portal (for DEO trades), against your region's Establishment ID.
    2. Open plapps.indianoilpipelines.in and fill Part-I of the IOCL Pipelines application.
    3. In Part-II, upload your colour photograph (JPG, 20–50 KB) and signature in black ink (JPG, 10–30 KB).
    4. Choose only ONE trade code and submit the form before 5 December 2026.
    5. Save the acknowledgement — the merit list is prepared on your qualification percentage, then document verification and medical fitness follow.
