    Eligibility & fees

    • 61 vacancies (provisional) — Technician-B 46 and Drivers 15: Light Vehicle Driver-A 3, Heavy Vehicle Driver-A 10, Staff Car Driver-A 2. Some posts are at the Master Control Facility, Hassan, and the Department of Space.
    • Technician-B trades: Electronics Mechanic 24, Fitter 8, Electrician 4, Refrigeration & Air-Conditioning 5, Turner 1, Machinist 2, Motor Vehicle Mechanic 1, Electroplater 1.
    • Education: Technician-B — 10th pass with ITI/NTC/NAC in the relevant trade. Drivers — 10th pass with a valid driving licence and driving experience.
    • Age: 18–35 years as on 13 November 2026, with relaxations for reserved categories as per government rules. 21 Technician-B and 8 driver vacancies are reserved for ex-servicemen.
    • Selection: written test followed by skill test (dates to be announced on isro.gov.in / ursc.gov.in). Pay: Technician-B Level-3 ₹21,700; Drivers Level-2 ₹19,900, plus allowances.
    • Application fee: ₹500 for all candidates, paid online.
    • ₹400 is refunded if you appear in the written test — so the net cost is ₹100.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The sources read do not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — upload them in the format and size the ISRO/URSC portal asks for.
    • Keep your ITI/NTC/NAC certificate and driving licence ready before you start the form.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Choose the correct trade and post code — Technician-B has 8 different trades.
    • Pay the ₹500 fee online and keep the receipt — ₹400 comes back if you appear in the written test.
    • Check the written-test and skill-test dates on isro.gov.in / ursc.gov.in after applying — the notice gives no test date.
    • Submit before 13 November 2026, 11:55 PM.
    • Keep your ITI certificate and driving licence ready for document verification.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for a trade you do not hold an ITI/NTC/NAC in.
    • Do not assume the ₹500 fee is fully non-refundable — ₹400 is refunded on appearing in the written test.
    • Do not wait for the last night — the portal closes at 11:55 PM on 13 November 2026.
    • Do not apply offline — only the online link on isro.gov.in / ursc.gov.in counts.
