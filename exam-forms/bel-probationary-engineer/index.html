    Eligibility & fees

    • 350 posts: Probationary Engineer (Electronics) 200 and Probationary Engineer (Mechanical) 150, on an all-India basis.
    • B.E./B.Tech/B.Sc Engineering (4-year, AICTE-approved) in Electronics, Electronics & Communication, Electronics & Telecommunication, Communication or Telecommunication for the Electronics posts; Mechanical for the Mechanical posts. No GATE score is required.
    • First Class for UR/OBC (NCL)/EWS candidates; Pass Class is enough for SC/ST/PwBD.
    • Age: not more than 25 years (UR/EWS) as on 1 October 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, PwBD 10 years.
    • Selection: a computer-based test (125 questions in 120 minutes — 100 Technical + 25 General Aptitude & Reasoning, −0.25 per wrong answer; tentative date 22 December 2026), then an interview. Final weightage: CBT 85 : Interview 15. Qualifying marks: 35% for General/OBC/EWS and 30% for SC/ST/PwBD, separately in the CBT and the interview. Pay: E-II grade, ₹40,000–3%–₹1,40,000 (CTC around ₹13 lakh a year).
    • Application fee: ₹1,000 + GST = ₹1,180 for GEN / EWS / OBC (NCL) candidates, through SBI e-Pay Lite.
    • SC, ST, PwBD and Ex-servicemen candidates are exempt. The fee is never refunded.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: recent passport-size colour photograph, white background, not more than 3 months old — 100 KB to 200 KB, .jpg/.jpeg, 200 DPI, 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm. No cap, hat or dark glasses (religious headwear is allowed if the face is not covered).
    • Signature: sign on white paper with black ink and scan only the signature area — 80 KB to 150 KB, .jpg/.jpeg.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Take a fresh photo — it must not be more than 3 months old, on a plain white background
    • Keep the photo inside 100-200 KB and the signature inside 80-150 KB — compress them here to mid-band targets
    • Check the degree class rule before applying — First Class for UR/OBC (NCL)/EWS, Pass Class for SC/ST/PwBD
    • Keep the OBC (NCL) certificate dated on/after 1 October 2025, or the EWS certificate (FY 2025-26) dated on/after 1 April 2026, ready

    Avoid this

    • Do not upload an old photograph — one older than 3 months breaks the notice's rule
    • Do not wear a cap, hat or dark glasses in the photo
    • Do not submit and then expect an edit — a submitted application is final and cannot be withdrawn
    • Colour-blind candidates should note the notice says they are not suitable for this post
