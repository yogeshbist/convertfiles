    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Bharat ke nagrik hon.
    • Supervisor Trainee (Engineering): Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics ya Civil me full-time regular Diploma in Engineering kam se kam 65% aggregate ke saath (SC/ST ke liye 60%), ya B.E./B.Tech (full-time, relevant discipline) kam se kam 60% aggregate ke saath (SC/ST ke liye 55%).
    • Supervisor Trainee (HR): BBA/BBM/BBS/BMS/Social Work me full-time regular Bachelor's Degree kam se kam 65% aggregate ke saath (SC/ST ke liye 60%), recognised University/Institute se.
    • Supervisor Trainee (Finance): Commerce (B.Com) me full-time regular Bachelor's Degree kam se kam 65% aggregate ke saath (SC/ST ke liye 60%).
    • Age 01/11/2026 ko: sabhi Supervisor Trainee posts ke liye 27 saal. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 saal, OBC-NCL 3 saal, PwD 10 saal (UR/EWS), 13 saal (OBC), 15 saal (SC/ST), Ex-Servicemen Govt. rules ke hisaab se, J&K domicile (01/01/1980–31/12/1989) 5 saal.
    • Final-year students apply kar sakte hain, lekin document verification par qualification ka proof dena hoga (final degree/diploma 1 March 2027 tak ya document verification tak, jo pehle ho). AMIE eligible nahi hai.
    • UR/EWS/OBC candidates ke liye kul ₹1,072 (₹600 examination fee + ₹400 processing fee + GST) — non-refundable, 28 December 2026 tak online application ke saath pay karen.
    • SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates ke liye kul ₹472 (examination fee NIL + ₹400 processing fee + GST). Bank charges extra lagenge.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • 215 posts — Supervisor Trainee: Mechanical 103, Electrical 30, Electronics 11, Civil 26, HR 20, Finance 25. Advt. 03/2026. Regular recruitment hai, 1 saal ki training ke baad absorption hota hai. IT/CS ke liye Supervisor Trainee post NAHI hai.
    • Vacancy break-up (UR/EWS/OBC/SC/ST): Mechanical 103 (40/10/29/16/8; PwD 4), Electrical 30 (13/3/8/4/2; PwD 2), Electronics 11 (5/1/3/1/1; PwD 1), Civil 26 (10/2/8/4/2; PwD 1), HR 20 (8/2/5/3/2; PwD 1), Finance 25 (10/2/7/4/2; PwD 1). Kul 215 (86/20/60/32/17; PwD 10). Vacancies tentative hain, badal sakti hain.
    • Dates: online application 25 November 2026 (subah 10:00 baje) se 28 December 2026 (raat 11:45 baje) tak. Exam ki date 27 January 2027 (tentative — badal sakti hai).
    • Fee: UR/EWS/OBC ₹1,072 (₹600 exam fee + ₹400 processing fee + GST); SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen ₹472 (exam fee NIL + ₹400 processing fee + GST). Non-refundable hai; bank charges extra. Koi alag fee last date nahi hai — fee online application ke saath hi pay hoti hai.
    • Age 01/11/2026 ko: sabhi Supervisor Trainee posts ke liye 27 saal. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 saal, OBC-NCL 3 saal, PwD 10 saal (UR/EWS), 13 saal (OBC), 15 saal (SC/ST), Ex-Servicemen Govt. rules ke hisaab se, J&K domicile (01/01/1980–31/12/1989) 5 saal.
    • Qualification: Supervisor Trainee (Engineering) — full-time regular Diploma in Engineering kam se kam 65% aggregate ke saath (SC/ST ke liye 60%), ya B.E./B.Tech (full-time, relevant discipline) kam se kam 60% (SC/ST ke liye 55%). Supervisor Trainee (HR) — BBA/BBM/BBS/BMS/Social Work, 65% (SC/ST 60%). Supervisor Trainee (Finance) — B.Com, 65% (SC/ST 60%). Final-year students eligible hain (degree/diploma 1 March 2027 tak ya document verification tak). AMIE eligible nahi hai.
    • Pay: 1 saal ki training, basic pay ₹32,000, scale ₹32,000–1,00,000. Absorption ke baad Supervisor ke roop me scale ₹33,500–1,20,000, basic ₹33,500; lagbhag CTC ₹7.5 lakh saalana, plus DA, perks aur allowances, leave, medical (khud aur dependent family), PF, gratuity, uniform, company accommodation ya HRA.
    • Selection: Sirf Computer Based Examination (CBE) — merit sirf CBE score par banti hai, koi interview NAHI hota. Document verification ke liye 1:3 per discipline/category shortlist. CBE me minimum qualifying marks: 40% (UR/EWS), 30% (OBC-NCL/SC/ST); PwD candidates ko cut-off se 25% tak relaxation (floor 30% UR/EWS, 22.5% OBC/SC/ST). CBE ki duration, questions ki sankhya aur negative marking notification me NAHI di gayi hai.
    • Service bond: ₹3,00,000, training ke alawa 3 saal serve karne ke liye, plus surety ka stamped indemnity bond. Posting places: Bhopal, Haridwar, Hyderabad, Jhansi, Tiruchirappalli, Ranipet, Bengaluru aur Project Sites. Selected candidates ko allocated posting par kam se kam 10 saal serve karna hoga; pehle 10 saal me transfer nahi hoga (administrative grounds ke alawa).
    • Apply sirf verified BHEL ET/ST form (DigiALM/TCS iON) par online — Advt. 02/2026 aur Advt. 03/2026 ke liye ek hi common form hai, 25 November 2026 ko subah 10:57 baje live verify kiya gaya. ET aur ST ka CBE same discipline me ek hi din hota hai, isliye dono ke liye apply karne wale ko ek chunna hoga. BHEL me pehle se ET/ST/Artisan ke roop me selected candidates eligible nahi hain.
    • Photo/signature upload specifications detailed advertisement ya official FAQ me NAHI di gayi hain — wo sirf online application form me dikhti hain.
    • Upar ke saare facts Advt. 03/2026 aur official FAQ (careers.bhel.in) se liye gaye hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Sirf verified BHEL ET/ST form (DigiALM/TCS iON) par apply karen — naam bilkul Matriculation/10th certificate ke hisaab se likhen, OTP se verify karen, aur email/mobile par aaye Login ID aur Password ko sambhal kar rakhen.
    • Advt. 03/2026 poori padhen, phir discipline chunen — Supervisor Trainee (Mechanical 103, Electrical 30, Electronics 11, Civil 26, HR 20, Finance 25).
    • 5 test-centre preferences dhyaan se chunen — BHEL centres add ya cancel kar sakta hai.
    • Final printout aur fee receipt sambhal kar rakhen; CBE ke 5 din baad tak answer key aur response sheet check karen.

    यह मत करो

    • ET aur ST dono me same discipline ke liye apply mat karen — dono ka CBE ek hi din hota hai, isliye ek hi chunna hoga.
    • Successful fee transaction ke baad dobara application submit mat karen — reject ho sakti hai.
    • Pehle 10 saal me transfer ki ummeed mat rakhen — allocated posting par kam se kam 10 saal serve karna hoga.
    • Service bond mat bhoolen — training ke alawa 3 saal serve karne ke liye ₹3,00,000 ka bond hai.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • Diploma / Graduation certificate aur mark sheets (post ke hisaab se)
    • Caste/category certificate, agar lagu ho (OBC benefit sirf OBC-NCL ko)
    • PwBD certificate (kam se kam 40% relevant disability), agar lagu ho
    • Recent passport-size photo aur signature ke scans
    • Fee payment ka proof (₹1,072; SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen ke liye ₹472)
    • Matriculation/10th certificate (naam bilkul usi hisaab se hona chahiye)
