    Eligibility & fees

    • 1,237 posts: Group-A 243 (Assistant Tribal Development Officer 50, Social Welfare Inspector 89, Senior Clerk 51, Sub Registrar Grade-1 6 and Grade-2 15, Office Assistant 15, Head Clerk 10, Office Superintendent 7) and Group-B 994 (Junior Clerk).
    • A Bachelor's degree is required for both groups, with basic computer knowledge and adequate knowledge of Gujarati and/or Hindi.
    • Age: 20–35 years as on 30 November 2026, with the relaxations printed in the notice (up to a maximum of 45 years for some categories).
    • Selection: Preliminary MCQ examination (150 questions, 150 marks, 120 minutes, −0.25 per wrong answer; screening only), then the Mains — Group-A descriptive papers totalling 350 marks, Group-B a single MCQ paper of 200 marks. You must score at least 40% in every stage and every paper; this standard cannot be lowered for any category.
    • Pay is a fixed monthly amount for the first five years — ₹49,600 for Sub Registrar Grade-1, Office Superintendent and Assistant Tribal Development Officer; ₹40,800 for Sub Registrar Grade-2, Social Welfare Inspector and Head Clerk; ₹26,000 for Senior Clerk, Office Assistant and Junior Clerk — and the regular pay scale after that.
    • Prelims fee: ₹500 for unreserved candidates; ₹400 for women of all categories, SEBC, SC, ST, EWS, PwD and Ex-servicemen.
    • The fee is refunded to candidates who actually appear in the preliminary examination, through the same payment method. Candidates who stay absent get no refund, and a rejected application gets none either. The fee can be paid till 2 December 2026.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: JPG, 5 cm height x 3.6 cm width, not more than 15 KB — passport-size, white background, with the date the photo was taken printed on it; that date must not be more than one year before 30 November 2026.
    • Signature: JPG, 2.5 cm height x 7.5 cm width, not more than 15 KB.
    • Keep 4-5 copies of the same photo and signature — the same ones must be produced at every stage.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Get the photo printed with its date on it, and check that the date falls within one year of 30 November 2026
    • Keep both files at or under 15 KB — compress them here to the 15 KB target
    • Read the whole form once more before pressing Confirm — after that, nothing can be changed
    • Keep 4-5 printed copies of the same photo for the later stages

    Avoid this

    • Do not confirm the application with a wrong category — the category cannot be changed after confirmation
    • Do not assume the OJAS One Time Registration is the application — OTR only helps fill the form; you must still apply for this advertisement
    • Do not skip the prelims after paying — the fee is refunded only if you appear
    • Do not upload a photo without the printed date, or one taken more than a year ago

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Confirming the application with a wrong detail — after confirmation, no change is possible at all
    • A photo taken more than one year ago, or without the date printed on it
    • Files over 15 KB — the photo and the signature carry the same tight cap
    • Treating the OTR as the application and never actually applying for Advt 468/202627

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open ojas.gujarat.gov.in and select Advt 468/202627 (GSSSB CCE 2.0)
    2. Fill in your personal and education details — OJAS One Time Registration data can be pulled in if you have it
    3. Upload the photo (JPG, max 15 KB, dated within one year) and the signature (JPG, max 15 KB)
    4. Check everything, then press Confirm — after this step, nothing can be changed
    5. Pay the fee online (₹500 / ₹400) by 2 December 2026 and save the confirmed application copy

    Questions

    What is the last date to apply for GSSSB CCE 2.0 — Gujarat Class-3?
    The online form closes on 30 November 2026. The fee can be paid till 2 December 2026.
    What size should the photo and signature be?
    Photo: JPG, 5 cm height x 3.6 cm width, not more than 15 KB — passport-size, white background, with the date the photo was taken printed on it; that date must not be more than one year before 30 November 2026. Signature: JPG, 2.5 cm height x 7.5 cm width, not more than 15 KB.
    Is the fee really refunded?
    Yes — the notice says the paid fee is refunded to every candidate who actually appears in the preliminary examination, through the same payment method. Candidates who stay absent get no refund, and neither do rejected applications.
    Can I edit the form after confirming?
    No. Editing is possible only before you press Confirm Application. Once confirmed, neither the candidate nor the Board can change anything — including the category.
    What salary do these posts carry?

The regular pay scale applies after the five years.
