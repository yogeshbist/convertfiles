    Eligibility & fees

    • Relevant postgraduate medical qualification (MD/MS/DNB/MDS) for the department applied for — or M.Sc./M.Biotech with Ph.D. where the department specifies it.
    • Maximum age: 45 years as on 21 November 2026 (no minimum age is specified in the notification).
    • Age relaxation: SC/ST up to 5 years, OBC up to 3 years, PwBD up to 10 years (General), 13 years (OBC) and 15 years (SC/ST).
    • Candidates must be registered with the relevant Medical Council, where applicable.
    • ₹1,500 for General/EWS/OBC candidates; ₹1,000 for SC/ST candidates — pay by NEFT to the SBI AIIMS Mangalagiri account given in the advertisement, by 21 November 2026 (5 PM).
    • No fee for PwBD candidates.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 45 posts across 24 departments: UR 17, OBC 9, SC 9, ST 5, EWS 5. Anaesthesiology 10, General Medicine 4, Dentistry 2, General Surgery 2, Hospital Administration 2, Microbiology 2, Orthopaedics 2, Paediatrics 2, Pathology 2, Radiodiagnosis 2, Trauma & Emergency 2, and 1 each in Anatomy, Community & Family Medicine, Cardiothoracic Surgery, Dermatology, ENT, Forensic Medicine & Toxicology, Nephrology, Nuclear Medicine, Pharmacology, Physiology, Psychiatry, Radiation Oncology and Rheumatology.
    • ADDENDUM: the original advertisement (F. No/AIIMS/MG/Reg/SR(NA)/2026-27/04, dated 29 October 2026) advertised 28 posts in 20 departments. Addendum /04A dated 10 November 2026 raised this to 45 posts in 24 departments — this page uses the addendum figures.
    • Apply through the official Google Form by 21 November 2026 (5 PM) — submit the scanned Annexure I & II application forms with all documents. There is NO walk-in interview.
    • Appointment is under the Government of India Residency Scheme: 12 months at a time, up to 3 years in total, based on the Head of Department's recommendation.
    • Pay: Level 11 (₹67,700–2,08,700) plus Non-Practising Allowance for medical posts; non-medical appointments follow the separate scale in the notice.
    • Age and qualifications are counted as on 21 November 2026.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the advertisement and addendum /04A on aiimsmangalagiri.edu.in before applying — the post count changed from 28 to 45.
    • Apply only through the official Google Form (forms.gle/txqV4P64sK4TCrrUA) before 21 November 2026 (5 PM).
    • Fill Annexure I & II (the prescribed application forms), scan them and upload with all documents in the Google Form.
    • Pay the fee by NEFT to the SBI AIIMS Mangalagiri account (A/c 38321557910, IFSC SBIN0061485) and keep the transaction proof.

    Avoid this

    • Do not treat this as a permanent faculty post — it is a tenure-based Senior Residency (up to 3 years).
    • Do not use the old 28-post advertisement — the addendum /04A (45 posts) is the current one.
    • Do not go for a walk-in interview — there is none; applications are only through the Google Form.
    • Do not submit after 5 PM on 21 November 2026 — late forms are not accepted.
