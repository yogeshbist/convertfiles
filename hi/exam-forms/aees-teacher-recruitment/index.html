    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Pad: 254 (tentative) — Principal 4, Vice-Principal 1, PGT 27, TGT 102, Librarian 13, Primary Teacher 89, Primary Teacher (Music) 10, Balvatika Teacher 8. Vacancies tentative hain, badh ya ghat sakti hain.
    • Aayu (22 November 2026 ko): Principal 35–50 saal; Vice-Principal 35–45; PGT 40 ya kam; TGT aur Librarian 35 ya kam; PRT, PRT (Music) aur Balvatika 30 ya kam. Kam se kam umr 18 saal. Chhoot: SC/ST 5 saal, OBC (NCL) 3 saal, Mahila 10 saal (PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT/Balvatika), PwBD 10/13/15 saal.
    • Shiksha: Principal — Master's (50%) + B.Ed (50%) + PGT ke roop me 12 saal ki seva ya Vice-Principal ke roop me 3 saal; Vice-Principal — Master's (50%) + B.Ed (50%) + PGT ke roop me 6 saal; PGT — vishay me Master's (50%) + B.Ed (50%); TGT — vishay me Bachelor's (50%) + B.Ed (50%) + CTET Paper-II; Librarian — Library Science me Bachelor's (50%); PRT — 10+2 (50%) + D.El.Ed/B.El.Ed + CTET Paper-I; PRT (Music) — 10+2 (50%) + Music/Performing Arts me Bachelor's; Balvatika — 10+2 (50%, English ke saath) + 2-saal ka NTT/Pre-School/ECE diploma ya B.Ed (Nursery).
    • CTET: PRT aur TGT padon ke liye valid CTET certificate (22 November 2026 ko ya usse pehle jari) anivarya hai (Computer Science, Art Education aur P&HE ko chhodkar). CTET qualifying ank: UR ke liye 60%, aarakshit shreniyon ke liye 55%.
    • Chayan: Computer Based Test (MCQ, koi negative marking nahi) — Principal/Vice-Principal 150 sawal/150 ank, 2 ghante 30 minute; PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT 120 sawal/120 ank, 2 ghante. CBT qualifying: 60% (UR/EWS) ya 54% (SC/ST/OBC/PwBD). Uske baad PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT ke liye skill test (chalk-and-board teaching demo, Go/No Go), ya Principal/Vice-Principal ke liye interview (50:50 CBT:interview). CBT 44 shahron me; skill test/interview sirf AEES, Anushaktinagar, Mumbai me.
    • Posting: chayanit ummeedwaron ko Bharat bhar ke 15 kendron me 30 AEES schools me se kahin bhi bheja ja sakta hai. Probation: 2 saal.
    • ₹750 application fee + ₹250 processing fee = kul ₹1,000.
    • Mahila, SC, ST, PwBD aur Ex-Servicemen ummeedwaron ko application fee nahi deni, lekin har ummeedwar ko ₹250 processing fee deni hi hai — unke liye kul ₹250.
    • Bank dwara GST alag se lagta hai. Fee sirf online bhari jati hai aur wapas nahi hoti.
    • Fee har post ke liye alag se bharni hai — aur PGTs me sirf ek subject, TGTs me sirf ek subject ke liye apply kar sakte hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Vigyapan me photo aur signature ka koi fixed KB size nahi diya gaya hai — size online application portal par diye jate hain, isliye portal mange format aur size me upload karo.
    • Haal ki colour photo (JPG/JPEG; Principal ke liye PNG bhi) aur saaf signature scan; documents sahi scanner se scan karo, mobile phone camera se nahi, warna form reject ho sakta hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • PGTs me sirf ek subject aur TGTs me sirf ek subject ke liye apply karo — lekin alag-alag poston ke liye alag se apply kar sakte ho, har ek ki fee dekar.
    • Check karo ki tumhara CTET certificate valid hai aur 22 November 2026 ko ya usse pehle jari hua hai (PRT aur TGT ke liye).
    • Police Verification Certificate ready rakho — skill test/interview par ye 6 mahine se purana nahi hona chahiye.
    • Apply karne se pehle official notice me post-wise yogyata padho — Principal aur Vice-Principal ke liye sirf degree nahi, saalon ki seva bhi chahiye.
    • 22 November 2026 raat 11:50 se pehle submit karo aur confirmation page save/print karo — printout document verification me chahiye.

    यह मत करो

    • Do PGT subjects ya do TGT subjects ke liye apply mat karo — har group me sirf ek subject allowed hai.
    • Valid CTET certificate ke bina PRT/TGT ke liye apply mat karo (TGT CS, Art aur P&HE ko chhodkar).
    • Documents mobile phone camera se scan mat karo — vigyapan me sahi scanner use karne ko kaha gaya hai.
    • Confirmation page ka printout AEES ko mat bhejo — document verification ke liye sambhal kar rakho.
    • Aakhri din ka intezaar mat karo — fee aur form dono 22 November 2026 raat 11:50 par band hote hain.

    AEES Teacher Recruitment (Principal, PGT, TGT, PRT) के लिए आवेदन की आखिरी तारीख क्या है?
    Online फॉर्म 22 नवंबर 2026 को बंद होगा। शुल्क 22 नवंबर 2026 तक भरा जा सकता है।
    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर का size क्या होना चाहिए?
    Vigyapan me photo aur signature ka koi fixed KB size nahi diya gaya hai — size online application portal par diye jate hain, isliye portal mange format aur size me upload karo. Haal ki colour photo (JPG/JPEG; Principal ke liye PNG bhi) aur saaf signature scan; documents sahi scanner se scan karo, mobile phone camera se nahi, warna form reject ho sakta hai.
    Kya AEES me PGT padon ke liye CTET chahiye?
    Nahi. CTET sirf PRT aur TGT padon ke liye anivarya hai (TGT Computer Science, Art Education aur P&HE ko chhodkar). PGT padon ke liye vishay me Master's (50%) aur B.Ed (50%) chahiye, aur Librarian ke liye Library Science me Bachelor's.
    Kya AEES CBT me negative marking hai?
    Nahi. CBT MCQ-based hai, koi negative marking nahi — Principal/Vice-Principal 150 sawal, 2 ghante 30 minute; PGT/TGT/Librarian/PRT 120 sawal, 2 ghante.
    Kya main do PGT subjects ke liye apply kar sakta hoon?
    Nahi. PGTs me sirf ek subject ke liye apply kar sakte hain, aur TGTs me sirf ek subject ke liye. Alag-alag poston ke liye alag se apply kar sakte ho, har ek ki fee dekar.
    Mahila ummeedwaron ki fee kitni hai?
    Mahila ummeedwaron ko application fee nahi deni, lekin har ummeedwar ko ₹250 processing fee deni hi hai — kul ₹250 (GST alag se).
