    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • SSC/10th with science subjects, minimum 55% marks, plus ITI certificate or national/state trade certificate in the trade; OR SSC/10th (science, 55%) + 2 years full-time apprenticeship experience in the trade; OR SSC/10th (science, 55%) + 3 years work experience in the trade in a Ministry/Department/Organisation/PSU/Autonomous Body under the Government of India/State/UT.
    • Age 18–28 years as on the last date (08 January 2027); relaxations: SC/ST up to 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, CSIR/Government employees up to 5 years, widows/divorced women up to 35 years.
    • Rs. 500 via SBI Collect.
    • Exempt: SC/ST/PwBD/Women/CSIR Employees and Ex-Servicemen eligible for reservation. Non-refundable.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 28 Technician (1)/Group II (1) posts, Group 'C' (non-gazetted), Pay Level-2 (Rs. 19,900–63,200), approx. total emoluments Rs. 37,000 per month (Jamshedpur rates). Advt. No. 01/2026 dated 27 November 2026. Categories: UR 14, EWS 05, OBC (NCL) 01, SC 01, ST 07. Probation 2 years. Vacancies are provisional and may increase or decrease.
    • Trade-wise vacancies (12 trades): T-01 Fitter 3 (UR 1, EWS 1, ST 1); T-02 Turner 3 (UR 2, ST 1); T-03 Electrician 3 (UR 1, EWS 1, ST 1); T-04 Laboratory Assistant (Chemical Plant) 7 (UR 3, EWS 1, ST 2, OBC 1) — 1 post reserved for PwBD (Locomotor Disability: OA, OL, BL, including Dwarfism/Acid Attack Victim), 2 posts for Digha Centre and 2 for NML Madras Centre; T-05 Foundryman 1 (UR); T-06 Welder 1 (UR); T-07 Draughtsman (Civil) 2 (UR 1, ST 1); T-08 Computer Operator and Programming Assistant 3 (UR 1, SC 1, ST 1) — 1 post reserved for PwBD (Blind/Low Vision); T-09 Computer Hardware & Networking Maintenance 1 (UR); T-10 Driver cum Mechanic (Light Motor Vehicle) 1 (UR); T-11 Hospital House Keeping 2 (UR 1, EWS 1); T-12 Plumber 1 (EWS). Three posts are reserved for Ex-Servicemen.
    • Age: 18–28 years as on the last date (08 January 2027); relaxations: SC/ST up to 5 years, OBC (NCL) 3 years, CSIR/Government employees up to 5 years, widows/divorced women up to 35 years.
    • Qualification (same pattern for all trades): SSC/10th with science subjects, minimum 55% marks, plus ITI certificate or national/state trade certificate in the trade; OR SSC/10th (science, 55%) + 2 years full-time apprenticeship experience in the trade; OR SSC/10th (science, 55%) + 3 years work experience in the trade in a Ministry/Department/Organisation/PSU/Autonomous Body under the Government of India/State/UT.
    • Fee: Rs. 500 via SBI Collect; exempt: SC/ST/PwBD/Women/CSIR Employees and Ex-Servicemen eligible for reservation. Non-refundable.
    • Selection: Trade Test (qualifying in nature) followed by a competitive written examination — Paper-I Mental Ability Test (1 hour, 50 questions, 100 marks, no negative marking, threshold applies), Paper-II (30 minutes: General Awareness 25 questions/75 marks + English Language 25 questions/75 marks, 1 negative mark per wrong answer), Paper-III Concerned Subject (1 hour, 50 questions, 150 marks, 1 negative mark). OMR-based or computer-based MCQ, bilingual (except English), SSC+ITI/XII standard, 150 questions, 2 hours 30 minutes. Final merit on Paper-II and Paper-III.
    • Posting: normal place of posting CSIR-NML, Jamshedpur; appointees can be posted to the Digha (West Bengal) and Chennai units (2 posts each earmarked) and to any CSIR laboratory/institute in India at the Competent Authority's discretion. Helpline: 0657-2345158; email recruitment2025@admn.nml.in.
    • IMPORTANT — portal dates conflict: the apply portal (tiru.ngri.res.in/nml_tech) still shows the old schedule (opening 05-09-2026, closing 30-11-2026) and links a different English-only notification; it has not been updated to this notice yet. The dated official notice (27 November 2026) is authoritative: online applications open 15 December 2026 (10:00 AM) and close 8 January 2027 (5:00 PM). Apply only after the portal updates to these dates.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the 55-page bilingual advertisement (Advt. No. 01/2026) fully on nml.res.in before applying.
    • Apply only on the portal named in the notice — https://tiru.ngri.res.in/nml_tech/ — after it updates to the new dates (15 December 2026 – 8 January 2027).
    • Check the trade-wise vacancy and PwBD/Ex-Servicemen reservations for your trade before applying.
    • Prepare for the Trade Test (qualifying) followed by the written examination (Paper-I, II and III).
    • Save the application number and fee receipt after submission.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply before 15 December 2026 — online applications open on 15 December 2026 (10:00 AM), not before.
    • Do not trust the dates currently shown on the apply portal — it still shows the old schedule (05-09-2026 to 30-11-2026); the official notice dated 27 November 2026 is authoritative.
    • Do not apply in the wrong trade — there are 12 trades with separate vacancy lists; check your trade code (T-01 to T-12).
    • Do not miss the last date — online applications close on 8 January 2027 (5:00 PM).
