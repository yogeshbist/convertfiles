    Eligibility & fees

    • Posts: 1,501 in all — 195 Class-II posts (Advt 1/2026) and 1,306 Class-III & IV posts (Advt 2/2026). The notice itself does not print a grand total; 1,501 is the sum of the post-wise figures. The biggest post is Data Entry Operator — 609 seats across the three companies (CSPDCL 450, CSPGCL 95, CSPTCL 64).
    • Age as on 1 January 2026: minimum 21 years for Class-II and most Class-III posts, 18 years for Junior Engineer, Lab Technician, Radiographer, Dresser, ECG Technician, Attendant (Plant) and Fire Attendant. Maximum: 40 years for UR male (Chhattisgarh domicile), 45 for UR female and SC/ST/OBC; Fire Officer and Sub Fire Officer 30/35; Fire Attendant 25/30. Candidates from outside Chhattisgarh can apply only for Class-II posts, with a maximum age of 30.
    • Domicile: all Class-III and IV posts are only for Chhattisgarh domicile candidates. Class-II posts are open to candidates from outside the state too.
    • Education (post-wise): Assistant Engineer — 4-year B.E./B.Tech in the relevant branch; Junior Engineer — 3-year Diploma or engineering degree in the branch; Data Entry Operator — B.E./B.Tech or Master's in CS/IT, or B.Sc (CS/IT)/BCA, or any bachelor's degree plus a listed computer course; Medical Officer — MBBS with Medical Council of India registration; Accounts Officer — CA or CMA; Attendant (Plant) — 10th pass (Second Division) + 2-year ITI in the trade; Fire Attendant — 12th with Chemistry + NCC B/C certificate. Qualifications must be held on the last date of application.
    • Selection: online CBT — 2 hours, 100 MCQs (80 professional subject + 20 General Knowledge/English/Hindi/Reasoning or Quantitative Ability), −0.25 per wrong answer, qualifying 40% (UR/OBC) or 30% (SC/ST). Then, by post: interview (10 marks) for Assistant Engineer/Accounts/Medical/Programmer/Shift Chemist, physical fitness test for fire and security posts, typing/shorthand tests for Data Entry Operator and Stenographer, and document verification. CBT centres: Raipur, Bilaspur, Durg-Bhilai, Ambikapur and Jagdalpur.
    • Service conditions: after selection you sign a bond to serve the power companies for at least four years, with a security deposit — ₹3 lakh for Assistant Engineer-level posts, ₹2 lakh for Junior Engineer, ₹1 lakh for other Class-III posts, ₹50,000 for Class-IV. The National Pension System (NPS) applies after regularisation.
    • The whole recruitment is subject to the final outcome of SLP(C) 19668/2022 in the Supreme Court — category-wise numbers may change on that account.
    • ₹800 for UR, ₹700 for OBC, ₹600 for SC/ST — for Assistant Engineer, Fire Officer, Security Officer, Shift Chemist, Programmer, Accounts Officer, Medical Officer, Law Officer, Industrial Relation Officer, Junior Engineer, Sub Fire Officer, Security Inspector and Security Sub Inspector.
    • ₹400 for UR, ₹300 for OBC, ₹200 for SC/ST — for Stenographer, Lab Technician, Pharmacist, Staff Nurse, Radiographer, Data Entry Operator, Dresser, ECG Technician, Attendant (Plant) and Fire Attendant.
    • Regular employees of the three power companies pay no fee.
    • The fee is non-refundable and is paid online only (debit/credit card, net banking or UPI) by 26 November 2026, 11:55 PM.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The notice does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — upload them in the format and size the portal asks for on the upload screen.
    • Keep a recent colour photograph and a clear signature scan (signed on white paper) ready before you start.
    • Upload only clear, readable scans; a blurred photo or signature can get the form rejected.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check which Class your post is in before paying the fee — the fee is different for the two groups (₹800/₹700/₹600 vs ₹400/₹300/₹200).
    • If you are from outside Chhattisgarh, apply only for a Class-II post — Class-III and IV posts are CG-domicile only.
    • Give your company preferences (1st, 2nd, 3rd) for posts common to all three companies — no change of company is entertained later.
    • Apply before 26 November 2026, 11:55 PM — the form opens at 8 PM on 6 November and there is no correction window in the notice.
    • For Data Entry Operator and Stenographer, check the typing/shorthand requirement (5,000/10,000 key depressions per hour, shorthand 100 wpm) before applying.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for a Class-III or IV post if you are not a Chhattisgarh domicile candidate — the application will be rejected.
    • Do not apply for more than one branch in Assistant Engineer or Junior Engineer — only one branch is allowed.
    • Do not register twice for the same post — a duplicate registration can lead to cancellation without notice.
    • Do not carry a mobile phone, calculator or watch into the CBT hall — they are prohibited and can get you debarred.
    • Do not forget the 4-year service bond and security deposit — read the service conditions before you apply.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Chhattisgarh domicile certificate (for Class-III and IV posts)

    • Recent colour photograph and signature scan
    • Fee payment mode ready (debit/credit card, net banking or UPI)
    • Company preferences decided (for posts common to all three companies)

    • Caste / non-creamy layer / income certificate (where applicable)

    • Registration certificates where the post requires them (Medical Officer, Pharmacist, Nursing, Paramedical)

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Paying the wrong fee — the fee differs between the two post groups, so check your post's group first.
    • Applying for a Class-III/IV post without a Chhattisgarh domicile certificate.
    • Applying for two branches in Assistant Engineer or Junior Engineer — only one branch is allowed.
    • Registering twice for the same post — it can cancel the application without notice.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open cspc.co.in and go to the Recruitment section for Advertisements 1/2026 and 2/2026.
    2. Read the official notification and confirm your post, branch and category.
    3. Register (or sign in) on the application portal and choose your post and branch.
    4. Fill the form, give company preferences where asked, and upload the documents the portal asks for.
    5. Pay the fee online (₹200–₹800 by post and category; departmental candidates exempt) and submit before 26 November 2026, 11:55 PM.
    6. Download and keep the confirmation page — the admit card will be sent to your registered email.
