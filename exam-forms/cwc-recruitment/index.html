    Eligibility & fees

    • 327 posts: Assistant Manager 171 (General 70, Technical 60, Finance 10, Engineering-Civil 22, Engineering-Electrical 5, Engineering-Mechanical 4) and Junior Executive 156 (General 78, Technical 78). Each post needs a separate application and a separate fee.
    • Eligibility is post-wise: AM (General) — a 2-year PG Degree/PG Diploma in any discipline; AM (Technical) — B.Tech/B.E. in Agriculture/Food Technology/Food Science/Biotechnology-type fields; AM (Finance) — a Commerce graduate with 3 years' post-qualification finance & accounting experience, or any graduate with CA-Inter/CMA-Inter; AM (Engineering) — B.E./B.Tech in the branch; JE (General) — any graduate with working computer/MS Office knowledge; JE (Technical) — a B.Sc in Agriculture/Botany/Zoology/Biotech-type subjects with computer knowledge.
    • Age: 18–28 years for every post, as on 15 November 2026 (SC/ST +5, OBC (NCL) +3, PwBD +10, Ex-servicemen +5).
    • Your qualification must be complete, with the mark sheet/certificate in hand, as on 15 November 2026 — final-year and result-awaited candidates are NOT eligible.
    • Selection: AM — online test, then document verification and an interview (final weightage Online Test : Interview = 85:15); JE — online test and document verification only. The test runs 3 hours with a penalty of one-fourth of a question's marks for each wrong objective answer. Pay (IDA): AM E-1, ₹40,000–1,40,000; JE S-V, ₹29,000–93,000.
    • UR / EWS / OBC: application fee ₹850 + intimation charges ₹500 = ₹1,350 in total.
    • SC / ST / Women / PwBD / Ex-servicemen: application fee nil, intimation charges ₹500 = ₹500 in total.
    • Fees include GST; gateway charges are extra. The fee is expressly non-refundable, and a separate fee is charged for every post you apply to.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photograph: recent colour photo, light (preferably white) background, 4.5 cm x 3.5 cm, 200 x 230 pixels preferred, 20 KB to 50 KB, JPG/JPEG. A live photograph capture (webcam, or mobile via QR code) is also mandatory.
    • Signature: on white paper with black ink, 140 x 60 pixels preferred, 10 KB to 20 KB. A signature in CAPITAL letters is not accepted.
    • Left thumb impression: 240 x 240 pixels at 200 DPI (3 cm x 3 cm), 20 KB to 50 KB, JPG/JPEG, in black or blue ink.
    • Hand-written declaration — the text printed in the advertisement, written by you in English in black ink (not in capitals): 800 x 400 pixels at 200 DPI (10 cm x 5 cm), 50 KB to 100 KB. Scan everything at a minimum of 200 dpi, in true colour.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Prepare all four uploads to the printed sizes before you start — photo 20-50 KB, signature 10-20 KB, thumb 20-50 KB, declaration 50-100 KB
    • Keep a webcam or your phone ready — the live photograph capture is compulsory
    • Write the declaration yourself, in English, in black ink — and not in capital letters
    • Use SAVE AND NEXT and the Preview to check everything before COMPLETE REGISTRATION

    Avoid this

    • Do not sign in CAPITAL letters — the signature and the declaration are both rejected in capitals
    • Do not apply if your result is awaited — the certificate must be in hand on 15 November 2026
    • Do not assume one fee covers all posts — every post is a separate application with its own fee
    • Do not click COMPLETE REGISTRATION with anything unchecked — after that click nothing can be changed

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • All four uploads ready to size — photo, signature, left thumb impression, hand-written declaration
    • Webcam or phone ready for the mandatory live photo capture
    • Degree/PG certificate and mark sheets in hand (result-awaited candidates are not eligible)
    • Fee ready per post — ₹1,350 or ₹500
    • Application printout can be taken till 30 November 2026 — do not miss it

    Documents you will need

    • Left thumb impression — 20-50 KB, 240 x 240 px

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Clicking COMPLETE REGISTRATION with a wrong detail — after that, no particular can be changed
    • Capital-letter signature or declaration — both are expressly not accepted
    • Applying while the result is awaited — the qualification must be complete, with the certificate in hand, on 15 November 2026
    • Missing the live photograph capture — it is compulsory, not optional

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open cewacor.nic.in → Career @CWC (Direct Recruitment)-2026 → the apply-online link for Advertisement No. 2026/01
    2. Register and fill in your details post by post (a separate application and fee for each post)
    3. Upload the photo, signature, left thumb impression and hand-written declaration, and complete the live photo capture
    4. Preview everything, pay the fee (₹1,350 / ₹500), then click COMPLETE REGISTRATION
    5. Submit by 15 November 2026 and take the application printout by 30 November 2026

    What is the last date to apply for CWC Assistant Manager & Junior Executive 2026?

    What size should the photo and signature be?
    Photograph: recent colour photo, light (preferably white) background, 4.5 cm x 3.5 cm, 200 x 230 pixels preferred, 20 KB to 50 KB, JPG/JPEG. A live photograph capture (webcam, or mobile via QR code) is also mandatory. Signature: on white paper with black ink, 140 x 60 pixels preferred, 10 KB to 20 KB.     Can I change anything after registering?
    No. The advertisement states that once you click COMPLETE REGISTRATION, all particulars are final and no change — of any kind, including category — will be possible. Editing is possible only before that, through Preview and SAVE AND NEXT.

    I am in my final year — can I apply?
    No. The qualification must be complete, with the mark sheet or certificate in hand, as on 15 November 2026. Final-year and result-awaited candidates are expressly not eligible.
