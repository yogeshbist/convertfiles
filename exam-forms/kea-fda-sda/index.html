    Eligibility & fees

    • 5,431 posts in all: FDA 2,726 (RPC 2,163 + Kalyana-Karnataka 563) and SDA 2,705 (RPC 2,070 + Kalyana-Karnataka 635), plus allied posts like Accounts Assistant, Warden, Hostel Supervisor and computer-operator posts.
    • FDA posts need a Bachelor's degree; SDA posts need 2nd PUC (12th pass) or a diploma, post-wise. The qualification must be held on or before 25 November 2026 — candidates awaiting results are not eligible.
    • Age (as on 25 November 2026): minimum 18; maximum 40 for General Merit, 43 for Cat-2A/2B/3A/3B, 45 for SC/ST/Cat-1 — these maximums already include a one-time 5-year relaxation. PwD candidates, widows and ex-servicemen get further relaxation as per the notice.
    • A compulsory Kannada Language Test (150 marks, minimum 50 to pass) applies unless you are exempt — for example, if you studied Kannada as a first or second language up to SSLC or higher.
    • Selection: offline OMR competitive exam — Paper-1 General Knowledge (100 marks) and Paper-2 General Kannada, General English & Computer Knowledge (100 marks); a minimum of 35% is compulsory; −0.25 for every wrong answer, and also for a question left with no circle shaded.
    • General Merit and Cat-2A/2B/3A/3B: ₹750.
    • SC, ST, Cat-1, Ex-servicemen and Transgender candidates: ₹500. PwD candidates: ₹250.
    • The fee is paid online only, last date 26 November 2026 — and the fee once paid is not refunded.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: JPEG, recent passport photograph on a white background, file size 50 KB to 200 KB.
    • Signature: JPEG, 50 KB to 70 KB — sign with a black sketch/marker pen on white paper and scan it.
    • The notice fixes no pixel dimensions or DPI — only the format and the KB bands above.
    • Uploads outside these bands can get the application rejected — check every file size before you submit.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Aim for about 100 KB for the photo and about 60 KB for the signature — safely inside both bands
    • Sign with a black sketch or marker pen on plain white paper, and crop the scan to the signature area
    • Keep every semester marks card, the PDC and the caste/reservation certificates (with RD number) ready before you start
    • Fill the form in one sitting and re-check names and dates against your SSLC record before submitting — nothing can be corrected later

    Avoid this

    • Do not submit a photo or signature outside the KB band — non-conforming uploads are rejected
    • Do not wait for the last day — the form closes 25 November 2026 and there is no correction at any stage
    • Do not apply expecting to fix a wrong category later — only the reservation claimed in the application counts
    • Do not leave a question with no circle shaded in the OMR expecting no penalty — an unshaded question also loses 0.25

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Your registration on the KEA portal cetonline.karnataka.gov.in
    • Photo ready in the 50-200 KB band — JPEG, white background
    • Signature ready in the 50-70 KB band — JPEG, black sketch pen on white paper
    • SSLC/birth proof, semester marks cards and PDC ready
    • Caste/reservation certificates in the prescribed forms, with the RD number
    • Fee money ready online — ₹750 / ₹500 / ₹250 by category, payable by 26 November 2026

    • SSLC / birth certificate (date-of-birth proof)

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Submitting the form with a detail wrong — after submission nothing can be changed at any stage
    • Photo below 50 KB or above 200 KB, or a signature outside the 50-70 KB band
    • Treating the Kannada Language Test as optional — below 50 of 150 makes you ineligible, even with good exam marks
    • Claiming a reservation in the form that your certificates (with RD number) do not support

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open the KEA portal cetonline.karnataka.gov.in and complete your registration
    2. Choose your post carefully — FDA (degree level) or SDA (PUC level) — and fill personal and education details exactly as in your records
    3. Upload the photo (JPEG, 50-200 KB) and signature (JPEG, 50-70 KB)
    4. Enter the reservation-certificate (RD) numbers exactly as printed on your certificates
    5. Pay the fee online — ₹750 / ₹500 / ₹250 by category — by 26 November 2026
    6. Submit by 25 November 2026 and save or print the application copy — nothing can be edited after submission

    Questions

    What is the last date to apply for KEA FDA & SDA (Karnataka Group-C)?
    The online form closes on 25 November 2026. The fee can be paid till 26 November 2026.
    What size should the photo and signature be?
    Photo: JPEG, recent passport photograph on a white background, file size 50 KB to 200 KB. Signature: JPEG, 50 KB to 70 KB — sign with a black sketch/marker pen on white paper and scan it.
    Can I correct the form after submitting?
    No. The notification is explicit — once the application is submitted, no information can be changed or corrected at any stage, and correction requests are not entertained. Check everything twice before you submit.
    What is the Kannada Language Test?
    A compulsory qualifying test of 150 marks — you must score at least 50. Its marks are not counted in the merit list, but failing it makes you ineligible. Candidates who studied Kannada as a first or second language up to SSLC or higher, studied in Kannada medium, or have passed the test before, are exempt.
    How much is the fee, and is it refundable?
    ₹750 for General Merit and Cat-2A/2B/3A/3B, ₹500 for SC/ST/Cat-1/Ex-servicemen/Transgender and ₹250 for PwD candidates, paid online by 26 November 2026. The fee is not refunded.
