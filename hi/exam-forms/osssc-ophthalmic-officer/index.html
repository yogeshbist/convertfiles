    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Varg ke hisaab se pad: UR 123, ST 67, SEBC 26, SC 22 — Health & Family Welfare Department ke tahat District Cadre Group-C pad, 29 jilon me.
    • Shiksha: +2 Science, aur Odisha ki Sarkari ya Sarkar-manyata prapt sanstha se 2-saala COA ya COSA course, aur Ophthalmic Officers' Council, Odisha ke saath valid registration. Dhyan do: Diploma ya B.Sc in Optometry detailed advertisement ke tahat is pad ki nirdharit yogyata NAHI hai.
    • Bhasha: ummeedwar ko nirdharit roop se Odia padhna, likhna aur bolna aana chahiye.
    • Aayu 5 November 2026 ko 21–42 saal. Chhoot: SC/ST/SEBC/Mahila +5 saal, PwD +10 saal.
    • Chayan: 100 ankon ki likhit pariksha — COA/COSA theory 60, practical skills 25, arithmetic 10, English 5 — phir document verification aur jila avntan.
    • Vetan: ORSP Rules 2017 ke tahat Level-7, ₹25,500–81,100.
    • Do tithiyan lagu hain: registration/re-registration 29 November 2026 ko band hota hai, aur bhara hua form 5 December 2026 tak final submit karna hai.
    • Pariksha shulk ₹500, online dena hai.
    • SC, ST aur PwD ummeedwaron ko fee me chhoot hai.
    • Fee un ummeedwaron ko wapas milti hai jo asal me likhit pariksha me baithte hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Vigyapan me photo aur signature ke liye koi pakki KB size nahi di gayi hai — portal ki upload screen par jo format aur size manga jaye, usi me upload karo.
    • Form shuru karne se pehle nayi colour photograph aur safed kagaz par kiya saaf signature scan ready rakho.
    • Sirf saaf, padhi ja sakne wali scans upload karo; dhundli photo ya signature par form reject ho sakta hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • 29 November 2026 tak osssc.gov.in par register ya re-register karo — 5 December ki final submission tithi registration ko nahi badhati.
    • Apply se pehle dekho ki tumhara Ophthalmic Officers' Council, Odisha registration valid hai.
    • Sirf tabhi apply karo jab tumhari yogyata COA/COSA course ho — optometry degree/diploma yahan nirdharit yogyata nahi hai.
    • 5 December 2026 tak final submission poora karo aur submitted copy sambhalo.
    • Fee refund ke liye patrata chahiye to likhit pariksha me baitho.

    यह मत करो

    • Do tithiyon me gadbadi mat karo — registration 29 November ko, final submission 5 December ko band hota hai.
    • B.Sc/Diploma in Optometry ko samkaksh mankar apply mat karo; detailed advt COA/COSA nirdharit karta hai.
    • Samapt hue council registration se application mat bigadne do.
    • osssc.gov.in ke alawa kisi site par apply mat karo.
    • Fee di ho to pariksha mat chhodo — refund sirf baithne walon ko milta hai.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • +2 Science marksheet aur certificate scan kiye hue
    • COA / COSA course certificate scan kiya hua
    • Ophthalmic Officers' Council, Odisha registration certificate scan kiya hua
    • HSC certificate (aayu aur Odia ka saboot) scan kiya hua
    • Jaati praman patra (ST/SEBC/SC) scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho
    • Photo aur signature scans ready

    दस्तावेज़ जो लगेंगे

    • +2 Science marksheet aur certificate
    • COA ya COSA course certificate (2-saala course)
    • Ophthalmic Officers' Council, Odisha ka valid registration certificate
    • Janm tithi aur Odia ke liye HSC certificate
    • Jaati praman patra (ST/SEBC/SC), yadi lagu ho
    • PwBD praman patra, yadi lagu ho

    फॉर्म भरते समय आम गलतियाँ

    • Register karne ke liye 5 December tak rukna — registration khud 29 November 2026 ko band hota hai.
    • Optometry degree/diploma ke bal par apply karna, jise detailed advertisement is pad ke liye sweekar nahi karta.
    • Samapt hue Ophthalmic Officers' Council registration ke saath apply karna.
    • Ye na dekhna ki likhit pariksha me COA/COSA vishayon ke saath arithmetic aur English bhi hain.

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. osssc.gov.in kholo aur detailed Advt IIE-103/2026 poora padho.
    2. 29 November 2026 tak portal par register (ya re-register) karo.
    3. Apne COA/COSA aur council-registration vivaran ke saath application bharo.
    4. Photo, signature aur certificates upload karo, aur lagu ho to ₹500 fee online do.
    5. 5 December 2026 tak form final submit karo aur submitted copy sambhalo.
    6. Vigyapan ke syllabus ke hisaab se 100 ankon ki likhit pariksha ki taiyaari karo.
