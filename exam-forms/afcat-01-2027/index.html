    Eligibility & fees

    • 284 vacancies — Flying Branch 36; Ground Duty (Technical): Aeronautical Engineer (Electronics) 88 and (Mechanical) 36; Ground Duty (Non-Technical): Weapon Systems 30, Administration 53, Logistics 11, Accounts 8, Education 4, Meteorology 2; GATE Score Entry: AE(L) 11 and AE(M) 5. Men Permanent Commission 20, Women PC 11, Men Short Service Commission 177, Women SSC 76. NCC Special Entry is 10% of the AFCAT SSC seats, within this total.
    • Age reckoned on 1 January 2028 — Flying Branch: 20–24 years (born 2 January 2004 to 1 January 2008; CPL holders up to 26). All Ground Duty branches: 20–26 years (born 2 January 2002 to 1 January 2008).
    • Qualification — Flying: 10+2 with Physics and Maths plus any 3-year graduation or B.E./B.Tech. AE(L)/(M): B.E./B.Tech in the listed disciplines. Administration and Logistics: any graduation. Accounts: B.Com, BBA (Finance), CA, CMA, CS or CFA. Education: a post-graduation. Meteorology: B.Sc with Physics and Maths or listed engineering degrees.
    • Selection: online test (100 questions, 300 marks, 2 hours, +3 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one, English only) on 31 January 2027, 10:00 AM–12 noon, then AFSB interview at Dehradun, Mysuru, Gandhinagar, Varanasi or Guwahati, then CPSS for Flying Branch only, then a medical examination. Training starts in the first week of January 2028 (74 weeks for Flying/GD-Technical, 50 weeks for GD Non-Technical).
    • Pay: Level-10, ₹56,100–1,77,500, plus Military Service Pay ₹15,500 per month. Candidates must be unmarried at the start of the course. Apply online only from 12 November 2026, 11:00 AM to 11 December 2026, 11:30 PM.
    • AFCAT entry: ₹550 + 18% GST, paid online. NCC Special Entry and GATE Score Entry candidates pay no fee.
    • Pay the fee online on the AFCAT portal before 11 December 2026, 11:30 PM.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: JPG, 100–200 KB. Signature: JPG, 80–150 KB. Left-thumb impression: JPG, 50–100 KB.
    • Keep your Aadhaar card ready — Aadhaar details are mandatory in the application. Upload the photo, signature and thumb impression only in the sizes the portal asks for.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check your branch's exact qualification in the 66-page notification before applying.
    • Keep your Aadhaar details ready — they are mandatory.
    • Upload the photo (100–200 KB), signature (80–150 KB) and thumb impression (50–100 KB) in the exact sizes.
    • Download the admit card from 27 January 2027, 11:00 AM.
    • Note the exam date — 31 January 2027, 10:00 AM–12 noon, English only.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for a branch whose qualification you do not meet — branch-wise degrees are strictly checked.
    • Do not use an old or wrong-size photo — the portal rejects sizes outside 100–200 KB.
    • Do not miss the 11 December 2026, 11:30 PM deadline — the portal closes then.
    • Do not pay the fee for NCC Special Entry or GATE Score Entry — there is no fee for those entries.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • 10+2 certificate with Physics and Maths
    • Graduation / B.E./B.Tech degree (branch-wise)

    • Online fee payment (₹550 + GST, AFCAT entry only)
    • CPL licence (Flying Branch, if claiming age relaxation to 26)

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open the AFCAT portal and read Advertisement 01/2027 (66-page notification)
    2. Register with your Aadhaar details and choose your entry and branch
    3. Fill the online form and upload the photo, signature and thumb impression
    4. Pay the fee online (₹550 + GST; no fee for NCC Special / GATE entry)
    5. Submit before 11 December 2026, 11:30 PM and save the acknowledgement
