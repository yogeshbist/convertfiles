    Eligibility & fees

    • 7,268 posts in all: Constable (Civil) 4,219; Constable OISF 2,212; Firefighter 317; Constable (Communication) 300; Driver PMT 154; Firefighting Driver 62; Traffic Constable 4.
    • Most posts need 10+2. Constable (Communication) needs +2 Science (PCM) or a 3-year Engineering Diploma.
    • Driver posts (Driver PMT, Firefighting Driver, Traffic Constable) need a valid HMV/LMV driving licence, as the notice asks.
    • You must be able to read, write and speak Odia.
    • Age (as on 1 January 2026): Constable (Civil) 18-26; Constable (Communication) 18-23; OISF 18-23; Firefighter 18-25; Firefighting Driver 18-25; Driver PMT 18-23; Traffic Constable 21-32. SC/ST/SEBC and women candidates get 5 years' relaxation in the upper age.
    • Application fee: ₹500 for all categories — SC and ST candidates pay no fee.
    • Registration closes 12 November 2026 (10 PM); fee payment and final submission by 13 November 2026.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: upload, 40-100 KB — a clear, recent passport-style photograph.
    • Signature: upload, 20-50 KB — sign in your normal running hand on plain white paper.
    • Left thumb impression: upload, 20-50 KB.
    • Supporting documents: upload as PDF files.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Aim for the middle of each size band — about 70 KB for the photo, about 35 KB for the signature and thumb impression
    • Sign in your normal running handwriting on plain white paper and crop the scan tight
    • Check every name, date and category detail twice before final submission
    • Keep copies of the final uploaded files with your application details

    Avoid this

    • Do not submit in a hurry — there is no correction window to fix a mistake later
    • Do not submit more than one application
    • Do not let the signature or thumb impression slip below 20 KB — below the band is rejected too
    • Do not wait for the last evening — registration closes at 10 PM on 12 November 2026

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Left thumb impression ready in the 20-50 KB band
    • Certificates and documents ready as PDF files
    • A final check of every detail before submitting — after final submission NOTHING can be changed (there is no correction window)

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • A spelling or date-of-birth mistake in the form — with no correction window, it cannot be fixed after final submission
    • Photo or signature outside the size band — under the minimum fails just like over the maximum
    • Final-submitting before checking every section — final submission locks everything
    • Applying for a driver post without the licence the notice asks for

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Register on the official portal ousssc.odisha.gov.in
    2. Fill in your personal and education details very carefully — nothing can be changed later
    3. Upload the photo (40-100 KB), signature (20-50 KB) and left thumb impression (20-50 KB)
    4. Upload the supporting documents as PDF
    5. Pay the fee online (₹500; SC/ST candidates pay no fee)
    6. Final-submit by 13 November 2026 and save or print the application copy

    Questions

    What is the last date to apply for Odisha Police Constable & Other Posts (OUSSSC)?
    The online form closes on 12 November 2026. The fee can be paid till 13 November 2026.
    What size should the photo and signature be?
    Photo: upload, 40-100 KB — a clear, recent passport-style photograph. Signature: upload, 20-50 KB — sign in your normal running hand on plain white paper.
    Can I correct a mistake after submitting the form?
    No. This recruitment has NO correction window — only one application is accepted and nothing can be changed after final submission. Check every detail carefully before you submit.
    What is the selection process?
    A written exam (100 MCQ, 100 marks, 120 minutes, −0.25 for a wrong answer; separate papers for the Communication and Driver posts), then physical measurement, PET, a driving test where the post needs it, medical examination and document verification.
    How much is the application fee?
    ₹500 for all categories, except SC and ST candidates, who pay no fee. Registration closes at 10 PM on 12 November 2026; fee payment and final submission end on 13 November 2026.
