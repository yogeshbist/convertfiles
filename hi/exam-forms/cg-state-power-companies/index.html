    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Indian citizen.
    • Qualification post-wise hai: Assistant Engineer ke liye B.E./B.Tech, Junior Engineer ke liye Diploma, Medical Officer ke liye MBBS, Data Entry Operator aur Stenographer ke liye Graduation + computer/typing, aur anya post-wise qualifications advertisement me hain.
    • Age: minimum 18 ya 21 saal, post par nirbhar; maximum UR (Male) 40 saal, UR (Female) aur SC/ST/OBC (Male/Female) 45 saal; Fire Attendant Gr. II ke liye UR (Male) 25 saal aur anya 30 saal. Sabhi relaxations sahit maximum age limit 45 saal hai. Ex-servicemen Chhattisgarh government rules ke anusaar.
    • Class III & IV posts sirf Chhattisgarh domicile candidates ke liye hain.
    • Senior posts (AE, Fire Officer, Security Officer, Shift Chemist, Programmer, Accounts Officer aur similar): UR ₹800, OBC ₹700, SC/ST ₹600.
    • Anya posts (Stenographer Gr. III, Data Entry Operator, Assistant Pharmacist, Assistant Staff Nurse aur similar): ₹400 / ₹300 / ₹200, unhi teen categories me.
    • Departmental candidates fee se exempt hain (advertisement ke anusaar).

    • Bade groups: Data Entry Operator 545 (95 + 450), Junior Engineer (T&D) Electrical 124, Assistant Engineer (T&D) Electrical (CSPDCL) 62, Assistant Engineer (General) 60 (Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics/CS/IT), Medical Officer 15, Accounts Officer / Assistant Manager (F&A) 15, plus Shift Chemist, Programmer, Fire Officer, Security Officer, Sub Fire Officer, Security Inspector/Sub Inspector, Stenographer, Attendant (Plant), Assistant Dresser, Assistant Pharmacist, Assistant Staff Nurse, Fire Attendant aur anya posts.
    • Dates: online applications 06 November 2026 (8:00 PM) se 03 December 2026 (11:55 PM) tak. Last date 25 November 2026 ke extension notice (CSPTCL letter No. 01-01/HR-VIII/765183/2026) se 26 November 2026 se badhakar 03 December 2026 ki gayi hai.
    • Age: minimum 18 ya 21 saal, post par nirbhar; maximum UR (Male) 40 saal (Fire Attendant Gr. II ke liye 25 saal), UR (Female) aur SC/ST/OBC (Male/Female) 45 saal (Fire Attendant ke liye 30 saal); sabhi relaxations sahit maximum age limit 45 saal hai.     • Qualification: post-wise — Assistant Engineer ke liye B.E./B.Tech, Junior Engineer ke liye Diploma, Medical Officer ke liye MBBS, Data Entry Operator aur Stenographer ke liye Graduation + computer aur typing/shorthand (poori post-wise tables advertisement ke pp.6-12 me).     • Class III & IV posts sirf Chhattisgarh domicile candidates ke liye hain. Upar ke saare facts official 25-page advertisement (cspc.co.in) se hain; 1,501 ka kul advertisement ki saari 46 vacancy rows ko jodkar verify kiya gaya hai.

    • Verified DigiALM form par hi apply karen — us par key dates dikhti hain (start 06 November 2026, last date 03 December 2026).

    • Purani photo upload na karen — recent passport-size colour photo chahiye, 3 hafte se purani nahi, light background par.
    • Fee kisi aur mode se na bharen — fee sirf SBI payment gateway (Net Banking / Debit / Credit / UPI) se online bharni hai.
