    Eligibility & fees

    • Posts: Senior Relationship Manager 791, Territory Head 35, Group Head 8, Investment Product Manager 2, Head – Investment 1.
    • Age and experience are post-wise — roughly 24–50 years of age with 3–12 years of relevant experience. Graduation is compulsory for all posts; NISM/IRDA certifications are required for the relevant roles, and a PG/MBA is preferred.
    • Selection: shortlisting on eligibility and experience, after which the Bank may hold an online test, a psychometric assessment and group discussion/interview.
    • Remuneration is market-linked and fixed per post at the offer stage — the advertisement does not print one common pay scale.
    • ₹850 including GST for UR, EWS and OBC candidates.
    • ₹175 including GST for SC, ST, PwBD, ESM-DESM and women candidates.
    • Payment gateway charges are extra, and the fee is not refunded.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photograph: recent colour photo, 200 × 230 pixels, JPG, 20–50 KB.
    • Signature: scan at 140 × 60 pixels, JPG, 10–20 KB — not in capital letters.
    • Documents: PDF files in A4 size, each up to 500 KB; scan at 200 DPI.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only for a post whose experience band (3–12 years) you genuinely fall in.
    • Keep NISM/IRDA certificates ready if the post asks for them — shortlisting checks them.
    • Prepare a clean, updated resume; experience documents decide the shortlist.
    • Keep the photo at 20–50 KB and signature at 10–20 KB exactly as Annexure-II says.
    • Apply before 24 November 2026 — applications and fee close together.

    Avoid this

    • Do not treat this as a permanent bank job — it is a fixed-term contract up to age 60.
    • Do not claim experience you cannot prove with certificates or relieving letters.
    • Do not sign in capital letters.
    • Freshers should not apply — every post carries a minimum-experience condition.
    • Do not upload documents larger than 500 KB each.

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Applying as a fresher or with less experience than the post's band — the shortlist is experience-based.
    • Missing that the engagement is contractual (5 + 5 years), not a regular appointment.
    • Signature in capital letters or documents above the 500 KB PDF limit.
    • Claiming certifications (NISM/IRDA) that are expired or not held.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open the Careers section of bankofbaroda.bank.in and read Advt BOB/HRM/REC/ADVT/2026/20 in full.
    2. Choose the one post whose age and experience band fits you.
    3. Register and fill the application with exact experience details.
    4. Upload photo, signature and documents in the sizes given in Annexure-II.
    5. Pay the fee online (₹850, or ₹175 for SC/ST/PwBD/ESM-DESM/women) and submit before 24 November 2026.
    6. Save the application number and print.
