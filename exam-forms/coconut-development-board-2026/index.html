    Eligibility & fees

    • Deputy Director (Development): Master's Degree in Horticulture, Agriculture or Plant Sciences with prescribed experience in agricultural or horticultural development (maximum age 40).
    • Assistant Director (Development): Master's Degree in Horticulture, Agriculture or Plant Sciences with prescribed experience (maximum age 35).
    • Administrative Officer: Master's Degree with 5 years' prescribed experience in administration and establishment matters (maximum age 30).
    • Accounts Officer: Associate qualification of ICAI/ICMAI or the prescribed Commerce postgraduate and intermediate professional qualification route, with experience (maximum age 30).
    • Development Officer: Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture or Horticulture with 2 years' experience (maximum age 30). Development Officer (Technology): B.Tech in Food Processing or Food Technology, or Master's in Food and Nutrition, with 2 years' food-processing experience (maximum age 30).
    • Assistant: Bachelor's Degree in Arts, Science or Commerce with 3 years' experience in a Government office, autonomous body or statutory organisation (maximum age 30). Auditor: Master's in Commerce or prescribed intermediate professional accountancy qualification with 5 years' relevant experience (maximum age 30).
    • Junior Translation Officer: Master's Degree in Hindi with English studied as prescribed, with a translation diploma or accepted translation experience (maximum age 30). Stenographer Grade II: Graduation with shorthand 120 wpm and typing 45 wpm (maximum age 30).
    • Field Officer: Intermediate/Class 12 with Science and a Diploma or Certificate in Agriculture or Horticulture (maximum age 27). Data Entry Operator: Bachelor's Degree with computer data-entry speed of 15,000 key depressions per hour (maximum age 27).
    • Lower Division Clerk: Class 12 pass with computer typing speed of 35 wpm in English or 30 wpm in Hindi (maximum age 27).
    • Age is calculated as on the last date for receipt of online applications. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3 years, PwBD 10 years, Ex-Servicemen and eligible Government servants as per Government of India rules.
    • ₹300 application fee — pay through SBI Collect by 21 December 2026.
    • No fee for SC, ST, PwBD, Ex-Servicemen and women candidates.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 35 vacancies across 13 post categories: Deputy Director (Development) 4, Assistant Director (Development) 1, Administrative Officer 1, Accounts Officer 1, Development Officer 2, Development Officer (Technology) 2, Assistant 1, Auditor 1, Junior Translation Officer 1, Stenographer Grade II 2, Field Officer 5, Data Entry Operator 1, Lower Division Clerk 13.
    • LAST DATE — READ CAREFULLY: the notice says applications within 30 days of the advertisement's appearance in Employment News (issue dated 21–27 November 2026). The reference schedule lists 21 December 2026 as the closing date; one source lists 20 December. Confirm the final date and time on the official CDB vacancy portal before the last day.
    • Online applications open 21 November 2026 through the CDB Vacancy page.
    • Pay: Level 11 (Deputy Director, ₹67,700–2,08,700) down to Level 2 (LDC, ₹19,900–63,200) under the 7th Central Pay Commission.
    • Selection: post-specific written examination, skill test or interview, as notified — the short advertisement does not publish a detailed exam pattern, syllabus or test date; check later CDB announcements.
    • The advertised vacancies include anticipated vacancies and may increase or decrease.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the official notification on coconutboard.gov.in before choosing a post — qualifications and experience differ sharply by post.
    • Apply only through the CDB Vacancy page — online only, from 21 November 2026.
    • Pay the ₹300 fee only through SBI Collect — other payment methods are not accepted.
    • Confirm the final closing date on the official portal — the notice gives a 30-day window from Employment News publication, and sources differ between 20 and 21 December.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for a senior post without the prescribed experience — Deputy Director and Accounts Officer need years of experience, not just a degree.
    • Do not pay the fee by any method other than SBI Collect.
    • Do not assume the closing date — verify it on the official vacancy portal.
    • Do not apply for more than one post without checking whether the notice allows it.
