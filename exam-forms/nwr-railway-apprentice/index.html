    Eligibility & fees

    • 2,008 Act Apprentice posts across North Western Railway units and divisions (Notice 04/2026) — choose only ONE unit.
    • Qualification: Class 10 with at least 50% marks, plus ITI in the relevant trade.
    • Age 15-24 years as on 22 November 2026.
    • Selection is purely merit-based — a merit list from your Class 10 and ITI marks. There is NO written exam.
    • This is apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act — it is not a permanent railway job.
    • Application fee: ₹100 — SC, ST, PwBD and women candidates pay no fee.
    • Last date for the fee is the same as the form: 22 November 2026.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: JPG/JPEG, 3.5 × 3.5 cm, 100 DPI, 20-70 KB.
    • Signature: JPG/JPEG, 3.5 × 2.5 cm, 100 DPI, 20-30 KB.
    • Keep the photo recent (not older than 3 months) and front-facing, without cap or sunglasses — as the official revised notice asks.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Use a recent, front-facing photo — the notice asks for one not older than 3 months
    • Aim for the middle of each band — about 50 KB for the photo, about 25 KB for the signature
    • Check that your Class 10 percentage is 50% or more before applying

    Avoid this

    • No cap or sunglasses in the photo
    • Do not let the signature slip outside 20-30 KB — the band is narrow
    • Do not apply for more than one unit — choose only one unit

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Your registration on rrcjaipur.in and one unit chosen carefully
    • Recent photo ready: JPG, 20-70 KB (aim for about 50 KB), 3.5 × 3.5 cm at 100 DPI
    • Signature ready: JPG, 20-30 KB (aim for about 25 KB), 3.5 × 2.5 cm at 100 DPI
    • Class 10 marksheet (50% or more) and ITI certificate details ready
    • Fee ready: ₹100 (no fee for SC/ST/PwBD/women candidates)

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Applying with less than 50% in Class 10 — the merit list itself is built on Class 10 + ITI marks
    • A signature outside the narrow 20-30 KB band
    • Expecting an exam call — there is no exam; selection is by merit list and document verification

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Register on the official portal rrcjaipur.in
    2. Choose your one unit and trade carefully
    3. Fill in your Class 10 and ITI details exactly as on the certificates
    4. Upload the photo (20-70 KB) and signature (20-30 KB)
    5. Pay the fee online — ₹100 (no fee for SC/ST/PwBD/women)
    6. Submit by 22 November 2026 and save or print the application copy
