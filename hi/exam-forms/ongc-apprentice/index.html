    विज्ञापनAdvt ONGC/APPR/1/2026

    ध्यान दो: यह Apprentices Act, 1961 के तहत apprenticeship training है — permanent naukri nahi. 12 mahine ki training ke dauran monthly stipend milega, aur training poori karne se regular ONGC post ki guarantee nahi milti.

    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Aayu 29 November 2026 ko 18–24 saal (janm 29 November 2002 se 29 November 2008 ke beech). Chhoot: SC/ST +5 saal, OBC (NCL) +3 saal, PwBD niyamanusar +10 se 15 saal.
    • Yogyata trade ke hisaab se hai: Library Assistant jaise trades ke liye 10th pass; COPA, Electrician, Fitter jaise trades ke liye sambandhit trade me ITI; Fire Safety Technician ke liye 12th ya ITI; Lab Chemist ke liye B.Sc (Chemistry); Executive (HR) ke liye BBA; Secretarial Assistant ke liye koi bhi graduate; diploma/graduate executive trades ke liye 3-saala Diploma ya Degree; Petroleum Executive ke liye Geology ki degree.
    • Qualifying pariksha pass kiye 3 saal se zyada nahi hue hone chahiye, aur vigyapan ke district-of-residence niyam tay karte hain ki aap kaun sa work centre chun sakte hain.
    • Chayan qualifying pariksha ke ankon ki merit par hoga, phir document verification — koi likhit pariksha nahi hai. Vigyapan me result/selection ki tithi 15 December 2026 di gayi hai.
    • 12 mahine ki training ke dauran stipend: Graduate apprentice ₹12,300/mahina, 3-saala Diploma ₹10,900, 2-saala ITI ₹10,560, 1-saala ITI ₹9,600, Trade apprentice (10th/12th) ₹8,200.
    • Vigyapan me koi application fee nahi di gayi hai — application sirf NAPS portal (ITI/trade seats) ya NATS portal (diploma/graduate seats) se hoti hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Vigyapan me photo aur signature ke liye koi pakki KB size nahi di gayi hai — portal ki upload screen par jo format aur size manga jaye, usi me upload karo.
    • Form shuru karne se pehle nayi colour photograph aur safed kagaz par kiya saaf signature scan ready rakho.
    • Sirf saaf, padhi ja sakne wali scans upload karo; dhundli photo ya signature par form reject ho sakta hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Sahi portal par register karo — ITI/trade seats ke liye NAPS, diploma/graduate seats ke liye NATS.
    • Wahi trade aur work centre chuno jo tumhare district aur yogyata ke hisaab se allowed hai.
    • NAPS/NATS registration number sambhal kar rakho; aage har jagah lagega.
    • 20 November se pehle apply karo — uske baad na fee window hai, na correction window.
    • Qualifying exam ki marksheet ready rakho; chayan usi ke ankon par hai.

    यह मत करो

    • Ise permanent ONGC naukri mat samjho — ye 12 mahine ki apprenticeship training hai.
    • Is application ke liye kisi ko paise mat do — vigyapan me koi fee nahi hai.
    • Qualifying exam pass kiye 3 saal ho gaye hon to apply mat karo.
    • Apni yogyata se alag trade ke liye apply mat karo.
    • Correction window ka intezaar mat karo — vigyapan me koi correction window nahi hai.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • NAPS ya NATS registration number ready
    • Qualifying exam ki marksheet aur certificate scan kiye hue
    • 10th certificate (aayu ka saboot) scan kiya hua
    • Jaati / PwBD certificate scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho
    • Aadhaar card aur bank khata vivaran (stipend ke liye) ready
    • Nayi colour photograph scan ki hui

    दस्तावेज़ जो लगेंगे

    • 10th ki marksheet aur certificate
    • Qualifying pariksha ki marksheet aur certificate (trade ke hisaab se ITI / Diploma / Degree)
    • Jaati praman patra (SC/ST/OBC-NCL), yadi lagu ho
    • PwBD praman patra, yadi lagu ho
    • Aadhaar card
    • Stipend ke liye bank khata vivaran

    फॉर्म भरते समय आम गलतियाँ

    • Galat portal par register karna — ITI ummeedwar NATS par ya graduate NAPS par — jisse ONGC application link hi nahi hoti.
    • Apne niwas ke liye allowed district se bahar ka work centre chunna.
    • Qualifying exam ke 3 saal poore hone ke baad apply karna, jisse ummeedwar ayogya ho jata hai.
    • Ye samajhna ki training permanent post me badal jayegi — vigyapan me aisi koi guarantee nahi hai.

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. ongcindia.com par Advt ONGC/APPR/1/2026 padho aur apne trade ki yogyata aur work centres note karo.
    2. ITI/trade seats ke liye apprenticeshipindia.gov.in (NAPS) par, diploma/graduate seats ke liye nats.education.gov.in (NATS) par register karo.
    3. Portal profile poora karo aur registration number note karo.
    4. Apne trade aur work centre me ONGC apprentice engagement (Advt ONGC/APPR/1/2026) ke liye apply karo.
    5. Documents upload karke 20 November 2026 se pehle submit karo.
    6. Submitted application sambhal kar rakho aur 15 December 2026 ke aas-paas selection list dekho.

    ONGC Apprentice के लिए आवेदन की आखिरी तारीख क्या है?

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर का size क्या होना चाहिए?
    Vigyapan me photo aur signature ke liye koi pakki KB size nahi di gayi hai — portal ki upload screen par jo format aur size manga jaye, usi me upload karo. Form shuru karne se pehle nayi colour photograph aur safed kagaz par kiya saaf signature scan ready rakho.
    Kya ONGC apprentice permanent naukri hai?
    Nahi. Ye Apprentices Act, 1961 ke तहत 12 mahine ki apprenticeship training hai jisme monthly stipend milta hai. Vigyapan me training ke baad regular ONGC post ka koi vaada nahi hai.
    Kya koi application fee ya likhit pariksha hai?
    Vigyapan me koi fee nahi di gayi hai, aur koi likhit pariksha bhi nahi hai — chayan qualifying pariksha ke ankon ki merit par hoga, phir document verification hoga.
