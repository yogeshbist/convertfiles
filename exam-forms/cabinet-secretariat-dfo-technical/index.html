    Eligibility & fees

    • 250 tentative vacancies for Deputy Field Officer (Technical), Group 'B', Non-Gazetted, Level-7 of the Pay Matrix — Computer Science/IT 112, Electronics/Communication 112, Data Science/AI 10, Physics 5, Mechanical 4, Civil 3, Chemistry 2, Mathematics 1, Statistics 1.
    • Education: a Bachelor's degree in Engineering or Technology, or a Master's degree in Science, in one of the notified subjects — and a valid GATE Score Card with qualifying cut-off marks in GATE 2024, 2025 or 2026 in the matching subject.
    • Age: not more than 30 years as on the closing date, 15 November 2026. Relaxation for SC, ST, OBC, Central Government employees and ex-servicemen as per Central Government orders.
    • Selection: applications are ranked by valid GATE score for each subject separately; candidates are shortlisted at five times the vacancies and called for interview. Final selection is on GATE score + interview, followed by character and antecedent verification and a medical examination.
    • Pay: Level-7; total initial monthly emoluments including all allowances are about ₹1,00,000 for a posting in New Delhi. Initial posting is New Delhi, with field postings and All India transfer liability.
    • No application fee is specified in the notification — the only cost is downloading the form and sending it by ordinary post.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Two recent passport-size colour photographs, with your name and date of birth written on the back, self-attested — the form is sent by post, so no KB sizes apply.
    • The application form must be typed on A-4 size paper and filled in English capital (block) letters, using black or blue ink only.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply for only one subject — the one matching your degree and GATE paper.
    • Type the form on A-4 paper in English capital (block) letters with black or blue ink.
    • Send it by ordinary post so that it reaches Post Bag No. 001, Lodhi Road Head Post Office, New Delhi-110003 on or before 15 November 2026.
    • Attach self-attested copies of all documents — matriculation, 10+2, degree, GATE score card, caste certificate (if claiming relaxation) and Aadhaar.
    • Sign the declaration with date and place, and affix a self-attested recent passport-size colour photograph.

    Avoid this

    • Do not send the form by speed post or courier — the notification asks for ordinary post only.
    • Do not leave any column blank — unsigned or incomplete applications are summarily rejected.
    • Do not apply for more than one subject.
    • Do not assume an online form exists — there is no online application for this recruitment.

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Sending the form by speed post or courier instead of ordinary post
    • Leaving a column blank or forgetting the signature and date
    • Applying for more than one subject
    • Posting late — the form must REACH New Delhi by 15 November 2026, not just be posted by then

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Download the notification and the application form from the Cabinet Secretariat vacancies page (cabsec.gov.in)
    2. Choose one subject and note its GATE subject code
    3. Type the form on A-4 paper in English capital letters with black or blue ink
    4. Affix a self-attested photograph, sign the declaration, and attach self-attested documents
    5. Post it by ordinary post to Post Bag No. 001, Lodhi Road Head Post Office, New Delhi-110003, so it reaches by 15 November 2026
