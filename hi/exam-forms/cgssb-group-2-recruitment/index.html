    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Pad: Assistant Grade-3 — 1,206; ASI (District Unit Cadre) — 279; Registrar Clerk — 50; ASI (General Branch) — 40; SI-A Stenographer — 34; Stenographer (Hindi) — 30; Data Entry Operator — 25; Steno-Typist — 23; Stenographer (Bilingual) — 13; Stenographer (English) — 3.
    • Yogyata aur aayu post ke hisaab se hai — ye Graduate aur Post-Graduate star ke Class-III pad hain, aur portal par detailed advertisement ki eligibility table hi antim hai. Fee dene se pehle apne post ki row dhyaan se dekho.
    • Chayan ek hi likhit pariksha se hoga — 10 January 2027 (subah 10:00–12:15) ko paanchon sambhagiya mukhyalayon par. Admit card 4 January 2027 ke aas-paas aane ki ummeed hai.
    • Submitted form ki galtiyon ke liye 28 se 30 November 2026 tak correction window rahegi.
    • Application fee apply karte samay CGSSB portal par varg ke hisaab se online lagti hai.
    • Is board ka khaas niyam: Chhattisgarh ke sthaniya niwasi ummeedwar jo asal me likhit pariksha me baithte hain, unhe pariksha shulk wapas milta hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Vigyapan me photo aur signature ke liye koi pakki KB size nahi di gayi hai — portal ki upload screen par jo format aur size manga jaye, usi me upload karo.
    • Form shuru karne se pehle nayi colour photograph aur safed kagaz par kiya saaf signature scan ready rakho.
    • Sirf saaf, padhi ja sakne wali scans upload karo; dhundli photo ya signature par form reject ho sakta hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Post chunne se pehle detailed advertisement me post-wise eligibility table padho.
    • 27 November 2026 (shaam 5:00 baje) tak apply karo — window chhoti hai.
    • Koi asal galti ho to correction window (28–30 November) ka upyog karo.
    • CG sthaniya niwasi: domicile certificate ready rakho — fee refund aur aarakshan dono isi par nirbhar hain.
    • Apna pariksha kendra sambhag note karo; pariksha sirf paanch sambhagiya mukhyalayon par hogi.

    यह मत करो

    • Ye mat samjho ki har post ki yogyata ek jaisi hai — yogyata post ke hisaab se hai.
    • Fee refund chahiye to pariksha me baithna mat chhodo — refund sirf unhi ko milta hai jo asal me baithte hain.
    • Aadhikarik CGSSB portal ke alawa kisi site par apply mat karo.
    • Galat post chunne par correction ka intezaar tab tak mat karo jab tak notice ke correction niyam na padh lo.
    • Dhundli photo ya signature upload mat karo.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • Degree / PG marksheets aur certificates scan kiye hue (post ki mang ke anusar)
    • Chhattisgarh domicile certificate scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho
    • Jaati praman patra scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho
    • 10th certificate (aayu ka saboot) scan kiya hua
    • Photo aur signature scans ready
    • Application fee dene ka sadhan ready

    दस्तावेज़ जो लगेंगे

    • Apply kiye gaye post ke liye Degree/PG marksheets aur certificate
    • Chhattisgarh domicile certificate (aarakshan aur fee refund ke liye)
    • Jaati praman patra, yadi lagu ho
    • Janm tithi ke liye 10th certificate
    • Photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID)

    फॉर्म भरते समय आम गलतियाँ

    • Eligibility table me apne post ki yogyata row padhe bina post chunna.
    • 27 November ko shaam 5:00 baje ki bandi chook jana — window zyadatar portals se pehle band hoti hai.
    • Ye bhoolna ki fee refund sirf un CG sthaniya niwasiyon ko milta hai jo asal me pariksha me baithte hain.
    • Correction window (28–30 November) nikal jane dena jabki form me galat vivaran pada ho.

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. cgssb.cgstate.gov.in kholo aur Exam G20227 ki detailed advertisement padho.
    2. 27 November 2026, shaam 5:00 baje se pehle apne post ke liye register karke application bharo.
    3. Photo, signature aur documents upload karo, aur varg ke hisaab se fee online do.
    4. Kuch galat ho to 28 se 30 November 2026 ke correction window me sudharo.
    5. 4 January 2027 ke aas-paas admit card download karo aur 10 January 2027 ko likhit pariksha do.

    CGSSB Group-2 (Class-III Posts) के लिए आवेदन की आखिरी तारीख क्या है?

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर का size क्या होना चाहिए?

    हाँ। Correction window 28 नवंबर 2026 से 30 नवंबर 2026 तक खुला रहेगा।
    Kya CGSSB ki fee asal me wapas milti hai?

    Kya main apna CGSSB form sudhar sakta hoon?
    Haan — application band hone (27 November) ke turant baad 28 se 30 November 2026 tak correction window khuli hai. Kaun se fields sudhar sakte hain, ye notice me dekho.
