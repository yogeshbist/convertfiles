    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • Trainee Engineer (E&C): full-time regular Bachelor's Degree in Engineering / Technology / B.Sc. (Engineering) in Electronics & Communication discipline (Electronics & Communication / Electronics / Electronics & Tele-communication / Electronics & Power / Power Electronics / Electronics & Instrumentation / Instrumentation & Control) from a recognised Indian University / Institute approved by AICTE, with minimum 60% marks; AMIE (enrolment up to 31.05.2013) with minimum 60% marks is also eligible.
    • Trainee Engineer/Officer (Environment): B.E. in Environmental Engineering or M.Sc. in Environmental Science, with minimum 60% marks.
    • Trainee Engineer/Officer (IT): B.E. in IT / Computer Science / Computer Engineering or MCA, with minimum 60% marks.
    • Trainee Officer (Geology): M.Sc. in Geology / M.Tech in Applied Geology, with minimum 60% marks.
    • Trainee Officer (Geophysicist): M.Tech / M.Sc. (Tech) in Applied Geophysics / Exploration Geophysics, with minimum 60% marks.
    • Valid GATE-2026 score in the corresponding paper. Only the GATE-2026 score (normalised marks out of 100) is valid. Final-year appearing students are NOT eligible.
    • Maximum age 30 years as on 26 December 2026 (last date of application). Ex-servicemen relaxation as per Government guidelines.
    • Rs.708 (Rs.600 plus applicable taxes) for UR/EWS/OBC (NCL).
    • No fee for SC/ST/Women/PwBD/Ex-servicemen.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 47 Trainee Engineer / Officer posts (E&C, Environment, IT, Geology, Geophysicist) through GATE-2026 score. Advt. No. NH/Talent Acquisition/09/2026, dated 25 November 2026 (NHPC Limited, Navratna CPSE).
    • Breakup: Trainee Engineer (E&C) 9 + 2 backlog; Trainee Engineer/Officer (Environment) 8 + 2 backlog; Trainee Engineer/Officer (IT) 11 + 2 backlog; Trainee Officer (Geology) 7; Trainee Officer (Geophysicist) 4. Regular + backlog total 45 + 2 PwBD special-drive vacancies (IT 1, Environment 1) = 47.
    • Dates: online applications 27 November 2026 (10:00 AM) to 26 December 2026 (5:00 PM). Maximum age 30 years as on 26 December 2026.
    • Qualification: E&C — B.E. in Electronics & Communication / Electronics / Electronics & Tele-communication / Electronics & Power / Power Electronics / Electronics & Instrumentation / Instrumentation & Control, minimum 60% marks; Environment — B.E. in Environmental Engineering or M.Sc. in Environmental Science, minimum 60% marks; IT — B.E. in IT / Computer Science / Computer Engineering or MCA, minimum 60% marks; Geology — M.Sc. in Geology / M.Tech in Applied Geology, minimum 60% marks; Geophysicist — M.Tech / M.Sc. (Tech) in Applied Geophysics / Exploration Geophysics, minimum 60% marks. Final-year appearing students are NOT eligible.
    • Selection: GATE-2026 normalised score 75% + Group Discussion 5% + Personal Interview 20%; shortlisting up to 5 times the vacancies; minimum combined GD+PI qualifying marks 40% (UR/OBC (NCL)/EWS) and 30% (SC/ST/PwBD). Only the GATE-2026 score (normalised marks out of 100) is valid.
    • Pay: E2 scale Rs.50,000–3%–Rs.1,60,000 (IDA), about Rs.16–17 lakh total annual emoluments; 1 year training + 1 year probation.
    • Fee: Rs.708 (Rs.600 plus applicable taxes) for UR/EWS/OBC (NCL); no fee for SC/ST/Women/PwBD/Ex-servicemen. Bank charges/processing charges extra, to be borne by the candidate.
    • Surety bond: Rs.4,00,000 plus applicable GST (UR/OBC/EWS) and Rs.2,00,000 plus GST (SC/ST/PwBD), minimum 4 years' service including the training period.
    • Photo/signature: passport-size colour photograph and signature in JPEG format of less than 50 KB and 25 KB respectively. Scanned copies of certificates should be readable, PDF 100-400 KB each.
    • Note: the registration portal's 'view full advertisement' button links to a stale 2023-24 advertisement — the correct notice is the Advt. 09/2026 PDF linked on this page. The registration form was seen live on 27 November 2026 after 10:00 AM.
    • All facts above are from Advt. No. NH/Talent Acquisition/09/2026 (nhpcindia.com) and the live registration form.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advt. No. NH/Talent Acquisition/09/2026 (dated 25 November 2026) fully on nhpcindia.com before applying.
    • Check the GATE-2026 paper for your post in the advertisement (E&C: EC; Environment: ES; IT: CS; Geology: GG; Geophysicist: GG).
    • Apply on the verified NHPC registration form (RECTT_452026) — it was seen live on 27 November 2026 after 10:00 AM.
    • Keep your photo (JPEG, less than 50 KB) and signature (JPEG, less than 25 KB) ready before starting the form.
    • Save the registration number after submitting the form — all future correspondence is by email only.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply with any other year's GATE score — only the GATE-2026 score is valid for this recruitment.
    • Do not apply if you are a final-year appearing student — final-year students are NOT eligible for Advt. 09/2026.
    • Do not enter a wrong GATE registration number — applications registered with an invalid GATE registration number are rejected.
    • Do not use the portal's 'view full advertisement' button — it links to a stale 2023-24 advertisement; the correct notice is the Advt. 09/2026 PDF linked on this page.
    • Do not miss the window — applications close 26 December 2026 at 5:00 PM.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Valid GATE-2026 score card with registration number (GATE-2026 only)
    • Aadhaar card, mobile number and email ID
    • Recent passport-size colour photo (JPEG, less than 50 KB) and signature (JPEG, less than 25 KB)
