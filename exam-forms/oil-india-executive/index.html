    Eligibility & fees

    • 32 posts — Senior Officer (Mechanical) 14, Senior Accounts Officer / Senior Internal Auditor 6, Superintending Research Scientist 5, Senior Officer (Petroleum) 4, Hindi Officer 2, Confidential Secretary 1.
    • Research Scientist: PhD in Chemistry, Geology or Petroleum Engineering, plus a relevant Master's with 60% and 4 years of experience; UR age up to 37. Senior Officer (Mechanical): 4-year B.E./B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering with 65%; UR age up to 27.
    • Senior Officer (Petroleum): a 2-year Master's in Petroleum Engineering/Technology with 60% plus an engineering degree. Accounts: Associate Membership of ICAI or ICMAI. Hindi Officer: PG in Hindi plus 3 years of experience. Confidential Secretary: graduation plus a 1- or 2-year secretarial diploma plus 2 years of experience (PwBD backlog post).
    • Grade B posts (except Accounts) carry a 3-year service bond; probation is 1 year. All eligibility is counted as on 14 November 2026.
    • Selection: a CBT of 100 marks in 90 minutes (qualifying 50 for UR/OBC/EWS, 40 for SC/ST/PwBD), then a Personal Interview of 15 marks — final merit is CBT × 0.85 + interview — for Grade B/C posts. For Grade A posts: CBT plus a proficiency/skill test (qualifying; merit on the CBT). The CBT is tentatively on 28 December 2026.
    • Apply online through oil-india.com (apply via the DigiALM portal) from 15 October 2026 (4:00 PM) to 14 November 2026, 11:00 AM. No correction window is mentioned.
    • Application fee: ₹500 plus taxes for General/OBC-NCL candidates. SC/ST/PwBD/EWS/ex-servicemen pay nothing. The fee is non-refundable.
    • Pay the fee by 14 November 2026, 11:00 AM.
