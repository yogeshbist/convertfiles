    Eligibility & fees

    • 1,145 permanent Sub-Surveyor posts in the Directorate of Land Records & Surveys, Department of Land & Land Reforms and Refugee Relief & Rehabilitation, West Bengal.
    • Madhyamik (10th) plus a 3-year Diploma in Survey Engineering — OR Madhyamik plus a 2-year ITI Surveyor certificate course with 1 year's experience in Survey & Settlement work, as the notice describes.
    • Age: 21–45 years as on 1 January 2024 (SC/ST of West Bengal +5 years, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) of West Bengal +3 years, PwBD (40%+) up to 45 years). Age is taken only from the Madhyamik/equivalent certificate.
    • You must be able to read, write and speak Bengali — this is not compulsory if your mother tongue is Nepali, unless specified.
    • Selection: a written examination — Paper-I (Bengali/Nepali, 50 marks, qualifying only; 30% needed) and Paper-II (200 MCQ — English 50, Elementary Mathematics 50, Survey Engineering 100; −0.25 per wrong answer). Final merit is on Paper-II marks alone. Pay: Level-7, ₹24,700–63,900.
    • Application fee: ₹110 plus service charges, online only.
    • SC/ST candidates of West Bengal and PwBD (40%+) pay no fee. OBC candidates pay the full fee, and SC/ST of other states get no exemption. The fee is never refunded — and it must be paid by 9 November 2026, 3:00 PM.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photograph: the advertisement prints no fixed file size or dimensions for the upload — the online form at psc.wb.gov.in shows the accepted requirement at the upload step. Keep a recent passport-size photograph ready.
    • Signature: no size is printed in the advertisement either — keep a clear signature scan on white paper ready, and set the target to whatever the form shows when you upload.
    • Quote one photo identity proof in the form — Madhyamik certificate bearing a photograph, Passport, PAN, EPIC, Driving Licence or Aadhaar — and bring the same one in original to the exam venue.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Submit well before 3:00 PM on 9 November — this window closes in the afternoon, not at midnight
    • Read the size the upload step shows and set exactly that target in the tools here
    • Use the one-time edit window (11-16 November) only after re-checking the whole form — it can be availed only once
    • Keep ready the same photo ID that you quote in the form — the original is checked at the exam venue

    Avoid this

    • Do not plan to apply on the evening of 9 November — applications and the fee both close at 3:00 PM
    • Do not spend the single edit chance before checking every field of the submitted form
    • Do not quote one photo ID in the form and carry a different one to the exam
    • SC/ST candidates from outside West Bengal should not assume the fee exemption — it covers West Bengal SC/ST only

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Missing the real deadline — 3:00 PM on 9 November 2026, not the end of the day
    • Trying to change caste/category claims later — on those, the submitted application is treated as final
    • Spending the single edit window without first re-checking the entire form
    • Forgetting that an address change must first be made in the Profile Section, before using the edit window

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Log in at psc.wb.gov.in and open the Sub-Surveyor (Advt 22/2024) application
    2. Fill in your details and quote one photo ID proof
    3. Upload the photograph and signature as the form directs
    4. Pay the fee online (₹110 + service charges; exempt for SC/ST of West Bengal and PwBD 40%+)
    5. Submit before 3:00 PM on 9 November 2026 and save the application copy
    6. If truly needed, use the edit window once, between 11 and 16 November 2026 (make address changes in the Profile Section first)

    Questions

    What is the last date to apply for WBPSC Sub-Surveyor?
    The online form closes on 9 November 2026. The fee can be paid till 9 November 2026.
    What size should the photo and signature be?
    Photograph: the advertisement prints no fixed file size or dimensions for the upload — the online form at psc.wb.gov.in shows the accepted requirement at the upload step. Keep a recent passport-size photograph ready. Signature: no size is printed in the advertisement either — keep a clear signature scan on white paper ready, and set the target to whatever the form shows when you upload.
    Can I correct the form after submitting?
    Yes. The correction window runs from 11 November 2026 to 16 November 2026.
    What exactly is the last date and time?
    9 November 2026, up to 3:00 PM — for both the application and the fee payment. This is an afternoon closing, not midnight.
    Can I edit the form after submitting?
    Yes — an edit window runs from 11 to 16 November 2026, but it can be availed only once and only some fields can be changed. An address change must first be made in the Profile Section. No change is entertained after 16 November.
    What is the salary?
    Pay Level-7 under the WBS (ROPA) Rules, 2019: ₹24,700–63,900 plus admissible allowances. These are permanent posts.
