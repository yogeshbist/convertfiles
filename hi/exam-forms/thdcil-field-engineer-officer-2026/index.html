    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Bharat ke nagrik hon.
    • Field Officer (Law): Law me full-time graduate degree (60%) ya 5 saal ki integrated Law degree (60%), plus Legal department me 1 saal ka post-qualification experience.
    • Field Engineer (Safety): full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Engineering (4 saal, 60%) plus 1 saal ka PG Diploma in Industrial Safety (60%), plus 1 saal ka experience.
    • Field Engineer (Structural): full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Civil (60%) AUR M.Tech. in Structural Engineering — koi experience nahi chahiye.
    • Field Engineer (Geo-Technical): full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Civil (60%) AUR M.Tech. in Geo-Technical Engineering — koi experience nahi chahiye.
    • Age: chaaron posts ke liye upper age limit 32 saal, advertisement ki date (25 November 2026) ko. Age relaxation Government of India ke guidelines ke hisaab se.
    • ₹600 UR/EWS/OBC candidates ke liye (sirf online). SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen/Departmental candidates/Doob Kshetra/Project Affected candidates ko koi fee nahi deni.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • 13 posts — Field Officer (Level I) – Law: 6 (UR 2, EWS 1, OBC 2, SC Nil, ST 1); Field Engineer – Safety: 4 (UR 2, EWS Nil, OBC 1, SC Nil, ST 1); Field Engineer – Structural Engineering: 1 (UR 1); Field Engineer – Geo-Technical Engineering: 2 (UR 1, OBC 1). Advt. No. 09/2026, dated 25 November 2026.
    • Dates: online application 25 November 2026 subah 10:00 baje se; closing 24 December 2026 shaam 06:00 baje.
    • Age: chaaron posts ke liye upper age limit 32 saal, advertisement ki date (25 November 2026) ko. Age relaxation Government of India ke guidelines ke hisaab se.
    • Qualification: Law — Law me full-time graduate degree (60%) ya 5 saal ki integrated Law degree (60%), plus Legal department me 1 saal ka post-qualification experience; Safety — full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Engineering (4 saal, 60%) plus 1 saal ka PG Diploma in Industrial Safety (60%), plus 1 saal ka experience; Structural — full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Civil (60%) AUR M.Tech. in Structural Engineering, koi experience nahi chahiye; Geo-Technical — full-time B.E./B.Tech./B.Sc. Civil (60%) AUR M.Tech. in Geo-Technical Engineering, koi experience nahi chahiye.
    • Pay: Remuneration Level-I ₹53,580, notice ki remuneration table ke hisaab se.
    • Contract: fixed tenure — 1 saal, har saal extend ho sakta hai, 4 saal aur, maximum 5 saal; engagement sirf fixed-tenure basis par hai, regularization ka koi claim nahi, jo leave likhi hai uske alawa koi leave nahi, joining time nahi, interview ke liye koi TA/DA nahi, aur listed remuneration ke alawa koi benefit (accommodation sahit) nahi. Shortlisted candidates ko apne kharch par pre-engagement medical test karwana hota hai.
    • Fee: ₹600 UR/EWS/OBC ke liye (sirf online); SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen/Departmental candidates/Doob Kshetra/Project Affected candidates ko koi fee nahi.
    • Selection: sirf Personal Interview (100 marks, higher qualification ke liye 60% weightage component sahit); interview ke liye shortlist educational qualification aur experience ki merit par lagbhag 5x vacancies par; final selection interview performance par (qualifying 60 marks UR/EWS, 40 marks SC/ST/OBC/PwBD).
    • Photo/signature aur document specs (notice): safed background par recent passport-size colour photograph, maximum 50 KB (JPG/PNG/PDF); safed background par kaali syahi se signature, maximum 20 KB (JPG/PNG/PDF); Xth/XIIth certificates 125 KB; Master's certificates 1 MB; caste/PwBD/experience documents jaise list me likha hai (125 KB-2 MB).
    • Upar ke saare facts Advt. No. 09/2026 (thdc.co.in) se liye gaye hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Sirf verified THDC portal (recruitment.thdcil.in) par apply karen — Job Advertisement dropdown me Adv-09/2026 select karke Show par click karen.
    • Post-wise qualification aur experience check karen — Law ko 1 saal ka experience, Safety ko Industrial Safety me PG Diploma + 1 saal, Structural/Geo-Technical ko M.Tech. chahiye, koi experience nahi.
    • Form shuru karne se pehle photo (maximum 50 KB) aur signature (maximum 20 KB) ready rakhen.
    • Apply karne se pehle thdc.co.in par Advt. No. 09/2026 poori padhen.

    यह मत करो

    • Ise permanent job mat samjhen — engagement sirf fixed-tenure basis par hai, 1 saal, har saal extend ho sakta hai, maximum 5 saal, regularization ka koi claim nahi.
    • Interview ke liye TA/DA, joining time, leave (jo likha hai uske alawa) ya accommodation ki ummeed mat karen — listed remuneration ke alawa koi benefit nahi.
    • Last date miss mat karen — online applications 24 December 2026 shaam 06:00 baje band ho jati hain.
    • Pre-engagement medical test mat bhoolen — shortlisted candidates ko apne kharch par ye test karwana hota hai.
