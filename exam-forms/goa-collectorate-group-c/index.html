    Eligibility & fees

    • 94 regular Group C posts — Upper Division Clerk (North Goa) 11, Lower Division Clerk (North Goa) 33, Lower Division Clerk (South Goa) 41, Multi Tasking Staff (North Goa) 4, Multi Tasking Staff (South Goa) 5. The advertisement is No. 95/3/2026/Recruitment/COM/CAB, dated 10 November 2026.
    • Upper Division Clerk: Bachelor's degree from a recognised university + 1 year experience in administrative work + minimum 6-month certificate course in computers + knowledge of Konkani. Lower Division Clerk: Higher Secondary School Certificate (12th) + computer knowledge with English typing speed of 30 wpm + at least 1 year working experience + knowledge of Konkani. Multi Tasking Staff: Secondary School Certificate (10th) or an ITI certificate course in the relevant trade + at least 1 year working experience + knowledge of Konkani. Knowledge of Marathi is desirable for all posts.
    • Knowledge of Konkani is essential for every post, and a 15-year residence certificate in Goa (issued by the competent authority) is required. The Employment Exchange registration card is among the documents to enclose.
    • Age: maximum 45 years as on 27 November 2026 (the last date). Relaxation: SC/ST +5 years, OBC +3 years, PwD +10 years (PwD-SC/ST +15, PwD-OBC +13), ex-servicemen and government servants as per rules; overall maximum 56 years for all categories. PwD candidates are exempted from the essential experience requirement.
    • Selection: objective-type written test — Post-specific Subject Knowledge 25%, General Knowledge, Reasoning, Mathematics and Basic Computer Awareness 60%, Konkani Language 15% — then a merit list on written-test marks, a skill test wherever applicable for merit-listed candidates, and verification of essential documents. The syllabus will be available by 16 November 2026 on itggoa.formsubmit.in, northgoa.gov.in and southgoa.nic.in.
    • Pay: Upper Division Clerk Level 4, Lower Division Clerk Level 2, Multi Tasking Staff Level 1. File a separate application for each category of post you apply for. No documents need to be furnished at the time of applying — shortlisted candidates found ineligible at document verification will not be considered.
    • UR ₹600, OBC ₹300, EWS ₹300, SC/ST ₹200, PwD nil. Pay online only — Internet Banking, Debit Card, Credit Card or UPI; no physical payment is accepted.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only on itggoa.formsubmit.in — read the general instructions on the same portal first.
    • File a separate application for each post category you apply for.
    • Keep your 15-year Goa residence certificate ready.
    • Check the syllabus when it is published (by 16 November 2026) — Konkani carries 15% weightage.
    • Pay the fee online only and submit by 27 November 2026.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply without knowledge of Konkani — it is essential for every post.
    • Do not apply without a valid OBC certificate and expect OBC benefits — without it, apply under UR.
    • Do not file one combined application for two post categories — each needs its own application.
    • Do not wait for a correction window — the advertisement announces none.
