    Eligibility & fees

    • Sports qualification: the minimum sports achievement prescribed for your level, earned on or after 1 April 2024 in a championship conducted by a federation recognised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports (Level 4/5: represented India at the Olympics (Senior), or at least 3rd position in World Cup / World Championships / Asian Games / Commonwealth Games or other listed events; Level 2/3 and Level 1 norms are given game-wise in the notice).
    • Education (must be held on the date of application; candidates appearing or awaiting results are not eligible): Level 4/5 — a university degree or equivalent; Level 2/3 — 12th pass, or Matriculation + Course Completed Act Apprenticeship, or 10th pass + ITI / equivalent / NAC from NCVT; Level 1 — 10th pass or ITI or equivalent, or NAC from NCVT.
    • Age: 18 to 25 years as on 1 January 2027 (date of birth between 02.01.2002 and 01.01.2009). No age relaxation for any category.
    • Only one application is allowed. No post is reserved for SC/ST/OBC/EWS — selection is on merit within each sports discipline.
    • Read Employment Notice No. SER/Pers/Sports Quota/OA/2026-27 fully on rrcser.co.in before applying.
    • UR/OBC ₹500 — ₹400 is refunded (after bank charges) if you actually appear in the sports trial.
    • SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/PwBD/Women/Minorities/EBC ₹250 — the full ₹250 is refunded if you appear in the trial.
    • Pay online only (Internet Banking / Debit Card / Credit Card). Cash, cheque, money order or demand draft is not accepted.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 54 posts against Sports Quota (Open Advertisement) 2026-27 in units of South Eastern Railway: Group C Level 4/5 — 5 posts, Group C Level 2/3 — 16 posts, Group D Level 1 — 33 posts. Employment Notice No. SER/Pers/Sports Quota/OA/2026-27 dated 26.10.2026.
    • Disciplines — Level 4/5: Athletics (Men), Chess (Men), Hockey (Women), Table Tennis (Women). Level 2/3: Athletics (Men), Archery (Men and Women), Body Building (Men), Bridge (Men), Cycling (Men), Gymnastics (Women), Hockey (Men and Women), Rifle Shooting (Women). Level 1: Ball Badminton (Men), Cricket (Men), Football (Men), Golf (Men), Kabaddi (Men and Women), Kho Kho (Men), Volleyball (Men and Women), Water Polo (Men), Weight Lifting (Men). Vacancies are fixed discipline-wise and position-wise in the notice — check your game before applying.
    • Pay: PB-1 ₹5,200–20,200 with Grade Pay ₹2,800/₹2,400 (Level 5/4), ₹2,000/₹1,900 (Level 3/2) and ₹1,800 (Level 1) — 7th CPC Pay Levels 1 to 5.
    • Selection: merit list out of 100 marks for each discipline — recognised sports achievement 50 marks, sports trial (game skill, physical fitness and coach's observation) 40 marks, educational qualification 10 marks. Minimum qualifying marks: Level 4/5 — 70, Level 2/3 — 65, Level 1 — 60; a trial score below 25 out of 40 does not qualify. Followed by certificate verification and medical examination.
    • Sports achievements count only if earned on or after 01.04.2024. Volleyball certificates from events of the Volleyball Federation of India or its affiliated units held up to 28.10.2025 and after 21.04.2026 are not considered (National Championship certificates from 29.10.2025 to 21.04.2026 are valid); Body Building results from Indian Body Builders Federation events are not considered.
    • Trial dates and venues will be announced only on rrcser.co.in. SC/ST candidates get free second-class rail travel to attend the trials. Selected candidates must serve the Railways for 5 years (service bond).
    • All facts above are taken from Employment Notice No. SER/Pers/Sports Quota/OA/2026-27 of South Eastern Railway (rrcser.co.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check the sports norms for your level and discipline in the notice before paying the fee — the achievement bar decides eligibility.
    • Apply only through the RRC SER website link (rrcser.co.in > Online/E-Application for Sports Quota (OA)/2026-27).
    • Keep your Matric certificate details exact — name, father's name, community and date of birth must match it.
    • Carry originals of date of birth, educational and sports certificates and a photo ID on the trial day.

    Avoid this

    • Do not submit more than one application — multiple applications are invalid.
    • Do not pay by cash, cheque, money order or demand draft — only online payment is accepted.
    • Do not sign in capital or disjointed letters, and do not leave any column blank — such forms are rejected.
    • Do not wait for 30 November — no change of any kind is allowed after final submission.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Recent colour photograph 3.5 cm × 3.5 cm (JPG, up to 70 KB)
    • Signature on white paper (JPG, 10–40 KB)
    • Left thumb impression on white paper (JPG, 10–40 KB)
    • Matric certificate — name, father's name and date of birth in the form must match it
    • Educational certificate for the level applied (degree / 12th / ITI / NAC)
    • Sports achievement certificates (earned on or after 01.04.2024)
    • Community / EBC / minority certificate and annexures, if claiming the fee concession

    Documents you will need

    • Matriculation (10th) certificate — date of birth proof
    • Educational qualification certificate for your level (degree / 12th / ITI / NAC)
    • Sports achievement certificates (earned on or after 01.04.2024)
    • Community certificate (SC/ST/OBC), if applicable — OBC in Central Government format with Non-Creamy Layer
    • Photo, signature and left thumb impression scans

    How to apply — step by step

    1. rrcser.co.in par Employment Notice No. SER/Pers/Sports Quota/OA/2026-27 पूरा पढ़ें — apne game ke sports norms aur discipline-wise vacancy pehle check करें।
    2. rrcser.co.in par 'Online/E-Application for Sports Quota (OA) / 2026-27' link खोलें — iroams.com par RRC SER Sports Quota 2026 ka registration page khulega।
    3. 'New Registration' par naam, pita ka naam, janm tithi, community, email aur mobile bharen aur registration number/password note kar len।
    4. Login karke form bharen, apna discipline/position chunein, photo (3.5 cm × 3.5 cm, JPG, 70 KB tak), signature aur left thumb impression (JPG, 10–40 KB) aur certificates upload करें।
    5. Fee online bharen (UR/OBC ₹500; SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/PwBD/Women/Minorities/EBC ₹250) aur form 30 November 2026 (raat 11:59) se pehle final submit karke printout le len — submit ke baad koi change nahi hoga।
