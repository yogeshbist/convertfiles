    Eligibility & fees

    • Full-time first-class B.E./B.Tech in the relevant engineering discipline (varies by position code 101–137); M.E./M.Tech desirable.
    • Post-qualification experience: Engineer 2 years, Assistant Manager 4 years, Manager 8 years, Senior Manager 11 years, AGM 13 years, DGM 16 years, General Manager 19 years (Assistant Manager-Medical: nil).
    • Upper age as on 3 December 2026: Engineer 29, Assistant Manager 30 (Medical 38), Manager 34, Senior Manager 39, AGM 42, DGM 45, General Manager 48 (relaxation as per rules).
    • Non-engineering posts differ by code — check the notification table for your position code. Read Advt KP/S/18/2026 fully on bemlindia.in before applying.
    • ₹500 non-refundable for General/EWS/OBC candidates, payable online at the end of the form.
    • No fee for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 83 posts by grade: Engineer (Grade II) 56 (UR 24, SC 9, ST 4, OBC 14, EWS 5); Assistant Manager (Grade III) 10 (UR 5, SC 1, ST 1, OBC 2, EWS 1); Manager (Grade IV) 7 (UR 5, SC 1, OBC 1); Senior Manager (Grade V) 1 (UR 1); Assistant General Manager (Grade VI) 5 (UR 4, OBC 1); Deputy General Manager (Grade VII) 3 (UR 2, SC 1); General Manager (Grade VIII) 1 (UR 1). One of the 3 posts for position code 118 (Assistant Manager) is a backlog vacancy for ST. Positions are listed by code 101–137 in the notification.
    • Age / experience / pay (upper age as on 3 December 2026): Grade II Engineer — 2 years' post-qualification experience, max age 29, ₹40,000–1,40,000; Grade III Assistant Manager — 4 years, max age 30, ₹50,000–1,60,000 (Assistant Manager-Medical, code 123: experience nil, max age 38, same scale); Grade IV Manager — 8 years, max age 34, ₹60,000–1,80,000; Grade V Senior Manager — 11 years, max age 39, ₹70,000–2,00,000; Grade VI AGM — 13 years, max age 42, ₹80,000–2,20,000; Grade VII DGM — 16 years, max age 45, ₹90,000–2,40,000; Grade VIII GM — 19 years, max age 48, ₹1,00,000–2,60,000.
    • Age relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC-NCL 3 years, PwD additional 10 years over and above the applicable relaxation (minimum 40% disability); upper age may also be relaxed by equivalent years of excess post-qualification experience, subject to a maximum age of 57 years with relaxation.
    • Qualification: most engineering posts require a full-time First-Class Degree in the relevant engineering discipline from a recognised University/Institution (discipline varies by position code), with M.E./M.Tech desirable. Examples from the notification: General Manager (code 101, Project Management) — First-Class Degree in Mechanical/Electrical/Electronics Engineering, minimum 19 years' experience including 10 years in Rolling Stock metro contracts; Manager R&D TCMS (code 116) — First-Class Degree in Electronics & Communication/Electrical & Electronics/Instrumentation/Computer Science, minimum 8 years' experience; Engineer Grade II (codes 125, 128, 129, 132, 133, 135) — First-Class Degree in the relevant discipline, minimum 2 years' experience. Non-engineering posts (e.g. Assistant Manager-Medical) differ by code — check the notification table for your code.
    • Besides basic pay: Variable Dearness Allowance, perquisites/allowances (currently 15.12% of applicable basic pay under the Cafeteria System), company accommodation/HRA, PF, gratuity and PRP as per company rules. Posting across BEML units in India (Bengaluru, Mysore, K.G.F. and others).
    • Selection: eligible candidates are shortlisted for 'assessment'; based on the assessment, shortlisted candidates undergo pre-employment medical examination and original-document verification. No written-exam pattern or syllabus is given in the notification.
    • Dates: advertisement dated 16 October 2026; online applications close 3 December 2026 at 6:00 PM. Apply for only ONE position. Helpline: 9205114492, helpdesk.beml@gmail.com (10:00 AM–6:00 PM, Monday–Saturday).
    • All facts above are from Advertisement No. KP/S/18/2026, BEML (31-page notification, bemlindia.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advertisement No. KP/S/18/2026 fully on bemlindia.in — check the exact discipline, experience and age for your position code (101–137).
    • Apply only through the official BEML careers page (bemlindia.in) before 3 December 2026, 6:00 PM — and for only ONE position.
    • Keep your degree, experience certificates and photo/signature scans ready before starting the form.
    • Pay the ₹500 fee online at the end of the form (Gen/EWS/OBC; SC/ST/PwD exempt) and save the application printout.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for more than one position — multiple applications are not allowed.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes at 6:00 PM on 3 December 2026.
    • Do not apply with a part-time or distance degree where the notification asks for a full-time first-class degree.
    • Do not assume a written exam — selection is by shortlisting for assessment, then medical examination and document verification.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Degree certificate(s) and mark sheets (B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech if any)
    • Post-qualification experience certificate(s) — 2 to 19 years as per grade
    • Date of birth proof and ID proof
    • Caste certificate (OBC-NCL / SC / ST / EWS) or PwD certificate, if applicable
    • Recent passport-size photo and signature scans
    • Valid email ID and mobile number
