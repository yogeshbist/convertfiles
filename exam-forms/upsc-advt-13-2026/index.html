    Eligibility & fees

    • Dental Surgeon (5): A recognised Bachelor of Dental Surgery degree (under the Dentists Act, 1948) or an equivalent recognised dental qualification; registration with any State Dental Council or Tribunal in India; completion of compulsory rotating internship (candidates who have not yet completed it may still appear, but must complete it before appointment if selected). Maximum age: 35 years (UR), 35 years (EWS), 40 years (SC) as on 4 December 2026.
    • Ayurveda Physician (2): Degree in Ayurveda of a recognised University / Statutory State Board / Council / Faculty of Indian Systems of Medicine recognised under the Indian Medicine Central Council Act, 1970; enrolment on the Central or State Register for Indian Medicine. Maximum age: 35 years (UR), 35 years (EWS) as on 4 December 2026.
    • Homeopathic Physician (1): Degree in Homoeopathy of a recognised University / Statutory State Board / Council or equivalent recognised under the Homoeopathic Central Council Act, 1973; enrolment on the Central Register of Homoeopathy or a State Register of Homoeopathy. Maximum age: 35 years (EWS) as on 4 December 2026.
    • Scientist B (5): Master's Degree in Botany or Horticulture or Chemistry (Organic) from a recognised University or Institute. Maximum age: 35 years (UR), 35 years (EWS), 38 years (OBC), 40 years (SC) — counted as on 3 July 2026, the original closing date of Advertisement No. 06/2026, not 4 December 2026.
    • Age is further relaxable up to 10 years for PwBD candidates.
    • ₹25 application fee — pay by cash in any SBI branch, SBI net banking, or Visa/Master credit/debit card, by 4 December 2026 (6 PM).
    • No fee for SC/ST/PwBD/Women candidates of any community. OBC/EWS/Unreserved male candidates must pay the full fee — no exemption is available to them.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 13 Group A posts: Dental Surgeon 5 (Department of Health & Family Welfare; UR 3, EWS 1, SC 1), Ayurveda Physician 2 (New Delhi Municipal Council; UR 1, EWS 1), Homeopathic Physician 1 (New Delhi Municipal Council; EWS 1), Scientist B 5 (Botanical Survey of India; MSc Botany 3, MSc Horticulture 1, MSc Chemistry (Organic) 1).
    • Apply online 14 November–4 December 2026 (till 6 PM) at upsconline.nic.in. The last date for printing a completely submitted online application is 5 December 2026 (till 6 PM).
    • NO written examination — selection is by shortlisting followed by interview.
    • Pay: Level 10 (₹56,100 entry) for all four posts.
    • Age is counted as on the closing date, 4 December 2026 — EXCEPT for Scientist B, where the original closing date of 3 July 2026 applies (see eligibility).
    • Scientist B is a re-advertisement of Advertisement No. 06/2026 (closing date 3 July 2026); candidates who applied earlier should check the new advertisement for the fresh terms.
    • All facts above are taken from the official UPSC PDF of Advertisement No. 13/2026 (28 pages).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advertisement No. 13/2026 fully on upsc.gov.in before applying — the age-counting date differs for Scientist B.
    • Apply only at upsconline.nic.in before 4 December 2026 (6 PM).
    • Scientist B candidates: note that age is counted as on 3 July 2026, not the new closing date.
    • Keep your degree certificate, registration certificate (Dental/Ayurveda/Homoeopathy) and internship completion proof ready.

    Avoid this

    • Do not expect a written examination — selection is by shortlisting and interview.
    • Do not apply for Scientist B using the 4 December 2026 date for your age — the advertisement fixes 3 July 2026 for that post.
    • Do not pay the fee if you are SC/ST/PwBD or a woman candidate — you are exempt; OBC/EWS/Unreserved male candidates must pay ₹25.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes at 6 PM on 4 December 2026.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Registration certificate (State Dental Council / Indian Medicine Register / Homoeopathy Register), as applicable

    • Caste / PwBD certificate, if applicable
    • Recent colour photograph and signature scan

    How to apply — step by step

    1. upsc.gov.in पर Advertisement No. 13/2026 पढ़ें और अपनी post चुनें।
    2. upsconline.nic.in पर Online Recruitment Application (ORA) खोलें।
    3. Form भरें, photo, signature और documents upload करें।
    4. ₹25 fee भरें (SBI cash / net banking / Visa-Master card) — SC/ST/PwBD/महिलाओं को छूट — 4 दिसंबर 2026 शाम 6 बजे तक।
    5. Form submit करें और submitted application का printout 5 दिसंबर 2026 शाम 6 बजे तक निकाल लें।
