    Check first: These are NOT regular government posts: all 15 teacher posts are scheme-based, temporary engagements for five years on a fixed honorarium of ₹30,000 per month (payable for 10 months in an academic year).     Eligibility & fees

    • 2,423 posts in 15 subjects (post codes 26046–26060): English 187, Mathematics 281, Hindi 187, Political Science 156, History 156, Economics 156, plus Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Sanskrit, Computer, Music, Commerce, Physical Education and Fine Arts. The total is the sum of the subject-wise figures in the notice and is tentative.
    • Most subjects need a Master's degree in the subject (50%) with B.Ed (50%) and TET passed; English and Mathematics follow their own pattern (55% in the subject at graduation). All qualifications must be held by 5 December 2026.
    • Age: 18–45 years as on 1 January 2026. The notice prints this as a single limit for everyone — it does not list category-wise age relaxation.
    • One Time Registration (OTR) on the HPRCA portal is compulsory before the online application, and you must be able to teach in Hindi and English.
    • Selection: a single Computer Based Test / written screening test — 120 MCQ, 120 marks, 1½ hours. Qualifying marks: 45% for General category and 40% for SC/ST/OBC/Wards of Freedom Fighters. The notice prescribes no negative marking.
    • Examination fee ₹100 + processing fee ₹700 = ₹800 for every candidate — there is no category exemption.
    • Correction, when the window opens, costs ₹100. The fee once paid is neither refunded nor held for another examination.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: latest passport-size photograph, not older than one month; JPG, JPEG or PNG; maximum 1 MB.
    • Signature: JPG or JPEG only; maximum 1 MB.
    • The notice fixes no pixel dimensions, DPI or background colour for these uploads — only the formats and the 1 MB cap above.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Use a photograph taken within the last month — an older photo breaks the notice rule
    • Keep both files well under the 1 MB cap; compress them here if a scan is heavier
    • Copy your name, father's/mother's name and date of birth exactly from your Matric certificate
    • Complete the OTR first, then fill the application — and submit only one application per post

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply thinking this is a regular teaching post — it is a five-year scheme engagement with no regularisation right
    • Do not submit more than one application for the same post — it is liable to be rejected
    • Do not miss the correction window — it opens only for four days, three days after the closing date, and the category cannot be changed once it closes
    • Do not upload the signature as a PNG — the notice accepts only JPG/JPEG for the signature

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Applying without completing the OTR — the application cannot be filled without it
    • Using a photograph older than one month
    • Assuming a fee concession — every candidate pays ₹800, whatever the category
    • Treating the engagement as a regular post and missing that there is no regularisation right

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Complete your One Time Registration (OTR) on hprca.hp.gov.in
    2. From 10 November 2026 (10:00 AM), open the Advt 14/2026 application for your subject (post codes 26046-26060)
    3. Fill in details exactly as in your Matric certificate and upload the photo (max 1 MB) and signature (JPG/JPEG, max 1 MB)
    4. Pay ₹800 online (₹100 examination fee + ₹700 processing fee)
    5. Submit by 5 December 2026 (11:59 PM) and save the application copy
    6. If you make a mistake, use the four-day correction window that opens three days after the closing date — the correction fee is ₹100

    Questions

    What is the last date to apply for HPRCA Teacher Recruitment?
    The online form closes on 5 December 2026. The fee can be paid till 5 December 2026.
    What size should the photo and signature be?
    Photo: latest passport-size photograph, not older than one month; JPG, JPEG or PNG; maximum 1 MB. Signature: JPG or JPEG only; maximum 1 MB.
    Are these regular government teacher posts?
    No. All 15 posts are scheme-based temporary engagements for five years at a fixed ₹30,000 per month (payable for 10 months in an academic year). The notice says there is no right to regularisation and the teachers are not deemed government employees in any manner.
    Is there any fee concession for SC/ST or women candidates?
    No. Every candidate pays ₹100 examination fee + ₹700 processing fee = ₹800, and reserved-category candidates of other states are treated as General. The fee is not refunded.
    Can I correct the application after submitting?
    Yes — the notice opens a correction window for four days, starting three days after the closing date, at a fee of ₹100. On that wording it falls around 8–11 December 2026; the exact dates will show on the portal. Category cannot be changed after the window closes.
