    Eligibility & fees

    • Posts by category: UR 123, ST 67, SEBC 26, SC 22 — District Cadre Group-C posts under the Health & Family Welfare Department, spread over 29 districts.
    • Education: +2 Science, plus the 2-year COA or COSA course from a Government or Government-recognised institution of Odisha, and valid registration with the Ophthalmic Officers' Council, Odisha. Note: a Diploma or B.Sc in Optometry is NOT the qualifying qualification for this post under the detailed advertisement.
    • Language: the candidate must be able to read, write and speak Odia as prescribed.
    • Age 21–42 years as on 5 November 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST/SEBC/Women +5 years, PwD +10 years.
    • Selection: a written test of 100 marks — COA/COSA theory 60, practical skills 25, arithmetic 10, English 5 — then document verification and district allocation.
    • Pay: Level-7, ₹25,500–81,100 under ORSP Rules 2017.
    • Two deadlines apply: registration/re-registration closes on 29 November 2026, and the filled form must be finally submitted by 5 December 2026.
    • ₹500 examination fee, paid online.
    • SC, ST and PwD candidates are exempt from the fee.
    • The fee is refunded to candidates who actually appear in the written test.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The advertisement does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature — upload them in the format and size the portal asks for on the upload screen.
    • Keep a recent colour photograph and a clear signature scan (signed on white paper) ready before you start.
    • Upload only clear, readable scans; a blurred photo or signature can get the form rejected.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Register or re-register on osssc.gov.in by 29 November 2026 — the final submission date of 5 December does not extend registration.
    • Check your Ophthalmic Officers' Council, Odisha registration is valid before applying.
    • Apply only if your qualification is the COA/COSA course — optometry degrees/diplomas are not the prescribed qualification here.
    • Complete the final submission by 5 December 2026 and save the submitted copy.
    • Appear in the written test to become eligible for the fee refund.

    Avoid this

    • Do not confuse the two dates — registration ends 29 November, final submission 5 December.
    • Do not apply with a B.Sc/Diploma in Optometry assuming it is equivalent; the detailed advt prescribes COA/COSA.
    • Do not let an expired council registration spoil the application.
    • Do not apply on any site other than osssc.gov.in.
    • Do not skip the exam if you paid the fee — the refund is only for those who appear.

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Waiting until 5 December to register — registration itself closes on 29 November 2026.
    • Applying on the strength of an Optometry degree/diploma, which the detailed advertisement does not accept for this post.
    • Applying with a lapsed Ophthalmic Officers' Council registration.
    • Missing that the written test includes arithmetic and English along with COA/COSA subjects.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open osssc.gov.in and read the detailed Advt IIE-103/2026 fully.
    2. Register (or re-register) on the portal by 29 November 2026.
    3. Fill the application with your COA/COSA and council-registration details.
    4. Upload photo, signature and certificates, and pay the ₹500 fee online if applicable.
    5. Finally submit the form by 5 December 2026 and save the submitted copy.
    6. Prepare for the 100-mark written test as per the syllabus in the advertisement.
