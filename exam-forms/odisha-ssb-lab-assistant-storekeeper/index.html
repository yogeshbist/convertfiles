    Eligibility & fees

    • 435 vacancies — 57 in Government Degree Colleges and 378 in Non-Government Aided Colleges. Group-B, Level-9 (ORSP 2017).
    • Education: a Bachelor's degree with Honours in the relevant practical subject from a recognised university.
    • You must be a resident of Odisha and able to speak, read and write Odia (Odia as a subject in HSC, or the ME-standard Odia test).
    • Age: 21–42 years as on 1 January 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST/SEBC/women/ex-servicemen +5 years, PwD +10 years.
    • Selection: a single written test only — 100 multiple-choice questions, 150 marks, 2 hours, +1.5 for a correct answer and −0.5 for a wrong one. Qualifying marks: 40% for UR, 30% for SC/ST/SEBC/PwD. There is no interview.
    • Apply online only on ssbodisha.ac.in from 12 October 2026 (2:30 PM) to 16 November 2026 (11:45 PM). There is no correction window — no change is possible after final submission.
    • Application fee: ₹500 for all candidates except SC/ST/PwD, who pay ₹200. Pay online only — the fee is non-refundable.
    • The fee must be paid by 16 November 2026, 11:45 PM, along with the final submission.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Upload a latest passport-size photo taken within the last 15 days, a scanned signature and a left-hand thumb impression.
    • The sources read do not print fixed KB or pixel sizes for the photo and signature — upload them in the format and size the ssbodisha.ac.in portal asks for.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check that your Honours subject matches the practical subject of the post before applying.
    • Keep your Odia language proof ready — it is a compulsory requirement.
    • Upload a photo taken within the last 15 days, not an old one.
    • Pay the fee (₹500, or ₹200 for SC/ST/PwD) online before the final submit.
    • Submit before 16 November 2026, 11:45 PM — there is no correction window after that.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply without an Honours degree in the relevant subject — a plain pass degree does not qualify.
    • Do not expect any correction window — the form is final once submitted.
    • Do not use an old photograph — it must be taken within 15 days.
    • Do not wait for the last hours — the portal closes at 11:45 PM on 16 November 2026.
