    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • Engineer Trainee: full-time Bachelor's Degree in Engineering/Technology OR 5-year integrated Master's/Dual Degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil or IT/CS. No minimum percentage is stated for the engineering degree.
    • Executive Trainee (HR): full-time regular Bachelor's with at least 60% aggregate + 2-year full-time regular PG Degree/Diploma in HR/Personnel Management/Industrial Relations/Social Work or MBA with at least 55% aggregate (Personnel Management/Labour Welfare/HRM specialisation/elective in the final year for Social Work/MBA).
    • Executive Trainee (Finance): CA or CMA qualified.
    • Age as on 01/11/2026: Engineer Trainee 27 years (29 years with a two-year full-time PG in Engineering or Business Administration/Management); Executive Trainee (HR and Finance) 29 years.
    • Final-year students may apply but must produce proof of qualification at document verification (final degree by 1 March 2027 or at interview, whichever is earlier). AMIE is not eligible; B.Arch is not eligible for Civil ET.
    • ₹1,072 total for UR/EWS/OBC candidates (₹600 examination fee + ₹400 processing fee + GST) — non-refundable, pay online with the application by 28 December 2026.
    • ₹472 total for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates (examination fee NIL + ₹400 processing fee + GST). Bank charges extra.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 200 posts — Engineer Trainee 160 (Mechanical 80, Electrical 30, Electronics 25, Civil 15, IT/CS 10) and Executive Trainee 40 (HR 20, Finance 20). Advt. 02/2026. Regular recruitment with 1-year training and absorption thereafter.
    • Vacancy break-up (UR/EWS/OBC/SC/ST): Mechanical 80 (32/8/24/12/4; PwD 3), Electrical 30 (13/3/8/4/2; PwD 2), Electronics 25 (11/2/6/4/2; PwD 1), Civil 15 (6/2/4/2/1), HR 20 (8/2/5/3/2; PwD 1), Finance 20 (8/2/5/3/2; PwD 1), IT/CS 10 (4/1/3/1/1). Total 200 (82/20/55/29/14; PwD 8). Vacancies are tentative and may vary.
    • Dates: online application 25 November 2026 (10:00 AM) to 28 December 2026 (11:45 PM). Date of Examination 27 January 2027 (tentative — may change).
    • Fee: UR/EWS/OBC ₹1,072 (₹600 exam fee + ₹400 processing fee + GST); SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen ₹472 (exam fee NIL + ₹400 processing fee + GST). Non-refundable; bank charges extra. No separate fee last date — fee is paid with the online application.
    • Age as on 01/11/2026: Engineer Trainee 27 years (29 years with a two-year full-time PG in Engineering or Business Administration/Management); Executive Trainee (HR and Finance) 29 years. Relaxations: SC/ST 5 years, OBC-NCL 3 years, PwD 10 years (UR/EWS), 13 years (OBC), 15 years (SC/ST), Ex-Servicemen per Govt. rules, J&K domicile (01/01/1980–31/12/1989) 5 years.
    • Qualification: Engineer Trainee — full-time Bachelor's in Engineering/Technology OR 5-year integrated Master's/Dual Degree in the relevant discipline (no minimum percentage stated). Executive Trainee (HR) — Bachelor's 60% + 2-year full-time PG in HR/Personnel Management/Industrial Relations/Social Work or MBA 55% (HR specialisation/elective in final year). Executive Trainee (Finance) — CA or CMA. Final-year students eligible (degree by 1 March 2027 or at interview). AMIE not eligible; B.Arch not eligible for Civil ET.
    • Pay: 1-year training, basic pay ₹50,000 in the scale ₹50,000–1,60,000. After absorption as Engineer/Executive, scale ₹60,000–1,80,000 with basic ₹60,000; approx. CTC ₹12.0 lakh per annum, plus DA, perks and allowances, leave, medical (self and dependent family), PF, gratuity, uniform, company accommodation or HRA.
    • Selection: Computer Based Examination (CBE) — minimum qualifying marks 40% (UR/EWS), 30% (OBC-NCL/SC/ST); PwD candidates get up to 25% relaxation below the cut-off (floor 30% UR/EWS, 22.5% OBC/SC/ST). Shortlist for interview 1:5 per discipline/category. Final merit: 75% CBE + 25% interview. CBE duration, number of questions and negative marking are NOT stated in the notification.
    • Service bond: ₹5,00,000 to serve 3 years excluding the training period, plus a stamped indemnity bond by a surety. Posting places: Bhopal, Haridwar, Hyderabad, Jhansi, Tiruchirappalli, Ranipet, Bengaluru and Project Sites. Selected candidates must serve at the allocated posting for at least 10 years; no transfer in the first 10 years except on administrative grounds.
    • Apply online only through the verified BHEL ET/ST form (DigiALM/TCS iON) — one common form for Advt. 02/2026 and Advt. 03/2026, verified live on 25 November 2026 at 10:57 AM IST. The CBE for ET and ST in the same discipline is held on the same day, so a candidate applying for both must choose one. Candidates already selected as ET/ST/Artisan in BHEL are not eligible.
    • Photo/signature upload specifications are NOT stated in the detailed advertisement or the official FAQ — they appear only in the online application form.
    • All facts above are from Advt. 02/2026 and the official FAQ (careers.bhel.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply only on the verified BHEL ET/ST form (DigiALM/TCS iON) — register with your name exactly as in the Matriculation/10th certificate, verify with OTP, and note the Login ID and Password sent to your email and mobile.
    • Read Advt. 02/2026 fully before choosing a discipline — Engineer Trainee (Mechanical 80, Electrical 30, Electronics 25, Civil 15, IT/CS 10) or Executive Trainee (HR 20, Finance 20).
    • Choose up to 5 test-centre preferences carefully — BHEL may add or cancel centres.
    • Keep the final printout and fee receipt safe; check the answer key and response sheet within 5 days after the CBE.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for both ET and ST in the same discipline — the CBE for both is held on the same day, so you must choose one.
    • Do not submit a second application after a successful fee transaction — it is liable to rejection.
    • Do not expect a transfer in the first 10 years — selected candidates must serve at the allocated posting for at least 10 years.
    • Do not forget the service bond — ₹5,00,000 to serve 3 years excluding the training period.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Degree / PG / CA / CMA certificate and mark sheets (as applicable to the post)
    • Caste/category certificate, if applicable (OBC-NCL only for OBC benefit)
    • PwBD certificate (at least 40% relevant disability), if applicable
    • Recent passport-size photo and signature scans
    • Fee payment proof (₹1,072; ₹472 for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen)
    • Matriculation/10th certificate (name must match it exactly)

    How to apply — step by step

    1. careers.bhel.in par Advt. 02/2026 padhen — last date 28 December 2026 (11:45 PM).
    2. Discipline chunen (Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Civil, IT/CS, HR ya Finance) aur uski qualification aur age check karen.
    3. Verified BHEL ET/ST form par REGISTER karen — Post Name dropdown se post chunen, naam Matriculation/10th certificate ke hisaab se likhen, mobile aur email OTP se verify karen.
    4. Login ID aur Password (email/mobile par aayega) se login karke form bharen aur fee pay karen (₹1,072; SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen ₹472).
    5. CBE ki taiyari karen — exam 27 January 2027 (tentative); answer key aur response sheet CBE ke 5 din baad tak milegi.
