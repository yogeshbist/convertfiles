    Eligibility & fees

    • 138 contractual posts — Block Technology Manager (BTM) 69 and Assistant Technology Manager (ATM) 69 — under the Centrally Sponsored Sub-Mission on Agriculture Extension (ATMA Scheme). One-third (23) of the posts in each cadre are reserved for women.
    • BTM: Post Graduate in Agriculture / Allied sector with computer skills. ATM: Graduate in Agriculture / Allied sector. The notice mentions no experience requirement for either post.
    • Age: the maximum age limit is as per State Government Rules / Policy — the notice does not print the age in years or a cut-off date.
    • Selection: no written examination. Candidates are shortlisted for interview in a 3:1 ratio on the basis of their Academic Performance Indicator (API) score — API 70 marks + interview 30 marks, total 100. The interview date will be notified later.
    • Pay (consolidated remuneration): BTM ₹30,000 per month, ATM ₹25,000 per month. No allowances are mentioned in the notice. The number of posts may increase or decrease.
    • Processing fee: ₹800 for UR candidates, ₹500 for ST/SC/OBC candidates, paid online with the application.
    • PwD candidates are exempt from the fee.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • The notice does not print a fixed KB size for the photo and signature in the sources read — upload them in the format and size the portal asks for.
    • The API score calculator is provided on the Department's website — use it to check your score before applying.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Calculate your API score on the Department website's calculator before applying — shortlisting is on API score.
    • Check the post-wise category break-up (UR 36, OBC 11, SC 1, ST 21 per post) before choosing.
    • Pay the processing fee online (UR ₹800, ST/SC/OBC ₹500, PwD exempt) with the form.
    • Submit before 26 November 2026.
    • Watch the Department website for the interview date — it will be notified later.

    Avoid this

    • Do not expect a written exam — there is none; selection is API score + interview.
    • Do not treat this as a permanent government job — it is contractual under the ATMA scheme.
    • Do not apply for BTM without a Post Graduate degree in Agriculture or an allied sector.
    • Do not assume an age limit in years — the notice leaves it to State Government Rules/Policy.

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Applying for BTM with only a graduation degree (PG is required)
    • Skipping the API score check — shortlisting is on API score alone
    • Treating a contractual ATMA post as a permanent government job
    • Missing the 26 November 2026 deadline

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open the online form on the Department of Agriculture, Manipur website (agrimanipur.mn.gov.in)
    2. Calculate your API score with the calculator on the website
    3. Fill the online form in the prescribed format
    4. Pay the processing fee online (UR ₹800, ST/SC/OBC ₹500, PwD exempt)
    5. Submit before 26 November 2026 and save the acknowledgement
