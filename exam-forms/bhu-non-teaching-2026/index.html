    Eligibility & fees

    • System Engineer (Group A, UR 1): M.E./M.Tech/M.S. (CS/IT/ECE) with 55% marks and 5 years' relevant experience, OR B.E./B.Tech (CS/IT/ECE), MCA or M.Sc Computer Science with 55% marks and 7 years' experience, OR M.Sc (CS/IT) with 55% marks and 8 years' experience — with experience in a computer centre (servers, storage, backup, Linux/Unix, databases, networks).
    • Maintenance Engineer (Junior) (Group A, OBC 1): B.E./B.Tech in Electronics & Communication or Computer Science & Engineering with 55% marks and 5 years' experience in computer network administration and hardware, including large UPS systems and central AC plants.
    • Computer Operator Gr-I (Group C, UR 2 + OBC 1): B.Sc Computer Science or BCA with 55% marks, OR B.Sc with 55% marks and PGDCA.
    • AC Operator (Group C, UR 2 + OBC 1): ITI (Refrigeration and Air Conditioning) from NCVT/SCVT and 2 years' experience in running and maintaining AC plants of over 500 TR, including VRV systems.
    • Laboratory Attendant (Group C, UR 1): 12th (Intermediate) with Science and a certificate course in computer hardware of at least 6 months.
    • Workshop Attendant (Group C, UR 1 + OBC 1): 10th pass and ITI in Electrician/Plumber/Fitter/Welder/Wireman from NCVT/SCVT with 1 year of experience in the trade.
    • Age (maximum, as on 25 November 2026): System Engineer 50 years, Maintenance Engineer (Junior) 40 years, all other posts 32 years. Age relaxation as per Government of India rules; no age bar for permanent BHU employees.
    • ₹1,000 for Group A posts and ₹500 for Group C posts (UR/EWS/OBC) — pay online by 25 November 2026.
    • No fee for SC/ST/PwBD/women candidates.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 6 posts: System Engineer 1, Maintenance Engineer (Junior) 1, Computer Operator Gr-I 3, AC Operator 3, Laboratory Attendant 1, Workshop Attendant 2.
    • Apply online 26 October–25 November 2026 (till 5 PM); then send the hard copy with all enclosures to the Registrar (Recruitment & Assessment Cell), Holkar House, BHU, Varanasi – 221005 by 30 November 2026 (5 PM).
    • Photo and signature: JPEG, each not more than 50 KB.
    • Selection: Written Test, then Skill Test, then Document Verification.
    • The same posts advertised in Advt. No. 20/2023-2024 and 06/2018-2019 are cancelled; those applicants can claim a fee refund as given in the notification (ar.rac@bhu.ac.in).
    • Pay: System Engineer Level 12 (₹78,800–2,09,200), Maintenance Engineer (Junior) Level 10 (₹56,100–1,77,500), Computer Operator Gr-I Level 5 (₹29,200–92,300), AC Operator Level 3 (₹21,700–69,100), Laboratory and Workshop Attendant Level 1 (₹18,000–56,900).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advt. No. 03/2026-2027 fully on bhu.ac.in before applying — check the post code (CC001 to CC006) for your post.
    • Register first on the BHU recruitment portal (Samarth), then fill every tab of the form.
    • Upload a passport-size photo and signature in JPEG, each under 50 KB.
    • After submission, send the hard copy with all enclosures to the Registrar (RAC), Holkar House, BHU, Varanasi – 221005 by 30 November 2026 (5 PM).

    Avoid this

    • Do not submit the online form without the hard copy — both are required.
    • Do not try to change the form after final submission — no change is allowed.
    • Do not apply for a post whose experience condition you do not meet — every post needs experience.
    • Employed candidates must apply through proper channel or attach a No Objection Certificate — do not skip this.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Degree / ITI / 12th certificate as per your post
    • Experience certificates for the required years
    • Date of birth proof
    • Caste certificate, if applicable
    • Passport-size photo (JPEG, under 50 KB) and signature (JPEG, under 50 KB)
    • No Objection Certificate, if employed

    Documents you will need

    • Educational qualification certificates (degree/ITI/12th as per post)
    • Experience certificates
    • Date of birth proof

    • Recent passport-size photo and signature (JPEG, under 50 KB each)
