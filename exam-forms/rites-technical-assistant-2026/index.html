    Eligibility & fees

    • Matriculation + 2-year ITI Draughtsman (Civil) course.
    • Minimum 6 years post-qualification experience, including 5 years in preparation of Bridge Drawings and railway alignment drawings and related structures; AutoCAD proficiency.
    • Age 36 years as on 15 December 2026 (relaxation as per the RITES notice).
    • Read the RITES notice Vc. Nos. RG/50/26 to RG/52/26 fully on rites.com before applying.
    • General/OBC ₹300 + taxes as applicable; EWS/SC/ST/PwD ₹100 + taxes as applicable, online only.
    • The 3 posts are all UR — no posts are reserved for SC/ST/PwD. The fee charged from SC/ST/PwD candidates is refunded on participation in the further selection process, after submission and scrutiny of the category certificate, deducting GST/bank charges.
    • Payment issues: 011-33557000, Ext. 13221, or pg.service@hdfc.bank.in.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 3 Technical Assistant (Draughtsman / Bridges & Railway alignment) posts, Civil discipline — all UR (UR 3; EWS, OBC (NCL), SC, ST nil). Pay ₹24,000–90,000, on regular basis.
    • Online applications 16 November–15 December 2026 on recruit.rites.com. Age, experience and all other eligibility criteria are reckoned as on 15 December 2026. One application for one vacancy — apply separately, with a separate fee, for each post code. Hard copies are NOT to be sent by post/courier.
    • Age: 36 years as on 15 December 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) 3 years, J&K Domicile 5 years, PwBD 10 years (in addition to category relaxation, where the post is identified suitable for PwBD — Locomotor disability and Hearing Impairment for Civil/Architecture), RITES regular/contract employees 5 years. The notice states no specific number of years for Ex-Servicemen.
    • Qualification and experience: Matriculation + 2-year ITI Draughtsman (Civil) course; minimum 6 years post-qualification experience, of which minimum 5 years in preparation of Bridge Drawings and railway alignment drawings and related structures; the candidate should be proficient in AutoCAD.
    • Selection: Written Test — 125 objective questions, 1 mark each, 2.5 hours, NO negative marking; PwBD candidates get 50 minutes compensatory time. Qualifying marks: UR/EWS 50%, SC/ST/OBC (NCL)/PwBD 45% (against reserved posts). Then Document Scrutiny of written-test qualifiers, initially in the ratio 1:3 of the vacancies notified; offers in order of merit, only to the extent of vacancies notified. Tie of marks: the older candidate is placed above the younger. Candidates may appear in Hindi or English. No interview stage is described for these posts in the notice.
    • Written Test centres (6): Delhi/Gurgaon, Kolkata, Bangalore, Mumbai, Guwahati, Bhubaneswar — give two centre preferences at application; RITES may allot a centre not preferred. All centres except Delhi/Gurgaon are tentative, depending on the number of candidates and RITES' discretion. Exact date, time and venue are communicated separately.
    • RG/51/26 and RG/52/26 Technician-I (Draughtsman) posts (5 posts) are separate vacancies under the same combined notice — this entry covers only RG/50/26 Technical Assistant (3 posts).
    • All facts above are taken from the RITES recruitment notice Vc. Nos. RG/50/26 to RG/52/26 (rites.com).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the RITES combined notice fully on rites.com before applying.
    • Apply only on recruit.rites.com — 'Online Registration' (frmRegistration.aspx) — and choose the correct vacancy code RG/50/26.
    • Keep your 6-year experience proof ready, with 5 years specifically in Bridge Drawings and railway alignment drawings.
    • Pay the fee online only (General/OBC ₹300 + taxes; EWS/SC/ST/PwD ₹100 + taxes) before 15 December 2026.
    • Download and save the final application form after payment — it is needed at selection time.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply for RG/50/26 if you do not have the 5 years of Bridge/railway-alignment drawing experience — it is essential.
    • Do not send any hard copy by post/courier — it is not accepted.
    • Do not confuse RG/50/26 Technical Assistant with RG/51/26 & RG/52/26 Technician-I (Draughtsman) — they are different posts with different qualifications.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes on 15 December 2026.
