    Eligibility & fees

    • 83 regular vacancies — Technical Assistant 30, Technician 'B' 41, Administrative Officer 2, Accounts Officer 1, Purchase & Stores Officer 2, Junior Personal Assistant 7.
    • Technical Assistant: a first-class 3-year full-time Diploma in the relevant engineering branch (Physics/Chemistry via a first-class B.Sc) plus 2 years of post-qualification experience; age 18–30. Technician 'B': Matric plus a 2-year ITI (NCVT)/NTC/NAC in the trade plus 2 years of experience; age 18–27.
    • Junior Personal Assistant: 12th pass plus shorthand at 80 words per minute; age up to 25. Officers: a degree, PG, MBA or CA/ICWA with 2–10 years of experience; age up to 35. All ages as on 30 November 2026.
    • Relaxation: SC/ST +5 years, OBC-NCL +3 years, PwBD +10/13/15 years.
    • Selection: a CBT (200 marks, 120 minutes, −0.25 per wrong answer) plus an interview for officers (60:40 CBT to interview) or a skill test for other posts (qualifying). JPA: a 100-mark Part A plus a stenography test. The exam date is to be announced; centres include Delhi, Chandigarh and Kolkata.
    • Apply online on scl.gov.in (Career page) from 1 November 2026 (11:00 AM) to 30 November 2026 (5:00 PM). No correction window is mentioned.
    • Application fee: ₹1,500 for officer posts, ₹1,300 for Technical Assistant, and ₹1,200 for Junior Personal Assistant and Technician-B — including a ₹600 non-refundable processing fee.
    • The application-fee part is refunded to women, SC/ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen candidates who appear in the CBT. Pay by 30 November 2026, 5:00 PM.
