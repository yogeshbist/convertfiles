    Eligibility & fees

    • Officer presently working on regular (on absorption) or contract basis in Railways, other Government departments, Central PSUs, Ports, JV companies under Government control, or private companies.
    • Finance: full-time Graduate plus CA/ICWAI, with the stated Finance & Accounts experience. Mechanical: full-time Degree in Mechanical Engineering, with the stated project experience.
    • Meeting the pay-scale criteria in Annexure-I (CDA PB-2 + GP 4200 / Level 6 for 2 years; or IDA Rs. 40,000–1,40,000 for 2 years; or IDA Rs. 30,000–1,20,000 for 2 years; or private sector — DM-equivalent and above, minimum gross Rs. 50,000/month, 04 years' executive-level experience, company turnover Rs. 75 Crores+).
    • Age up to 32 years as on 04.12.2026 (relaxation only for IPRCL employees on the rolls, to the extent of service in IPRCL).
    • Physically and medically fit (medical examination after selection).
    • No application fee is mentioned in the official notification (Vacancy Circular No. 12/2026, pages 1–5, and the application form, Annexure-II, pages 6–8).

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 2 posts — Deputy Manager (Finance)/E1, 01, Mumbai; Deputy Manager (Mechanical)/E1, 01, Paradip. Vacancy Circular No. 12/2026 dated 04.11.2026; closing date 04.12.2026.
    • Terms of appointment: ON ABSORPTION. Monthly remuneration: IDA Scale of Pay Rs. 40,000–1,40,000/- plus applicable allowances and PRP (for IDA as per policy). The officer can, however, be posted anywhere in India.
    • Age: up to 32 years as on 04.12.2026. Age relaxation is available only for employees presently working on the rolls of IPRCL, to the extent of their service period rendered in IPRCL. No SC/ST/OBC age relaxation is stated.
    • Essential Educational Qualification: for Deputy Manager (Finance)/E1 — full-time Graduate plus CA/ICWAI. For Deputy Manager (Mechanical)/E1 — full-time Degree in Mechanical Engineering.
    • Required Experience — Finance: experience in Finance & Accounts with IND-AS, Finalization of Accounts, GST, Income Tax, Internal & Statutory Audit, Banking and Treasury, Billing, Vetting of proposals, with knowledge of Tally, MS Office, ERP, Tenders & Contracts etc.
    • Required Experience — Mechanical: experience of all phases of project i.e. planning, procurement of trainsets/wagons/rolling stock, tender document preparation, bidding execution, commissioning, post commissioning, knowledge of testing, managing operations, maintenance of locomotives/wagons in Railway projects, Mechanical engineering related infrastructure works such as Metro / MRTS / Railways / Elevated Roads / Ropeways etc.
    • Eligibility criteria: the applicant must be an officer presently working on regular (on absorption) or contract basis in Railways, other Government departments, Central PSUs, Ports, JV companies under Government control / private companies, meeting the pay-scale criteria: CDA — PB-2 + GP 4200/- (pre-revised 6th CPC), revised to Pay Matrix Level 6 under 7th CPC, for 2 years; IDA — Rs. 16,400–40,500 (pre-revised 2nd PRC) revised to Rs. 40,000–1,40,000 under 3rd PRC for 2 years, OR Rs. 12,600–32,500 revised to Rs. 30,000–1,20,000 for 2 years; Private sector — officer at Deputy Manager equivalent and above drawing minimum gross Rs. 50,000 per month, with at least 04 years' overall Executive (Officer) level service experience, working in a company with sales turnover of at least Rs. 75 Crores in the preceding Financial Year. For Mechanical, IDA officers may alternatively have at least 04 years in consolidated remuneration (non-IDA) in a supervisory role as Project Engineer / Site Engineer / Jr. Site Engineer / Sr. Site Engineer etc. in the concerned discipline in a PSU / Govt. Company / J.V. Company under Govt. control. Higher grade pay granted under ACP/MACP/NF by the parent department is not counted.
    • Crucial date for determining eligibility with reference to age, essential educational qualification, length of experience etc. is 04.12.2026.
    • Selection: shortlisting based on eligibility/experience in the relevant field, followed by Personal Interview (at the Corporate Office, or through video-conference, or at any other location at IPRCL's discretion). If 05 or more eligible candidates apply for a vacancy, shortlisting is in the ratio of 1:5. Intimation for the interview is sent by e-mail and posted on the IPRCL website. Travelling expenses for the interview are paid as per Company Policy. Selected candidates must be physically and medically fit, with a medical examination after selection.
    • How to apply: NO online form. Duly filled application as per Annexure-II (for absorption) along with enclosures, superscribing 'Application for the post of ____' on the envelope, to be sent by post / courier or dropped personally to Chief General Manager (HR), Indian Port Rail & Ropeway Corporation Ltd (IPRCL), 4th Floor, Nirman Bhavan, Mumbai Port Trust Building, M.P. Road, Mazgaon (E), Mumbai-400010, by 04.12.2026. Officers serving in Railways / Govt. departments / Central PSUs / Ports / JV companies must apply through proper channel, submit copies of the last 05 years' APARs, and produce a No Objection Certificate at the interview.
    • All facts above are from Vacancy Circular No. 12/2026 dated 04.11.2026 (8 pages, iprcl.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Annexure-I of the circular carefully — the pay-scale criteria decide eligibility, not just the degree.
    • Use the Annexure-II form inside the same PDF (headed 'APPLICATION FORM (TO BE USED FOR APPLYING ON ABSORPTION ONLY)').
    • Superscribe 'Application for the post of ____' on the top of the envelope.
    • If you are in a Govt./PSU/Port/JV job, apply through proper channel and keep the last 5 years' APARs and NOC ready.

    Avoid this

    • Do not look for an online form — there is none; this recruitment is offline only.
    • Do not apply if you are not presently working as an officer in Railways / Govt. / PSU / Port / JV / private company — this is an absorption appointment, not a fresh direct recruitment.
    • Do not leave any column of the form blank — the circular says an application with a blank column may be rejected.
    • Do not send the application after 04.12.2026 — IPRCL will not be responsible for late submission for any reason.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Filled Annexure-II application form (from the notice PDF)
    • Matriculation certificate (for age proof)
    • Degree / CA / ICWAI certificates and mark sheets
    • Experience certificates clearly indicating length and field of experience
    • Appointment letter + valid ID card / current pay slip of the present organisation
    • Last 5 years' APARs/ACRs (not for private-company officers)
    • Latest updated detailed CV
    • Photo ID proof and address proof; NOC if working in Railways / Govt. / PSU / Port / JV
    • Copy of audited balance sheet for proof of sales turnover (private-company officers only)
