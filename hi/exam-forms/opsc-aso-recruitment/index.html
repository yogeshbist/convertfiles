    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Varg ke hisaab se pad: UR 144, SEBC 38, SC 81, ST 77 — jinme 114 pad mahilaon ke liye hain. Ye Home Department ke tahat Odisha Secretariat Service Cadre ke Group-B pad hain.
    • Shiksha: kisi bhi vishay me Bachelor's degree, computer applications ka paryapt jnana, aur nirdharit Odia bhasha yogyata — tumhe Odia padhna, likhna aur bolna aana chahiye.
    • Aayu 1 January 2026 ko 21–42 saal (janm 2 January 1984 se 1 January 2005 ke beech). Chhoot: SEBC/SC/ST/Mahila/ESM +5 saal, PwD +10 saal.
    • Chayan: 400 ankon ki likhit pariksha — Samanya Jagrukta, Reasoning & Mental Ability ke saath Mathematics, English, aur Odia — jisme −0.25 negative marking aur prati-paper qualifying ank hain (UR/SEBC 35%, SC/ST 30%, PwD 28%), phir 50 ankon ka Computer Skill Test (practical, 40% par qualifying). Antim rank sirf likhit ankon par banti hai. Likhit pariksha sambhavit roop se 17 January 2027 ko hai.
    • Vetan: ₹35,400 (ORSP Rules 2017 ke tahat Level-9, Cell-1) aur bhatte.
    • Neeche chhoot walon ke alawa sabhi ummeedwaron ke liye application fee ₹700.
    • Odisha ke SC aur ST ummeedwar, aur PwD ummeedwar, fee se mukt hain.
    • Fee sirf un ummeedwaron ko wapas milti hai jo asal me likhit pariksha me baithte hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • Vigyapan me photo aur signature ke liye koi pakki KB size nahi di gayi hai — portal ki upload screen par jo format aur size manga jaye, usi me upload karo.
    • Form shuru karne se pehle nayi colour photograph aur safed kagaz par kiya saaf signature scan ready rakho.
    • Sirf saaf, padhi ja sakne wali scans upload karo; dhundli photo ya signature par form reject ho sakta hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • 9 November 2026 ko window khulne ke baad hi apply karo, aur 30 November (shaam 5:00) se achhi pehle poora karo.
    • Odia bhasha ki shart dekho — nirdharit yogyata ke saath Odia padhna, likhna aur bolna anivarya hai.
    • Computer jnana ka certificate ready rakho; paryapt computer jnana patrata ka hissa hai.
    • Yaad rakho, har paper ke apne qualifying ank hain — zyada kul ank bhi cut-off se neeche wale paper ko nahi bachata.
    • Fee wapas chahiye to pariksha me baitho — refund sirf unhi ko milta hai jo asal me baithte hain.

    यह मत करो

    • Vigyapan me nirdharit Odia yogyata ke bina apply mat karo.
    • Computer Skill Test ko vaikalpik mat samjho — ye 40% par qualifying hai aur pass karna anivarya hai.
    • Aakhiri shaam ka intezaar mat karo; application 30 November ko shaam 5:00 baje band hoti hai.
    • Apne certificate se alag varg mat bharo — vargon ke andar 114 pad mahilaon ke liye nirdharit hain.
    • Ye sochkar koi paper mat chhodo ki kul ank sambhal lega; prati-paper qualifying ank lagu hain.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • Bachelor's degree certificate aur marksheets scan ki hui
    • HSC certificate (aayu aur Odia ka saboot) scan kiya hua
    • Computer jnana ka certificate scan kiya hua
    • Jaati praman patra (SEBC/SC/ST) scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho
    • PwBD / ESM praman patra scan kiya hua, yadi lagu ho
    • Photo aur signature scans ready

    दस्तावेज़ जो लगेंगे

    • Bachelor's degree certificate aur marksheets
    • HSC (10th) certificate — aayu aur Odia bhasha yogyata ke liye
    • Paryapt computer jnana ka certificate
    • Jaati praman patra (SEBC/SC/ST), yadi lagu ho
    • PwBD / Ex-Serviceman praman patra, yadi lagu ho
    • Photo ID (Aadhaar / Voter ID)

    फॉर्म भरते समय आम गलतियाँ

    • Ye samajhna ki fee gayi so gayi — ye wapas milti hai, lekin sirf un ummeedwaron ko jo asal me likhit pariksha me baithte hain.
    • Prati-paper qualifying ankon ko nazarandaaz karke sirf zyada kul ank ki taiyaari karna.
    • Nirdharit Odia yogyata ke bina apply karna.
    • Computer Skill Test ko aupcharikta samajhna — iske 40% qualifying ank par fail hone se ummeedwari khatam ho jati hai.

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. opsc.gov.in kholo aur Advt 15 of 2026-27 poora padho.
    2. Register karo aur 9 November 2026 se ASO application bharo.
    3. Photo, signature aur certificates upload karo.
    4. ₹700 fee online do (Odisha ke SC/ST aur PwD ummeedwaron ke liye chhoot hai).
    5. 30 November 2026, shaam 5:00 se pehle submit karo aur application copy sambhalo.
    6. Likhit pariksha (sambhavit 17 January 2027) aur uske baad ke Computer Skill Test ki taiyaari karo.

    आम सवाल

    OPSC Assistant Section Officer (ASO) के लिए आवेदन की आखिरी तारीख क्या है?
    Online फॉर्म 30 नवंबर 2026 को बंद होगा। शुल्क 30 नवंबर 2026 तक भरा जा सकता है।
    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर का size क्या होना चाहिए?
    Vigyapan me photo aur signature ke liye koi pakki KB size nahi di gayi hai — portal ki upload screen par jo format aur size manga jaye, usi me upload karo. Form shuru karne se pehle nayi colour photograph aur safed kagaz par kiya saaf signature scan ready rakho.
    Kya OPSC ASO ke liye Odia anivarya hai?
    Haan. Bachelor's degree aur computer jnana ke saath vigyapan me nirdharit Odia yogyata zaroori hai — ummeedwar ko Odia padhna, likhna aur bolna aana chahiye.
    Kya OPSC ASO ki fee wapas milti hai?
    ₹700 fee sirf un ummeedwaron ko wapas milti hai jo asal me likhit pariksha me baithte hain. Odisha ke SC/ST ummeedwaron aur PwD ummeedwaron ko fee deni hi nahi padti.
