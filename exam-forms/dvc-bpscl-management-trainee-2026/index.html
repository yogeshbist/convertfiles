    Eligibility & fees

    • Full-time B.E./B.Tech in the relevant discipline with minimum 65% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).
    • Valid GATE-2025 score in the matching paper: Mechanical (ME), Electrical (EE), Civil (CE) or Instrumentation (IN).
    • Age as on 6 December 2026: maximum 28 years (UR); OBC +3, SC/ST +5, PwBD +10–15, departmental 45 years.
    • ₹300 for General/OBC/EWS candidates — pay online by 6 December 2026.
    • No fee for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and departmental candidates.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 18 Management Trainee (Technical) posts in Bokaro Power Supply Co. (P) Ltd (BPSCL), a SAIL–DVC joint venture: Mechanical 10, Electrical 4, Civil 1, Instrumentation & Control (C&I) 3.
    • Apply online 6 November–6 December 2026 (till 23:59 hrs) at dvc.gov.in (Career → Recruitment Notices).
    • Selection is on GATE-2025 marks only, followed by document verification and medical examination — there is NO separate written test.
    • 1-year training with pay of ₹50,000–1,60,000, then absorption as Assistant Manager (E-1) at ₹60,000–1,80,000.
    • Age as on 6 December 2026: maximum 28 years (UR); OBC +3 years, SC/ST +5 years, PwBD +10–15 years, departmental candidates 45 years.
    • This is a different drive from the DVC Executive Trainee recruitment (Advt No. PLR/ET/04/2026/05, 108 posts) — do not confuse the two.
    • All facts above are taken from Advt No. PLR/BPSCL/05/2026/07, dated 6 November 2026.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Advt No. PLR/BPSCL/05/2026/07 fully on dvc.gov.in before applying.
    • Apply only on dvc.gov.in (Career → Recruitment Notices) by 6 December 2026 (11:59 PM).
    • Keep your GATE-2025 scorecard, B.E./B.Tech degree and category certificates ready.
    • Do not confuse this with the DVC Executive Trainee drive (Advt PLR/ET/04/2026/05) — the two are separate.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply without a valid GATE-2025 score in the matching paper — selection is on GATE-2025 marks only.
    • Do not apply with a GATE score from any other year — only GATE-2025 counts.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes on 6 December 2026 at 11:59 PM.
    • Do not expect a separate written test — there is none.
