    Eligibility & fees

    • 10th pass / ITI / 12th pass, as per the post and game.
    • Recognised sports achievements between 1 April 2024 and 9 December 2026 in Football, Kabaddi, Hockey, Tennis or Volleyball.
    • Age 18–25 years as on 1 January 2027 (born 02.01.2002–01.01.2009). No age relaxation is allowed.
    • ₹500 for General/OBC/EWS candidates — pay online by 9 December 2026.
    • ₹250 for SC/ST/Ex-Servicemen/PwBD/Women/Minorities/EBC candidates. The ₹250 concessional fee is refunded to candidates who appear in the trials (minus bank charges).

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 17 Group C posts for outstanding sportspersons against Sports Quota (Open Advertisement) 2026–27: Pay Level-1 (Grade Pay ₹1,800) 9 posts, Pay Level-2 (Grade Pay ₹1,900) 8 posts. Games: Football, Kabaddi, Hockey, Tennis, Volleyball.
    • Apply online 10 November 2026 (9 AM)–9 December 2026 (11:59 PM) at pb.icf.gov.in.
    • Age 18–25 years as on 1 January 2027 (born between 02.01.2002 and 01.01.2009, both dates inclusive). NO age relaxation is permissible for recruitment through open advertisement.
    • Only sports achievements between 1 April 2024 and 9 December 2026 will be considered.
    • Selection is by sports trials, sports achievement and education — there is NO written examination.
    • These are regular Group C posts in the Integral Coach Factory, Chennai (Ministry of Railways).
    • All facts above are taken from Employment Notice No. PB/RR/39/Sports-Open/01/2026-2027, dated 10 November 2026.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read Employment Notice No. PB/RR/39/Sports-Open/01/2026-2027 fully on pb.icf.gov.in before applying — check the game and position-wise posts.
    • Apply only online at pb.icf.gov.in between 10 November and 9 December 2026.
    • Keep your sports achievement certificates (1 April 2024–9 December 2026) and education certificates ready.
    • Keep the fee payment transaction ID (starting with 'DU') safe — it is needed later.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply with sports achievements outside 1 April 2024–9 December 2026 — only achievements in this window count.
    • Do not expect any age relaxation — none is permissible in this open advertisement.
    • Do not expect a written test — selection is by trials, sports achievement and education.
    • Do not wait for the last day — the portal closes on 9 December 2026 at 11:59 PM.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. pb.icf.gov.in par Employment Notice No. PB/RR/39/Sports-Open/01/2026-2027 padhen aur apna game/position chunen.
    2. ICF portal par registration karen aur form bharen.
    3. Sports achievement certificates aur education documents upload karen.
    4. Fee online bharen (₹500 Gen/OBC/EWS; ₹250 SC/ST/Ex-SM/PwBD/Women/Minorities/EBC) — 9 दिसंबर 2026 tak — aur 'DU' se shuru hone wala transaction ID save karen.
    5. Form submit karen aur confirmation save karen. Trials ki date ke liye ICF website dekhte rahen.
