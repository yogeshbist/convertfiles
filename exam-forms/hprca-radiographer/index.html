    Eligibility & fees

    • 43 vacancies — Health & Family Welfare 19 and Medical Education & Research 24. Post Code 26061.
    • Education: 10+2 Science plus a B.Sc. in Medical Radiology & Imaging, Radiography, Radiation Technology or Medical Imaging Technology (or equivalent), and registration with the HP Para Medical Council (now the State Allied Health Care Council, HP).
    • Matric and 10+2 must be from an institution in Himachal Pradesh, except for bonafide Himachalis.
    • Age: 18–45 years as on 1 January 2026. Relaxation up to 5 years for HP SC/ST/OBC/PwD candidates, HP government employees and HP ex-servicemen.
    • Selection: a CBT / written screening test (120 multiple-choice questions, 120 marks, 90 minutes), followed by document verification.
    • Apply online on hprca.hp.gov.in from 12 November 2026, 10:00 AM to 7 December 2026, 11:59 PM. A 4-day correction window opens 3 days after the closing date.
    • Application fee: ₹800 for all categories (₹100 examination fee + ₹700 processing fee), paid online only.
    • A 4-day correction window opens 3 days after the closing date (the notice does not print the exact calendar dates); the correction fee is ₹100.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: a latest passport-size photograph not older than 1 month. Signature: scanned signature. Both in JPG/JPEG/PNG format, maximum 1 MB each.
    • Complete your One Time Registration (OTR) on hprca.hp.gov.in before applying.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Complete your One Time Registration (OTR) on hprca.hp.gov.in first — the form needs it.
    • Check that your HP Para Medical Council registration is valid before applying.
    • Use a passport photo not older than 1 month.
    • Pay the ₹800 fee online — it is the same for every category.
    • Submit before 7 December 2026, 11:59 PM.

    Avoid this

    • Do not treat this as a regular pay-scale post — it is a Job Trainee engagement at a fixed ₹18,000 per month.
    • Do not apply without HP council registration — it is compulsory.
    • Do not use a photo older than 1 month.
    • Do not assume the correction window dates — the notice gives the rule (4 days, starting 3 days after closing), not the calendar dates.

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open hprca.hp.gov.in and complete your One Time Registration (OTR)
    2. Open Advertisement No. 15/2026 (Post Code 26061) and read it
    3. Fill the online form and upload the photo (max 1 MB) and signature (max 1 MB)
    4. Pay the ₹800 fee online
    5. Submit before 7 December 2026, 11:59 PM and save the acknowledgement
    6. Prepare for the CBT — 120 MCQs, 120 marks, 90 minutes
