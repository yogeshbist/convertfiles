    Eligibility & fees

    • 17 contractual High Performance Coach posts across SAI centres in India. The engagement is for 1 year, or till age 64, whichever is earlier (maximum tenure till 65).
    • Qualification (any one): a Diploma in coaching from SAI NS NIS Patiala or an equivalent institute; OR participation in the Olympics, Paralympics, Asian Games or World Championship with a certificate course in coaching; OR a Dronacharya Awardee.
    • AND: you must hold an analogous post, OR have 5 years of regular service in Pay Level-12 (₹78,800–2,09,200) under the Central or State Government or a UT. 5 years of coaching experience is desirable.
    • Age: maximum 64 years as on the opening date, 30 October 2026. This is effectively a post for retired or senior serving government coaches — not a freshers' exam.
    • Selection: shortlisting on relevant experience and job description, followed by an interview. There is no written exam.
    • Pay: Level-13, ₹1,23,100–2,15,900 (last pay drawn minus pension). Apply online on sportsauthorityofindia.nic.in/saijobs from 30 October to 1 December 2026.
    • No application fee is specified in the notice — applying is free.
