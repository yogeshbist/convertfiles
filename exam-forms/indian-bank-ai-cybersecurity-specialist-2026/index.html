    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • B.E./B.Tech in CSE/IT/ECE OR M.E./M.Tech in CSE/IT/ECE.
    • Vice President: age 35–48 years, mandatory CISSP/CISA/CISM certification, minimum 12 years' post-graduation experience in Information Security, of which minimum 5 years in the BFSI sector.
    • Assistant Vice President: age 27–40 years, mandatory CISSP/CISA/CISM, minimum 5 years' post-graduation experience in Information Security in the BFSI sector.
    • Associate Manager – Senior Officer: age 25–35 years, mandatory CISSP/CISA/CEH, minimum 3 years' post-graduation experience in Information Security in the BFSI sector.
    • Age as on 1 November 2026. Relaxations: SC/ST 5 years, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) 3 years, PwBD 10 years, Ex-Servicemen 5 years, 1984 riot-affected persons 5 years.
    • ₹1,000 for all candidates except SC/ST/PwBD (non-refundable, inclusive of GST) — payable by Internet Banking/NEFT/RTGS/UPI to 'Indian Bank Recruitment Fee Collection Account', Account No. 7422772966, Indian Bank, Royapettah, Current Account, IFSC IDIB000R021, by 1 December 2026.
    • ₹175 for SC/ST/PwBD candidates.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Apply OFFLINE in the prescribed format (Annexure-A in the PDF) with self-attested credentials.
    • Superscribe the closed envelope exactly: 'Application for the post of AI - Cybersecurity Specialist Officers for Information System Security Department (ISSD) on Contractual Basis - 2026'.
    • Send it to reach the Chief General Manager (CDO & CLO), Indian Bank, Corporate Office, HRM Department, Recruitment Section, 254-260, Avvai Shanmugam Salai, Royapettah, Chennai – 600 014 on or before 1 December 2026.

    • Pay the fee by Internet Banking/NEFT/RTGS/UPI to the Indian Bank Recruitment Fee Collection Account (A/c 7422772966, IFSC IDIB000R021).

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply online — there is no online form for this recruitment.
    • Do not send the application without the Annexure-A format and self-attested credentials.
    • Do not miss the 1 December 2026 receipt deadline — late applications are not accepted.
    • Do not treat this as a permanent post — it is Fixed Term Employment (3 years, extendable to 5).
