    Eligibility & fees

    • Indian citizen.
    • Lokrakshak cadre (Advt. /1): Higher Secondary School Certificate (Class 12/HSC) pass or equivalent recognised by the Government.
    • Head Constable Driver Mechanic (Grade-2) and Motor Transport Supervisor (Grade-2) (Advt. /2, /3): Class 12 pass plus a Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate with 1 year of ITI apprenticeship, or a 3-year National Apprenticeship Training Scheme certificate in Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade.
    • PSI cadre (Advt. /4): Bachelor's degree in any discipline.
    • Age as on 16 December 2026: Lokrakshak 18-33 years (born 16.12.1993–16.12.2008); HCDM maximum 33 years (born on/after 16.12.1993); MTS 18-36 years (born 16.12.1990–16.12.2008); PSI maximum 35 years (born on/after 16.12.1991). Relaxations: General women 5 years, reserved-category men 5 years, reserved-category women 10 years, PwBD 10-20 years, sportspersons 5 years, Ex-servicemen service period + 3 years; maximum age 45 years in most cases.
    • Rs.100 for General-category candidates, plus bank service charges, online only.
    • No fee for EWS, SC, ST, SEBC and Ex-servicemen (PwBD also exempt in Advt. /2 and /3).
    • Bank charges: RuPay/UPI nil; credit card 2% + tax; INB-SBI Rs.25, INB HDFC/ICICI/AXIS/KOTAK Rs.40, INB-other Rs.30, NEFT Rs.30 per transaction.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • 13,152 Class-3 posts, 4 advertisements (all dated 26 November 2026) — Lokrakshak cadre 12,752, Head Constable Driver Mechanic (Grade-2) 133, Motor Transport Supervisor (Grade-2) 26, PSI cadre 241. Regular (permanent) direct recruitment by the Gujarat Police Recruitment Board, Home Department.
    • Lokrakshak breakup (Advt. GPRB/202627/1): Unarmed Police Constable 8,233; Armed Police Constable 1,132; Armed Police Constable (SRPF) 3,107; Jail Sepoy (Male) 251; Jail Sepoy (Female/Matron) 29; PwBD total 525.
    • Head Constable Driver Mechanic (Grade-2) 133 (PwBD 5) under Advt. GPRB/202627/2; Motor Transport Supervisor (Grade-2) 26 (PwBD 1) under Advt. GPRB/202627/3; PSI cadre 241 under Advt. GPRB/202627/4 — Unarmed Police Sub Inspector 225, Jailer Group-2 (female) 16.
    • CORRECTION (27 November 2026): the earlier 3,000-post figure (Lokrakshak 2,841) came from the 19 November 2026 short notice. GPRB's own 24 November notice flagged the Lokrakshak vacancy change and the new Unarmed PSI + Jailer Group-2 (female) advertisement; the four detailed advertisements dated 26 November 2026 on OJAS total 13,152 posts.
    • Dates: online applications 26 November 2026, 2:00 PM to 16 December 2026, 11:59 PM, on ojas.gujarat.gov.in. Online fee payment also closes 16 December 2026, 11:59 PM. A separate application is needed for each cadre.
    • Fee: Rs.100 for General-category candidates plus bank service charges, online only (no post-office/bank challan). No fee for EWS, SC, ST, SEBC and Ex-servicemen (PwBD also exempt in Advt. /2 and /3). Bank charges: RuPay/UPI nil; credit card 2% + tax; INB-SBI Rs.25, INB HDFC/ICICI/AXIS/KOTAK Rs.40, INB-other Rs.30, NEFT Rs.30 per transaction.
    • Age as on 16 December 2026: Lokrakshak 18-33 years (born 16.12.1993–16.12.2008); HCDM maximum 33 years (born on/after 16.12.1993); MTS 18-36 years (born 16.12.1990–16.12.2008); PSI maximum 35 years (born on/after 16.12.1991). Relaxations: General women 5 years, reserved-category men 5 years, reserved-category women 10 years, PwBD 10-20 years, sportspersons 5 years, Ex-servicemen service period + 3 years; maximum age 45 years in most cases.
    • Qualification: Lokrakshak — HSC passed; HCDM and MTS — HSC + Motor Mechanic Vehicle Trade Certificate + 1-year ITI apprenticeship or 3-year National Apprenticeship Training Scheme certificate; PSI — Bachelor's degree in any discipline. All Class-3, Home Department; 5-year probation/contract in the technical cadres.
    • Selection: Physical Test first (qualifying; men 5,000 m in max 25 min, women 1,600 m in max 8 min 30 sec, Ex-servicemen 2,400 m in max 12 min 30 sec), then Objective MCQ Test 200 marks, 3 hours, negative marking 0.25 (Lokrakshak); PSI Main Exam 300 marks (Paper 1 General Studies MCQ 200 marks/3 h; Paper 2 Gujarati and English Language Skill, descriptive, 100 marks/3 h); HCDM single-stage MCQ (Part A 90 + Part B 120 = 210 marks).
    • Pay (fixed contract): SRPF Lokrakshak Rs.25,000/month; Head Constable Driver Mechanic Rs.25,000/month; PSI Rs.49,500/month.
    • Detailed advertisements (OJAS PDFs): GPRB/202627/1 (Lokrakshak, 17 pp), GPRB/202627/2 (HCDM, 14 pp), GPRB/202627/3 (MTS, 13 pp), GPRB/202627/4 (PSI, 18 pp). The OJAS Apply Now form was seen live on 27 November 2026.
    • All facts above are from the four detailed advertisements dated 26 November 2026 (ojas.gujarat.gov.in / gprb.gujarat.gov.in).

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Read the detailed advertisement for your cadre on OJAS (GPRB/202627/1 for Lokrakshak, /2 for HCDM, /3 for MTS, /4 for PSI) before applying.
    • Check the age limit for your cadre as on 16 December 2026 — Lokrakshak 18-33, HCDM maximum 33, MTS 18-36, PSI maximum 35 years.
    • Apply on the verified OJAS portal — the Apply Now form was seen live on 27 November 2026.
    • Prepare for the Physical Test first — it is qualifying (men 5,000 m in max 25 min, women 1,600 m in max 8 min 30 sec, Ex-servicemen 2,400 m in max 12 min 30 sec).
    • Save the registration number and fee receipt after submission.

    Avoid this

    • Do not use the old 3,000-post figure — the detailed advertisements dated 26 November 2026 total 13,152 posts; the earlier figure came from the short notice.
    • Do not file one application for all cadres — a separate application is needed for each cadre.
    • Do not pay the fee by post-office or bank challan — fee is online only.
    • Do not miss the window — applications close 16 December 2026 at 11:59 PM.

    Common mistakes that get forms rejected

    • Purane 3,000-post figure par bharosa karna (sahi kul 13,152 hai)
    • Ek hi application se saare cadres ke liye apply karna (har cadre ke liye alag application)
    • Fee challan se bharne ki koshish karna (sirf online)
    • Physical Test ki taiyaari na karna (ye qualifying hai)

    How to apply — step by step

    1. OJAS portal (ojas.gujarat.gov.in) par apne cadre ka detailed advertisement padhen — last date 16 December 2026 (11:59 PM).
    2. Apne cadre ki age limit (16.12.2026 ko) aur qualification check karen.
    3. Physical Test ki taiyaari karen — ye qualifying hai (men 5,000 m / women 1,600 m).
    4. Verified OJAS form par register karen aur apne cadre ke liye alag application bharen.
    5. Fee ₹100 online bhugtan karen (agar lagu ho) aur registration number sambhal kar rakhen.
