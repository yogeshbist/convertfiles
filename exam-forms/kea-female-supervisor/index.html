    Eligibility & fees

    • 171 Female Supervisor (Mahila Melvicharakiyaru) Group-C posts — Residual Parent Cadre 141 (direct 134 + in-service 7) and Kalyana Karnataka 30 (direct 28 + in-service 2). In-service posts are for women employees of the WCD Department; if not filled, they go to general candidates. Women candidates only.
    • Education: Bachelor's degree in Home Science, Social Work or Sociology, or B.Sc in Home Science/Community Science. The degree marks card or certificate must be in hand by the last date — result-awaited candidates are not eligible, and no other qualification is accepted.
    • Age: 18–40 years as on the last date of application (General Merit); 43 years for Category 2A/2B/3A/3B; 45 years for SC/ST/Category-1. The limits already include a one-time 5-year relaxation under the Government Order of 29 January 2026. Further relaxation: PwD, widows and freed bonded labourers 10 years; Karnataka government employees service years or 10 years, whichever is less; ex-servicemen service + 3 years.
    • Selection: written competitive exam — Paper-1 General Knowledge (100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours) and Paper-2 Specific Paper (100 questions, 100 marks, 2 hours), −0.25 per wrong answer, 35% minimum to qualify — followed by a compulsory Kannada Language Test on 8 January 2027 (3–5 PM, 150 marks, 50 to qualify; marks not counted in merit). Exempt if Kannada was a first/second language in SSLC or above, or the candidate studied in Kannada medium.
    • Pay: ₹44,425–83,700, probation 2 years, National Pension Scheme (NPS) applies.
    • Application fee: ₹750 for General Merit and Category 2A/2B/3A/3B; ₹500 for SC/ST/Category-1/ex-servicemen/transgender; ₹250 for Persons with Disabilities. Pay online by 29 November 2026 — the fee is non-refundable.

    Photo & signature — what the form asks

    • Photo: recent passport-size photograph, JPEG, white background, 50–200 KB.
    • Signature: black or blue ink on white paper, JPEG, 50–70 KB.

    Photo & signature — do’s and don’ts

    Do this

    • Check that your degree is exactly in Home Science, Social Work or Sociology (or B.Sc Home Science/Community Science) — no other degree qualifies.
    • Fill the form carefully — no changes are allowed after submission.
    • Pay the fee online by 29 November 2026 (one day after the form closes).
    • Prepare for the compulsory Kannada Language Test on 8 January 2027 unless you are exempt.
    • Keep your degree marks cards of all semesters ready.

    Avoid this

    • Do not apply if you are a male candidate — these posts are reserved for women only.
    • Do not apply while awaiting your final degree result — result-awaited candidates are not eligible.
    • Do not submit in a hurry — no correction is possible after submission.
    • Do not miss the fee deadline (29 November 2026) — the form without fee is incomplete.

    Taiyaari checklist — keep these ready

    • Degree marks cards of all semesters + provisional/degree certificate

    • Photo JPEG 50–200 KB, signature JPEG 50–70 KB
    • Online fee payment (₹750 / ₹500 / ₹250) by 29 November 2026

    How to apply — step by step

    1. Open cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea and open the Female Supervisor notification (RPC or Kalyana Karnataka)
    2. Register with your mobile number and email ID
    3. Fill the form carefully — no changes are allowed after submission
    4. Upload the photo (JPEG, 50–200 KB), signature (JPEG, 50–70 KB) and certificate details
    5. Pay the fee online by 29 November 2026
    6. Submit before 28 November 2026 and download a copy of the application and fee challan
