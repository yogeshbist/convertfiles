    पात्रता और शुल्क

    • Bharatiya nagrik hon.
    • Bachelor's Degree (post-wise qualification notification table ke hisaab se); educational qualification ki crucial date 10 November 2026 hai.
    • Age post ke hisaab se minimum 18/20/21 saal aur maximum 30/44 saal (1 July 2026 ko).
    • Relaxations: SC/ST/BC/EWS 5 saal; SC/ST carried-forward vacancies 10 saal; PwBD 10 saal; Ex-Servicemen (service + 3 saal); NCC; regular AP Government employees 5 saal tak.
    • ₹250 application processing fee + ₹80 examination fee = kul ₹330 — 11 December 2026 tak application ke saath online pay karen.
    • ₹80 examination fee SC, ST, BC, PwBDs aur Ex-Servicemen/Personnel, Household Supply White Card wale families aur berozgar yuvaon ko nahi deni (G.O. Ms. No. 439). ₹250 processing fee sabko deni hai.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या चाहिए

    • 720 posts — Executive 471 aur Non-Executive 249. Executive: Municipal Commissioner Gr. III 6, Deputy Mandal Parishad Development Officer 176, Assistant Registrar of Co-operative Societies 10, Deputy Tahsildar 203 (01 carried forward), Sub-Registrar Gr. II 7, GST Officer 40, Prohibition & Excise Sub Inspector 29 (02 carried forward).
    • Non-Executive 249, jinme Assistant Section Officer (GAD) 71, ASO (Finance) 5, ASO (Law) 4, Senior Accountant (HOD's) 90, Senior Accountant (Districts) 1, Junior Accountant (HOD's) 47 aur 249 total tak ke aage ke posts shamil hain.
    • Notification No. 37/2026 — detailed notification 10 November 2026 ka hai (initial notification 13 October 2026).
    • Dates: online applications 10 November 2026 se 11 December 2026, raat 11:59 tak. Screening Test 21 February 2027; Main exam ki date baad me announce hogi.
    • Fee: ₹250 application processing fee + ₹80 examination fee = ₹330. ₹80 examination fee SC, ST, BC, PwBDs aur Ex-Servicemen/Personnel, Household Supply White Card wale families aur berozgar yuvaon ko nahi deni (G.O. Ms. No. 439); ₹250 processing fee sabko deni hai.
    • Eligibility: aam taur par Bachelor's Degree, post-wise notification table me. Educational qualification ki crucial date = detailed notification ki date (10 November 2026).
    • Age: 1 July 2026 ko minimum 18/20/21 saal aur maximum 30/44 saal, post ke hisaab se. Relaxations: SC/ST/BC/EWS 5 saal; SC/ST carried-forward vacancies 10 saal; PwBD 10 saal; Ex-Servicemen (service + 3 saal); NCC; regular AP Government employees 5 saal tak.
    • APPSC portal par apply karen — APPSC landing page par 'Group-II Application Submission' tile se GROUPII_2026 login khulta hai (OTPR ID / Password / Captcha).
    • Upar ke saare facts Notification No. 37/2026 (psc.ap.gov.in) se liye gaye hain.

    फोटो और हस्ताक्षर — क्या करें, क्या न करें

    यह करो

    • Apply sirf official portal https://portal-psc.ap.gov.in/GROUPII_2026/ par karen (psc.ap.gov.in par 'Group-II Application Submission' tile se).
    • Post chunne se pehle Notification No. 37/2026 ki post-wise qualification aur age table check karen.
    • Apni OTPR ID aur password ready rakhen — form me chahiye.
    • Final printout aur fee receipt safe rakhen.

    यह मत करो

    • Us post ko mat chunen jiski qualification ya age aap poori na karte hon — crucial date 10 November 2026 hai.
    • Agar ₹80 exam fee exempt hai to use pay mat karen — lekin ₹250 processing fee sabko deni hai.
    • Last date ka wait mat karen — portal 11 December 2026, raat 11:59 ko band hota hai.
    • Kisi aur ki OTPR ID use mat karen.

    तैयारी Checklist — ये सब तैयार रखो

    • Bachelor's Degree certificate aur mark sheets
    • OTPR registration (APPSC One Time Profile Registration)
    • Date of birth proof
    • Category/reservation certificate, agar applicable ho
    • Recent passport-size photo aur signature scans
    • Fee payment proof (₹330, agar ₹80 exam fee exempt hai to ₹250)

    • Post-wise qualification dekhe bina post chunna

    • OTPR ID ke bina form bharne ki koshish karna
    • Last date ka wait karna

    फॉर्म कैसे भरें — step by step

    1. psc.ap.gov.in par Notification No. 37/2026 (detailed notification, 10 November 2026) padhen — last date 11 December 2026.
    2. OTPR ID se https://portal-psc.ap.gov.in/GROUPII_2026/ par login karen.
    3. Post chunen, form bharen, documents upload karen aur ₹330 fee online pay karen (₹80 exam fee exempt ho to ₹250).
    4. Submit karke final printout nikaal kar rakhen.
    5. Screening Test 21 February 2027 ki taiyari karen; Main exam ki date baad me aayegi.
